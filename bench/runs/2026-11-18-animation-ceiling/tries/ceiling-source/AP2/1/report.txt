I wrote `src/pulse.ts` as a single custom animation task per button instead of a property-animation group. The task advances by the manager's delta, moves `scale` linearly 1 → 1.5 → 1 over 0.4 s, then sets it to 1 and detaches itself, so finished pulses leave nothing behind on the shared manager. A press resets the timer and scale to 1 and re-attaches the task if needed.

I couldn't check the package itself, so I'm assuming `@babylonjs/lite` exports `createAnimationTask`, `addAnimationTask`, `removeAnimationTask` and the `AnimationManager` type from its root, as the declarations suggest. If a call to `clearAnimationManager` removes a task mid-pulse, the button snaps back to scale 1.
