I wrote `src/hover.ts` as a plain animation task on the manager. It keeps its own phase from the manager's step, so a strength change takes effect on the next update without restarting the bob. Each pickup gets its own task, so many can share one manager.

I avoided property-animation groups because their weight blending on a single group doesn't blend toward rest. I wasn't sure that `createAnimationTask` and `addAnimationTask` are exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` root, since I only saw the per-file declarations. If they aren't, the import path needs adjusting.
