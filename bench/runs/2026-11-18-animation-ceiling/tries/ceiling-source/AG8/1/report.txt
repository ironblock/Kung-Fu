I read `runFrameInterpolation` in `animation-source.ts`. Its driver replaces any non-positive delta (0 ms or negative) with a nominal 1000/60 ms before dividing by 1000. So paused frames and backward clock steps both hand the step function about 0.016667 s, which is 16.67 after ×1000 and rounding. Positive deltas pass through unchanged: 1 ms gives 1 and 40 ms gives 40.

A paused game therefore does not freeze these camera moves: they keep advancing at 60 FPS pace. If you want pausing to hold them, you would have to cancel them or change that fallback.
