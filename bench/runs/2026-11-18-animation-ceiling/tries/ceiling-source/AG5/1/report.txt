`restoreMask` builds a fresh mask with `createAnimationGroupMask`, which copies `names` and installs the controller's mask resolver. A plain JSON object assigned to `group.mask` would be ignored if no mask had ever been created. It then resets the nodes the new mask blocks to their rest TRS, because masked targets are otherwise left frozen at their last animated pose after a mask change.

I could not see the controller source (`skeleton-updater`), so I'm not certain it re-resolves on a new mask object as the docs say. I'm also unsure the rest-pose reset is safe for nodes excluded from TRS writeback (skin joints and skinned-mesh ancestors); I assumed writing their unchanged rest values is harmless. I assumed `createAnimationGroupMask` and `animationGroupMaskRetainsTarget` are exported from `@babylonjs/lite`, and the reset reads the internal `_gltfMixer` field.
