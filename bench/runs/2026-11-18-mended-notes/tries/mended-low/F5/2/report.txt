I read the type declarations and the notes, then wrote the four answers to `notes/pointer-lock.json`. The key findings: `attachFreeControl` only turns the camera during an active drag (started by a `pointerdown` with button 0, 1, or 2), and it reads `clientX`/`clientY` deltas—not `movementX`/`movementY`—so pointer lock defeats it in both the no-button and button-held cases, while a plain right-drag and a one-finger touch drag both work normally. I was confident on all four; the one subtle point is that under pointer lock the `clientX`/`clientY` are frozen at the lock-time position, so even an active drag sees zero delta.
