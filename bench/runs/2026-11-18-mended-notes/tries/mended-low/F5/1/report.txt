I read the type declarations and the behavioural notes, then reasoned through each scenario against how `attachFreeControl` handles pointer events. The key facts are: (a) a drag session only begins on `pointerdown` with button 0, 1, or 2, and (b) once active, each `pointermove` rotates by the change in `clientX`/`clientY` divided by `angularSensitivity` — `movementX`/`movementY` are never read. Under pointer lock, `clientX`/`clientY` stay fixed, so even a held-button drag produces zero delta; with no button held there is no drag session at all. Right-drag (button 2) and one-finger touch (button 0, any pointerType) both start a drag and, without pointer lock, `clientX`/`clientY` change normally, so the camera turns. I was not unsure of anything — the notes are explicit on both the button requirements and the coordinate source.
