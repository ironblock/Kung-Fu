I wrote `src/turntable.ts` implementing `spinAround` as a `requestAnimationFrame`-driven loop that advances `camera.alpha` each frame using the same exponential easing the library's `interpolateArcRotateCamera` uses (note 34: cover `1 − 2^(−dt/0.1)` of the remaining distance), guaranteeing a continuous glide in the direction α grows with no velocity discontinuity, then snaps exactly to `startAlpha + 2π·turns` and resolves. I did not use `interpolateArcRotateCamera` directly because its "shortest arc mod 2π" rule (note 60) would send the camera the wrong way for any turn count whose angular span exceeds π. The one thing I was not sure of is whether the scene's internal frame clock could differ from `requestAnimationFrame` timing in a way that matters here; since I write `alpha` directly and the scene renders whatever value is present, the visual result should be identical regardless.
