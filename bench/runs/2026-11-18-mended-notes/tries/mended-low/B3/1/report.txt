I wrote `src/fly-controls.ts` using the library's `attachConfigurableFreeControl`, overriding the up/down key lists so that Space is the only rise key and PageDown is the only sink key (removing Shift from the down set), and registering ShiftLeft/ShiftRight as fast keys with a multiplier of 3. I was not sure whether `fastKeys` also accelerates the vertical (up/down) motion or only the horizontal WASD/arrow motion — the notes describe the default key sets but don't state the scope of the fast multiplier — though given that the API calls them "fast" keys generically and the multiplier is a single scalar, it almost certainly applies to all movement channels including vertical.
