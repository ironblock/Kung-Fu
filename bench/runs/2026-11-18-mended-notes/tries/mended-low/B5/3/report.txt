I wrote `src/label-position.ts` which projects the world point through the camera's view-projection matrix using Babylon Lite's `projectWorldToScreen`, then reads back `cssX`/`cssY` (already in CSS pixels from the canvas top-left) and marks the point visible when it is neither behind the camera nor offscreen — so points beyond the far plane still count as visible, while points outside the frustum's side/top/bottom edges do not. The one thing I'm not fully certain of is whether `projectWorldToScreen`'s `cssX`/`cssY` are measured from the canvas top-left or from the viewport top-left; the notes and the options shape (which include both the full-canvas `backingWidth`/`backingHeight` and the sub-rectangle `viewport`) strongly suggest canvas-relative, but the library isn't here to confirm.
