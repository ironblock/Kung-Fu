I wrote `src/label-position.ts` which projects the world point through the camera's view-projection matrix via `projectWorldToScreen`, returns the unclamped CSS-pixel coordinates (`cssX`/`cssY`), and marks the point visible when it is neither behind the camera nor outside the frame (so points beyond the far plane but within the frame are still visible). The one thing I'm not fully sure of is whether `canvas.clientWidth`/`clientHeight` is the right CSS-size source versus `getBoundingClientRect()` — they differ only if a CSS transform is scaling the canvas, which is unusual for a full-viewport 3D view.
