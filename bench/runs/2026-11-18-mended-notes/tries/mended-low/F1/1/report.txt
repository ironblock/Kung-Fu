I used the library's `attachConfigurableFreeControl` with `fastKeys` listing every movement key (WASD, arrows, Space/PageUp for rise, Shift/PageDown for sink) and `fastMultiplier: 20`, which makes each of those keys move the camera 20× as far per frame while leaving the mouse-drag look untouched. The returned dispose function from `attachConfigurableFreeControl` is passed through directly so it removes all canvas listeners and frame hooks.

The one thing I'm not fully certain of is whether `fastMultiplier` multiplies the per-frame step (making the key move 20× farther per frame) versus some other interpretation (e.g. temporarily scaling `camera.speed`); the API name and note 18 strongly suggest it scales the per-frame step, which is what "20 times as far while the key is held" requires, but I couldn't verify against the implementation.
