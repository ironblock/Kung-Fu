I wrote `src/focus.ts` which sets the camera's target in place (per the notes, the only way the camera will read it) and then nudges `alpha` by a sub-nanoradian amount and back, which forces the view and world matrices to rebuild immediately so the label code sees the new orbit centre in the same frame. The only thing I'm not fully sure of is whether this tiny alpha perturbation could reject a concurrently running `interpolateArcRotateCamera` promise (note 7 says any external alpha change rejects it); in practice a double-click to focus is unlikely to land mid-interpolation, but if your app does animate and focus simultaneously you'd want to guard against that.
