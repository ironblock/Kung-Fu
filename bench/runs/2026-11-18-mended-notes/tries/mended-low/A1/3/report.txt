I wrote `src/focus.ts`: it sets `camera.target` in place to the new point, then nudges `alpha` by 1e-7 and restores it to force the view-matrix rebuild in the same frame (since note 3 says a target write alone is invisible until the next alpha/beta/radius write that changes the value). The camera's angles and radius are untouched, so its position shifts by exactly the old-centre-to-point offset and it looks straight at the new point; subsequent angle or distance changes orbit the new target. I'm not aware of anything I'm unsure of, though the 1e-7 nudge is a practical epsilon—if the camera ever sat exactly at an alpha limit boundary the intermediate write could be clamped, but restoring `a` (which was already valid) corrects it.
