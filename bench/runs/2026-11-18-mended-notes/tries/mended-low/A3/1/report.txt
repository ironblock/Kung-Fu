I wrote `src/screen-shift.ts` which snapshots the camera's view and view-projection matrices (copied, per note 37) at mark time, then on `shift` re-reads the current matrices and projects the point with both sets through `projectWorldToScreen`, returning the difference in `cssX`/`cssY`. The one thing I'm not sure of is whether `Mat4` in Babylon Lite is a plain `Float32Array` or a class wrapping one — I assumed it's array-like so that `new Float32Array(mat)` produces a copy; if it's a class with an internal `.m` buffer, the copy line would need to be `new Float32Array(mat.m)` instead.
