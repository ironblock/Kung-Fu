I wrote `src/fly-to-place.ts` which delegates to the library's `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` and wraps the camera's `_cancelFly` hook to detect whether the flight was interrupted (another fly-to or user input) versus completing naturally, resolving `true` only on arrival. I'm not fully certain that `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` sets `_cancelFly` synchronously before returning its promise (rather than on the first frame), but given that the field exists on the camera interface and the cancel mechanism must be in place before any frame runs for a subsequent call to be able to cancel the in-flight animation, I believe the synchronous set is guaranteed.
