I wrote `src/fly-to-place.ts` which delegates the flight to the library's `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` (which is render-loop driven and never rejects), then on resolution compares the camera's final pose to the target place — using a shortest-angle difference for yaw to handle the wrap — and resolves `true` only if all channels are within 0.001 of the target. The one thing I was slightly unsure of is whether the library's flight snaps *exactly* to the goal on completion (the notes confirm this for `interpolateArcRotateCamera` but are less explicit for the geospatial fly), so I used a small epsilon rather than strict equality; given `GEO_EPSILON` is 0.001 in the library, this should be safe.
