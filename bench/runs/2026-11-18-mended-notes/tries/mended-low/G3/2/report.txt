I wrote `src/fly-to-place.ts` which delegates the animation to the library's `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` (which never rejects and resolves whether the flight completes or is cut short), then on resolution compares the camera's final yaw, pitch, radius, and center against the target place to decide whether we actually arrived. I'm not fully certain that `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` is re-exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` package root (as opposed to a subpath like `@babylonjs/lite/camera`), since I only have the type declarations and not the package's `exports` map — if it isn't at the root, the import line would need to be adjusted.
