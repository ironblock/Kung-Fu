I wrote `src/center-on.ts` which converts latitude/longitude (degrees) to a point on the planet sphere using the geospatial camera's coordinate convention (north = +Z, east = −Y at the +X centre, per note 58), then assigns a fresh object to `camera.center` so the re-orbit happens immediately (per note 13) while leaving yaw, pitch, and radius untouched. I'm confident in the mapping: lat 0 / lon 0 gives (R, 0, 0) matching the default centre, and yaw π/2 from there looks toward −Y which is the direction of increasing longitude.
