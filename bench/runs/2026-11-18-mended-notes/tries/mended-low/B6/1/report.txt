I created `src/chase-camera.ts` which uses `createBankedFreeCamera` (the only camera in the API that exposes a writable `upVector`) initialized upright, and on each `bank` call writes the car's up direction into `camera.upVector` so the camera's in-picture up aligns with the component of that vector perpendicular to the line of sight; when the perpendicular component vanishes (up parallel/anti-parallel to the gaze) it leaves the previous roll untouched. The one thing I'm not fully certain of is the exact internal cross-product ordering in Babylon Lite's basis construction (right- vs left-handed), but the high-level contract—setting `upVector` to a world direction makes the picture-up match its perpendicular projection—is the standard lookAt convention and is what the notes confirm.
