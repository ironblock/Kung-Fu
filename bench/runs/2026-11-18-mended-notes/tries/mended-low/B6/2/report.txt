I created `src/chase-camera.ts` which builds a `BankedFreeCamera` at the given eye/target with an initial world-up of (0,1,0), then on each `bank` call projects the car's up onto the plane perpendicular to the fixed view direction and writes the normalized result into the camera's `upVector`; when the projection is (nearly) zero—i.e. the car's up is aligned with the line of sight—it leaves the previous roll untouched.

One thing I was not sure of: the exact epsilon threshold for "parallel." I used 1e-6, which is generous enough to catch the exactly-aligned case the spec describes while not triggering on any realistic near-parallel situation; if your track geometry produces up vectors that are very close to (but not exactly) parallel to the line of sight and you'd rather those still bank, you could lower it.
