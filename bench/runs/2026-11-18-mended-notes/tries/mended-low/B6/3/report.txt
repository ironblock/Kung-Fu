I created `src/chase-camera.ts` which uses `createBankedFreeCamera` (the only camera type with a writable `upVector` per note 30) initialized upright at the given eye/target, and whose `bank` method writes the car's up direction into `upVector` — the camera's lookAt construction then projects it perpendicular to the line of sight, giving the closest achievable roll. When the up vector is (anti-)parallel to the look direction, the write is skipped so the previous roll is preserved. I'm slightly unsure whether the `1e-6` threshold is the right tolerance for "exactly along" in practice; if your game's loop geometry produces near-parallel (but not exactly parallel) ups that look bad, you may want to tune it.
