I created `src/walkthrough.ts` which builds a `FreeCamera` at the given eye/target, assigns it to `scene.camera`, attaches the standard free controls, and provides `jumpTo` that repositions the camera and synchronises the internal `_yaw`/`_pitch` so the next key-press or drag continues from the new orientation (per notes 6 and 56), and `dispose` that calls the detach function returned by `attachFreeControl` to remove all canvas listeners and the per-frame hook.

One thing I was not fully sure of: the exact sign convention for `_pitch` (whether positive means looking up or down). I derived it from the geometry — `atan2(dy, horizontalMagnitude)` gives positive pitch when the target is above the eye — which is consistent with note 43's clamp range of `±(PI/2 − 0.01)` and note 59's "Space moves straight up world Y" implying positive pitch is upward. If the library uses the opposite sign, the pitch line would need a negation.
