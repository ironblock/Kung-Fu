I computed `resolveCameraViewport` by converting the normalized viewport `{x:0.8, y:0.1, width:0.3, height:0.3}` to pixel coordinates on the 1000×500 canvas (remembering `y` is from the bottom), then clamping the right edge that overflows past 1000, giving `{x:800, y:300, width:200, height:150}`; and `getEffectiveAspectRatio` is simply `(1000/500) × (0.3/0.3) = 2.00` per note 38. I was not unsure of anything—the notes were explicit on both the clamping behaviour and the aspect formula.
