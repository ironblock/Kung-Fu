I halved `camera.angularSensibility` before calling the library's `attachControl`, which makes each drag move the alpha/beta inertial offsets twice as far (per note 74: `offset -= dx / angularSensibility`), and the glide inherits the doubled offset naturally; wheel zoom and panning are unaffected because they use `wheelPrecision` and `panningSensibility` respectively. The returned function restores the original sensitivity and calls the library's own dispose. I'm not 100% certain that `attachControl` reads `angularSensibility` at event time rather than capturing it at attach time, but note 74's phrasing and the general Babylon.js pattern strongly suggest it reads the live property each frame/event.
