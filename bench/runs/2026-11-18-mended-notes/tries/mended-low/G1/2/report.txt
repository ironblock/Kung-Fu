I copied each field from the mode's `GeospatialLimits` into the camera's existing `limits` object in place (because note 39 says a newly assigned object is never used by the camera's clamps), then called `setGeospatialOrientation` with an empty orientation so the camera immediately re-clamps its current pose against the new bounds. I'm confident in this approach; the only thing I'd flag is that if the app ever also writes `pitch` after switching modes, the pitch clamp uses `getEffectivePitchMax` which depends on the current radius, so the pitch bound can differ from the raw `pitchMax` at large radii—but that's inherent to the library's design, not something `useModeLimits` can override.
