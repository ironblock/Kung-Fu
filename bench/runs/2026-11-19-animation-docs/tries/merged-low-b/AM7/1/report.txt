I wrote the four values based on how `enableAnimationBlending` performs a weighted average of bone translations across groups, and how masks restrict which bones each group animates. I was not fully certain whether Babylon Lite's skeletal blending divides by the total weight (weighted average) or sums without dividing (like property blending does per note 12), but the standard Babylon.js approach for bone-matrix blending is a weighted average, and the rest position of 4 being exactly the midpoint of 2 and 6 strongly confirms that interpretation.
