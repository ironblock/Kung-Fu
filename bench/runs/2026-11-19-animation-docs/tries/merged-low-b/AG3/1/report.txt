I created `src/jump-to-frame.ts` with a two-step implementation: `goToFrame` seeks the group to the given frame (converting to seconds via the group's own `frameRate`) and pauses it, then `playAnimation` resumes it from that point at its existing `speedRatio` and `loopAnimation` settings. I'm not sure of anything — the notes confirm `goToFrame` handles both glTF and property groups, preserves loop/non-loop behavior, and that `playAnimation` is the correct way to resume after a seek.
