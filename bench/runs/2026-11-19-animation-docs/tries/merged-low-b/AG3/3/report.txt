I created `src/jump-to-frame.ts` which calls `goToFrame` to seek the group to the given frame (the library converts frames to seconds using the group's `frameRate`, wrapping for looping groups and clamping for non-looping ones), then calls `playAnimation` to resume playback from that point—preserving the group's speed, loop setting, and whether it plays once or loops. I'm not aware of anything I'm unsure of; the notes (especially 6, 38, 59, and 39) confirm this is the intended seek-and-resume pattern for both glTF and property clips.
