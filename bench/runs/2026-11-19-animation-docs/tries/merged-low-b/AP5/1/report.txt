I created `src/beacon.ts` which registers a custom animation task on the manager that tracks elapsed time, computes the flash envelope (linear rise over 0.1 s, linear fall over 0.1 s, flat zero for the remainder of the period), writes the scaled intensity to the lamp each update, and returns a setter that clamps the strength to [0, 1] so it takes effect at the next update without disturbing the rhythm. I was not sure whether "more than 0" in the spec meant I should reject 0 or simply that the caller's contract is to pass a positive value; I chose to clamp to 0 as a floor for robustness.
