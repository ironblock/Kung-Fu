I implemented `playAgain` by calling `stopAnimation` (which resets `currentTime` to the clip's start and marks the group stopped) followed by `playAnimation` (which clears the stop flag and resumes playback), which is the sequence the notes confirm replays a finished one-shot from its first frame while preserving `speedRatio`. I'm confident in this for both property and glTF groups; the only thing I wasn't independently verifying is the internal `_stopped` flag mechanics, but the notes' explicit statement that "stopAnimation then playAnimation replays from the start" covers both kinds.
