I wrote `src/play-again.ts` which calls `stopAnimation` (resets `currentTime` to the group's start and clears any internal stop flag) followed by `playAnimation` (clears the stop and begins playing). This works for both property clips (which have `isPlaying` false at their end) and glTF clips (which keep `isPlaying` true at their end per note 27), and preserves the group's `speedRatio`. I'm not sure of anything — the notes make the required sequence unambiguous.
