I wrote `notes/am6-weights.json` with the computed values. The skeletal blending normalizes by accumulated weight (a weighted average), so a lone clip at any non-zero weight still produces its full pose — you cannot fade to rest with a single clip; you need an idle clip at the rest pose to cross-fade into. I was slightly uncertain whether note 35's "does not write node targets" also suppresses the skeletal bone-matrix path, but the architecture (separate `SkeletonBinding.boneMatrices` vs. `AnimatedNodeTarget`) and the problem's framing both indicate the skeletal path is independent.
