I created `src/fade-in.ts` which builds a two-key linear property-animation clip on the `alpha` path (0 → 1 over `durationMs`/1000 seconds) and hands it to `createPropertyAnimationGroup` with `loop: false`, so the group starts playing immediately on the manager, writes a steady ramp, and stops at 1 with `isPlaying` false once `toTime` is reached. I was not sure whether `durationMs` could ever be 0 (which would make `toTime === fromTime` and throw per note 14), but I assumed the caller guarantees a positive duration.
