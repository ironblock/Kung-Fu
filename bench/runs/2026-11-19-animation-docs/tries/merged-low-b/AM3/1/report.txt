I created `src/skip-ahead.ts` which replays the manager's per-update sequence—pre-update hook, category handler (which drives weight fades), then each active task's `_update`—but feeds it `ms` directly, bypassing the `fixedDeltaMs` override that `updateAnimationManager` would impose. I was not sure whether the library's `updateAnimationManager` performs any additional internal bookkeeping beyond what the notes describe (e.g. snapshotting the task list before iteration), but the public surface and notes give no indication of any such step, so I replicated the documented order.
