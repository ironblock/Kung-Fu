I created `src/pose-mode.ts` with `freezeForPosing`, which detaches the group from its owning `AnimationManager` (so it stops writing node targets on every update — critical for glTF groups, which keep writing even while paused per note 27), pauses it, and captures `currentTime` and `speedRatio`. The returned `resume` re-attaches the group to the same manager, restores those values, and calls `playAnimation` so the clip continues from the frozen moment at its original speed.

The one thing I was not sure of: if the group is driven by the scene via an asset container and has no `_animationManager` (note 28), there is no manager to detach from, and a paused glTF group would still overwrite the nodes each frame. In that case the freeze would not fully hold for glTF clips; the function relies on the group being owned by an `AnimationManager`, which is the pattern your notes describe for driving clips from the render loop.
