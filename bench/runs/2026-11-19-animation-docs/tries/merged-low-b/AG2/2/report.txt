I created `src/pose-mode.ts` which freezes a playing `AnimationGroup` by removing it from its owning `AnimationManager` (so the per-frame tick no longer writes to the nodes) and, on resume, restores `currentTime`, re-adds the group to the manager, and calls `playAnimation` to clear any stop flag. I was not sure whether the group could alternatively be driven directly by the scene via `addToScene` (in which case it would have no `_animationManager` and the freeze would be a no-op), but given the problem states the clips are played through a manager that the scene skips, I assumed the manager path is the one in use.
