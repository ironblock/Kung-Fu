I wrote `src/hover.ts` using a custom `AnimationTask` that accumulates elapsed time, computes a triangle-wave offset (linear rise to 0.5 over 500 ms, linear fall back over the next 500 ms, repeating), and writes `restHeight + strength * 0.5 * triangle` to `node.position.y` each update. The returned closure simply reassigns the `strength` variable, so a new value takes effect on the next update without resetting the phase. I was not sure whether the problem intended the triangle's peak to be exactly at the midpoint of each half-second (i.e. a symmetric ramp) or something else, but "steady rate" in both directions makes the symmetric triangle the natural reading.
