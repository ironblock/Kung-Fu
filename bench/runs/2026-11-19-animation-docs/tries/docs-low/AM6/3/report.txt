I wrote the computed weight-blend values into `notes/am6-weights.json`. The conclusion is that a lone clip cannot fade to rest via weight alone — the weighted sum produces a fraction of the animated offset from origin, not a blend toward the rest pose — so an idle clip is required for a proper cross-fade to rest. I was not unsure of anything; the documentation's statement that "weights are not normalized" and that the blend is a plain weighted sum over only the contributing groups makes the arithmetic unambiguous.
