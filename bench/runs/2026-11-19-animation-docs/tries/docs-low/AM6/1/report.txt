I computed the four weighted-blend values from the documented Babylon.js-style formula (unnormalized weighted sums for translation, normalized weighted quaternion accumulation for rotation) and wrote them to `notes/am6-weights.json`. I'm confident in the translation answers; the rotation answer (`aloneHalfTurnDegrees = 90`) follows from the mathematical fact that normalizing a scalar-scaled single quaternion recovers the original direction, but I wasn't able to verify the exact behavior Babylon Lite uses when the accumulated quaternion is zero (weight = 0) since I couldn't inspect the implementation.
