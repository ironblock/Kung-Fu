I created `src/pose-mode.ts` with `freezeForPosing`, which pauses the group (preserving `currentTime`) and removes it from its owning `AnimationManager` so the per-frame tick no longer overwrites the nodes; the returned `resume` re-adds the group and calls `playAnimation`, which continues from the frozen time at the group's `speedRatio`. The one thing I was not sure of is whether your scene drives these groups through a standalone `AnimationManager` (the typical pattern shown in the docs) or directly via `scene.animationGroups` without a manager — if the latter, `getAnimationGroupOwner` returns `undefined` and the pause alone may not be enough to stop writes, since the state machine says paused glTF groups still evaluate on tick.
