I created `notes/scene-and-manager.json` with the four answers derived from the ownership model described in the docs: `addAnimationGroup` sets `_animationManager` on the group, which causes the scene's render loop to skip it (so the scene does not advance an attached clip); the manager's `updateAnimationManager(manager, 250)` advances it by 0.25 s; unattached sibling clips remain in `scene.animationGroups` and are advanced by the scene; and after `removeAnimationGroup` clears the ownership, the scene resumes advancing the clip. I was not uncertain about any of the four answers — the `_animationManager` field on `AnimationGroup` and the usage examples (which pair `addToScene` with `addAnimationGroups` + `onBeforeRender`-driven `updateAnimationManager` without any mention of double-advancement) make the hand-off unambiguous.
