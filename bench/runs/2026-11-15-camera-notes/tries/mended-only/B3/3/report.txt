I wrote `src/fly-controls.ts`, which wraps the library's `attachConfigurableFreeControl` with Space as the only rise key, PageDown as the only sink key, and Shift (either side) as a 3× speed multiplier. The default free-camera bindings put "down" on Shift, so the plain `attachFreeControl` would have sunk the camera on Shift. The returned function calls the library's detach function.
