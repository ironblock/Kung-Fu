I wrote `src/center-on.ts`. It converts latitude and longitude to a point on the sphere of radius `camera.planetRadius`, using north = +Z and east = -Y at longitude 0 (from note 16), so a heading of 90° looks toward increasing longitude. It assigns a new `Vector3` to `camera.center` because note 10 says in-place writes don't re-orbit the eye, and it leaves yaw, pitch and radius alone.

I'm not sure of a few things, since the library isn't on this machine and I couldn't compile or run anything:
- Whether `Vector3` is exported from `@babylonjs/lite`, and whether `center` is typed as `Vector3`.
- Whether the planet radius field is named `planetRadius`.
- Note 24 says a pending `limits` change is applied on any `center` assignment, so if limits were changed by hand, that assignment could clamp zoom or tilt.
