I wrote `/home/dev/54665a/app/src/focus.ts`. `focusOn` moves `camera.target` to `point` in place, then makes one alpha write that changes the value and restores the original alpha, so the new pose shows in the same call. Alpha, beta and radius are unchanged, so the camera moves by the offset to `point` and looks at it.
