It is unverified: `@babylonjs/lite` is not on this machine (the `tsconfig.json` path to `../babylon-lite` is also missing), so I could not check the function's real signature, compile, or run anything.

What I'm not sure of:

- **Behind-camera handling.** I assumed `z` is negative behind the camera. The notes don't say what `z` is for points behind the camera, and a depth ratio can land in 0..1 there too.
