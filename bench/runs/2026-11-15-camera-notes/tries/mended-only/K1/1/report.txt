I added `app/src/orbit-camera.ts`, which creates an ArcRotateCamera at radius 10 looking at the origin, calls `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls()`, and then attaches the mouse, touch and keyboard controls to the canvas with the scene passed in. I wrote it against the notes and my guesses about the library's API, so it is untested and unchecked.

Things I'm not sure of:
- **Library API shapes:** I couldn't find the library's type definitions or any existing source files in the app, so I guessed at `createArcRotateCamera(name, alpha, beta, radius, scene)`, at `attachControl(canvas, { scene, keyboard: true })`, and at `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls` being a free function. I also assumed `SceneContext` and `ArcRotateCamera` are exported from `@babylonjs/lite`. Any of these may not match 1.39.0, and the type checker would catch that.
- **Target:** I set the target's x/y/z in place, as your notes advise, rather than assigning a new object.
- **Canvas focus:** the notes say a canvas without a `tabindex` gets one automatically, so arrow keys should work once the canvas has focus.
