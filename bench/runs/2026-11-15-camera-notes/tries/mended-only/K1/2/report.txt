I added `src/orbit-camera.ts`, which exports `createOrbitCamera`. It creates an arc-rotate camera at radius 10 looking at the origin, calls `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls()`, then attaches the canvas controls with `keyboard: true`, and returns the camera.

I could not verify the API against Babylon Lite, so treat this as a draft. The library isn't on this machine, and I had no shell to list the project or check for a `SceneContext` type. I guessed these from the notes and from how the names are used there:

- The argument order of `createArcRotateCamera(name, alpha, beta, radius, target, scene)`.
- That `attachControl` is a camera method taking `{ keyboard: true }`.
- That `Vector3`, `SceneContext`, and `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls` are all exported from `@babylonjs/lite`.
- The starting alpha of 0 and beta of π/2, which I picked myself.
