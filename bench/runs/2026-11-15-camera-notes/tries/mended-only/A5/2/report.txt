I wrote `notes/inset-slide.json` with `x: 800, y: 300, width: 200, height: 150`, and `aspect: null`. I could not get the aspect ratio.

- **Rectangle:** This is derived from note 20 in `camera-notes.md`, not from running the library. The viewport's x range runs past the 1000 px edge, so the width is clamped to 200 px. The bottom-origin y is converted to top-origin, giving y = 300.
- **Aspect:** The notes don't say what `getEffectiveAspectRatio` returns, so I left it `null` instead of guessing. The answer depends on whether it uses the clamped rectangle (200/150 ≈ 1.33) or the unclamped one (300/150 = 2.00). Check this against the real library before deciding how to animate the inset.
