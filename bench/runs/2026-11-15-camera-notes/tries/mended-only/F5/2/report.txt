I wrote `/home/dev/049e40/app/notes/pointer-lock.json` with `lockedNoButton: false`, `lockedButtonHeld: true`, `rightDrag: true`, and `touchDrag: true`. The answers come from `camera-notes.md`, note 11, which says `attachFreeControl` turns on a drag with button 0, 1 or 2. I could not check the library itself.

Two of the answers are not fully settled:
- **`lockedButtonHeld`** is the one that matters for your shooter mode. The notes don't say whether the free-camera drag reads `clientX`/`clientY` or `movementX`/`movementY`. If it reads `clientX`, the locked drag will not turn the camera, and this should be `false`. Test it in the browser before you rely on it.
- **`touchDrag`** is inferred. A primary touch contact normally reports button 0, so it should turn, but the notes don't cover touch input for the free camera.
