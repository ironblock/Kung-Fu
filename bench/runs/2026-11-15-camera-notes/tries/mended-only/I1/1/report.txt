I wrote `notes/go-home.json` in the app with a = resolves, b = rejects, c = rejects, based only on note 7 in `camera-notes.md`. That note says the promise rejects when alpha, beta, radius or target change by anything other than the transition between its frames, including from a second call on the camera. A change made before the first frame becomes the start, and that first frame zeroes the inertia. I could not check this against the library, since it isn't on this machine. For (a), I'm not certain the coasting finishes before the first frame, so a late coasting step could in principle still count as an interfering change.
