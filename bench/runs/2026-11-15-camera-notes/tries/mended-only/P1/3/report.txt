I wrote `notes/preview-controls.json` with `{ "drag": false, "wheel": false, "pinch": true }`, based on your `camera-notes.md`. On the static preview page, mouse drag and the wheel leave `alpha`, `beta` and `radius` unchanged, because they only fill inertia offsets that are applied in the scene's render step. A two-finger pinch does change `radius` directly.

I could not check these against the library itself, so the answer depends on the notes being accurate for 1.39.0. The notes also say a touch drag always rotates the camera, but without a scene that rotation would not be applied, so QA should see no change from a one-finger drag either.
