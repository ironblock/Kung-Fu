Things I'm not sure of:
- **Imports:** I assumed `getViewMatrix`, `getViewProjectionMatrix` and `projectWorldToScreen` are exported from the package root. The `.d.ts` only shows which file each one lives in.
- **Canvas resize:** each side is projected at its own canvas size, so a resize between mark and shift shows up as movement. If you want only camera motion counted, that needs changing.
- **Camera viewport and floating origin:** I assumed `camera.viewport` is unset or full-canvas, and I did not pass `worldOrigin`. Points behind the camera are not handled.
- **Axis direction:** I assumed the returned `cssY` grows downward, which I inferred from the notes on viewport coordinates.
