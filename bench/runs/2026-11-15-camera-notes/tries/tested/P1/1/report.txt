I wrote `/home/dev/54eda9/app/notes/preview-controls.json` with `{ "drag": false, "wheel": false, "pinch": true }`. Drag and wheel only add to inertial offsets, and those are applied only by a per-frame hook on the scene, which a scene-less `attachControl` never gets (camera-notes.md item 1). A two-finger pinch sets `radius` directly with no scene frame needed (item 27), so it does change the camera.
