I wrote `notes/preview-controls.json` with `{ "drag": false, "wheel": false, "pinch": true }`. On the static preview page, mouse drag and the mouse wheel leave `alpha`, `beta` and `radius` unchanged, because without a scene they only add to inertia offsets that are applied in `_beforeRender`. A two-finger pinch writes `radius` directly, so it does change the camera.

I'm not certain of this because I only had your notes and the type declarations, not the library source, so I couldn't check the behaviour myself. The answer rests on notes 1 and 41 in `camera-notes.md`. The `.d.ts` file doesn't confirm either point. A one-finger touch drag also goes through the drag path, so QA should expect it to do nothing on the tablet.
