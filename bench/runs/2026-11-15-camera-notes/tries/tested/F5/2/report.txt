I wrote the answers to `/home/dev/938d18/app/notes/pointer-lock.json`, and that is the only file I changed. `lockedNoButton` and `rightDrag` follow from the notes; `lockedButtonHeld` and `touchDrag` are my best guesses and are not confirmed.

- **`lockedNoButton: false`** is supported. Note 11 says `attachFreeControl` only turns on a drag with button 0, 1 or 2, so a move with no button held does nothing.
- **`rightDrag: true`** is supported. Note 11 includes button 2 in the drag that turns the camera.
- **`lockedButtonHeld: false`** is a guess. A left-button drag should start a turn, but the notes don't say whether the turn is computed from `clientX`/`clientY` deltas or from `movementX`/`movementY`. If it uses `clientX`/`clientY`, the locked pointer gives zero deltas and the camera doesn't turn. Check the source or test it in a browser before you rely on this.
- **`touchDrag: true`** is a guess. Touch pointers normally report button 0, which should start the drag. The notes don't say whether touch input goes through the same path, and the browser may also use the touch for scrolling.
