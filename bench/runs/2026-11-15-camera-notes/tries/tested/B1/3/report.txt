I wrote `src/limited-orbit.ts`. It calls `setCameraLimits` with the radius bounds, which clamps the current distance immediately and every later radius write, then calls `attachControl` with the scene for drag, wheel and pinch. The returned function detaches the controls, releases the limits, and restores the camera's previous radius limit fields.
