I wrote `notes/arrow-keys.json` with `rotateFraction: 1.00`, `altFraction: null` and `ctrlSameDistance: null`. I could not measure anything, since the library isn't on this machine. The 1.00 comes from note 28, which says each held arrow adds its own offset per frame (Right to alpha, Up to beta), so Up shouldn't change how far alpha turns. The other two are null because the notes don't cover Alt+Right or Ctrl+Up, so I didn't want to guess. Also, the notes are a secondhand description of the library, not its source, so please check them against the real behaviour in a running scene before tuning anything.
