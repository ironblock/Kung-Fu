These come from reading `camera-notes.md` only, since I couldn't run the library; the answers depend on notes 9, 40 and 41-style key handling being accurate and on each key acting independently. Two things I'm not sure of: that Up with Right doesn't change how far Right turns alpha, and that Ctrl+Right and Ctrl+Up pan at the same per-frame speed. If they do, the diagonal pan covers about √2 (≈1.41) times the single-key distance. I'd confirm the first two on a real run before tuning anything.
