I wrote `app/src/label-position.ts`. It projects the point with `projectWorldToScreen`, which gives CSS-pixel `x`/`y`. It sets `visible` when the point is not `behindCamera` and lies inside the canvas, and it does not check depth, so far-off points still count as visible. I have not compiled or run it, since the library isn't on this machine.

Things I'm not sure of:
- **Imports:** I assumed `getEffectiveAspectRatio`, `getViewMatrix`, `getViewProjectionMatrix` and `projectWorldToScreen` are exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` index. The declarations only show where they are defined.
- **`behindCamera`:** I took it to mean "not in front of the camera." The declarations don't define it, and I didn't use `clipped` or `offscreen` for visibility because their meaning isn't documented.
- **Floating origin and ortho:** I did not handle `_useFloatingOrigin` (no `worldOrigin` is passed) or cameras with `ortho` set.
- **Viewport:** I assumed `camera.viewport` is unset or full-canvas, as you described.
- **Setup:** `tsconfig.json` maps `@babylonjs/lite` to `../babylon-lite/...`, which is also not on this machine.

I made no git commit because this directory is not a repository.
