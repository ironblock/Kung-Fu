I added `app/src/orbit-camera.ts`. It creates an arc-rotate camera at radius 10 looking at the origin, calls `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls()`, attaches the library's controls to the canvas with `keyboard: true` and the scene passed in, and returns the camera.

I was not able to check this against the library. I couldn't find `src/` contents or the library source on this machine, so I don't know whether `@babylonjs/lite` re-exports these names at the top level. I also haven't compiled it. Other things to know:

- I chose alpha = -π/2 and beta = π/2 (camera on the horizon, at z = -10). You didn't specify an angle, so change these if you want a different starting view.
- The function `attachControl` returns to remove its listeners is discarded, since the signature returns only the camera. Nothing can detach the controls later without changing that.
- I did not set any limits on the camera.
