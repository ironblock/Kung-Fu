I wrote `src/fly-controls.ts`, which wraps `attachConfigurableFreeControl` with Space/PageUp as up, PageDown as down, Shift (left or right) as a 3× speed key, and the returned disposer passed straight through. I did not use the default `attachFreeControl`, because note 11 says it treats Shift as move-down.

I could not verify several things, and nothing has been compiled or run:
- The declarations don't show whether `attachConfigurableFreeControl` is exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` root, so the import may fail.
- The declarations don't say whether WASD, arrows, and mouse look are on by default in the configurable variant. I expect they are, since it replaces only the up/down/fast keys, but I haven't confirmed it.
- I assumed `fastMultiplier` applies to all movement, and that the returned disposer removes the canvas listeners and the per-frame callback.
