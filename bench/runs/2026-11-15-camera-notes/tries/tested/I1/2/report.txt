I wrote `notes/go-home.json` in the app: (a) resolves, (b) rejects, (c) rejects. The reasoning comes from note 12 in `camera-notes.md`: the transition rejects when anything other than itself changes alpha, beta, radius or target between its frames (including attachControl inertia), and its first frame zeroes all five inertial offsets. So coasting is wiped out at the first frame and doesn't count as an outside change, a mouse drag keeps writing those offsets and so rejects it, and a second `goHome()` writes alpha and radius from its own transition, which rejects the first one. The notes don't say how the second call treats the first one directly, and I didn't pass an `AbortSignal`, so the only thing that can reject the first call is the mid-flight change.

I'm not fully sure of (a). The notes say the first frame zeroes the offsets, but not whether attachControl's coasting moves the camera in the same frame before or after that zeroing, or whether anything changes between `goHome()` and the first frame. I'd treat (a) as likely but worth checking in a test with a real flick, and put the try/catch around the `await` in `goHome()` to cover (b) and (c).
