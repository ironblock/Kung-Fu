- **centerSelf (2000):** Note 27 lists a center write as a trigger for the re-clamp, but no note says a center write clamps radius specifically. Treat this as less certain than radiusSelf.
- **emptyOrientation (2000):** The notes don't cover `setGeospatialOrientation` with an empty object. I assumed it writes the current values back through the same clamping path. This is a guess.

Before relying on `centerSelf` or `emptyOrientation`, check them against the library source or a quick run. For the mode switch, `camera.radius = camera.radius;` right after the limit line is the safe choice.
