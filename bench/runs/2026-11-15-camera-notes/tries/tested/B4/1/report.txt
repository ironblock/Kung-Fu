I wrote `src/globe-tilt.ts`. It sets `camera.pitch` directly and leaves yaw, radius and centre alone. Before the write it switches off `limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale` and raises `limits.pitchMax` to at least `pitch`, because the default radius clamps pitch to `pitchMin`. It throws if the pitch comes back clamped.

I could not run or type-check this, since the library isn't on the machine. The notes don't say what `null` for `pitchDisabledRadiusScale` means or how `getEffectivePitchMax` works, so I assumed `null` removes the radius-dependent limit. If it doesn't, the function will throw rather than silently clamp, and that is the first thing to test against the real library.
