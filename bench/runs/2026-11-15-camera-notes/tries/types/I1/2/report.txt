I wrote the answers to `/home/dev/280e3d/app/notes/go-home.json`: (a) resolves, (b) rejects, (c) rejects. The only file I changed is that one.

The answers come from the doc comment on `interpolateArcRotateCamera` in `camera-api.d.ts`. That comment says the transition cancels and the promise rejects when a user drag, zoom, or pan changes the pose, when inertia decays, or when a new interpolation starts on the same camera. It also says the first frame discards leftover inertia, which is why I expect (a) to resolve.

I could not check any of this against the library's runtime behaviour, since it isn't on this machine. Case (a) is the least certain: I'm relying on the first-frame inertia discard described in the comment, so the flick's coast is cut off rather than carried into the move. In (b) and (c), the rejection will be unhandled unless the `await` sits inside a try/catch. That is the case that makes the try/catch necessary.
