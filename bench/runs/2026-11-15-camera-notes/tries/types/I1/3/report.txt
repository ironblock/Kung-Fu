I wrote `notes/go-home.json` with `a: "resolves"`, `b: "rejects"`, `c: "rejects"`. The only change is that new file, under `/home/dev/661422/app/notes/`.

The answers come from the doc comment on `interpolateArcRotateCamera` in `camera-api.d.ts`:
- **(a)** resolves. The first frame of the transition discards leftover inertia, and you touch nothing afterwards.
- **(b)** rejects. A user drag cancels the transition.
- **(c)** rejects. A superseding interpolation cancels the first one, so the first `await` throws. The second call is the one that resolves.

For the try/catch: `goHome()` needs a catch for (b) and (c), because the `await` throws there. The declarations don't say what the rejection reason is, so check for it by type, not by message.
