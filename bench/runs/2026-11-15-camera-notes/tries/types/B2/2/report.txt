I wrote `/home/dev/cecc91/app/src/fast-orbit.ts`. It halves `camera.angularSensibility` (the library's drag-sensitivity setting, where higher means slower) and then calls `attachControl` with the defaults, so mouse, touch and wheel behave as the library does. The returned function detaches the controls and restores the original sensitivity.

- **I'm assuming touch rotation and the glide use the same setting.** The declarations describe the touch and inertia behaviour but don't say which field they read.
- **I used the camera's current sensitivity as the baseline,** not the library's built-in default. If your camera was already customized, the result is half that value.
