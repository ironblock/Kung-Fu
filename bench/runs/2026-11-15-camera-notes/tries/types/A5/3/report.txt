I'm not sure of two things:
- **Clipping:** the inset's right edge is at x = 1100, past the 1000 px canvas. The docs don't say whether `resolveCameraViewport` clips to the target. If it does, the width would be 200 and the aspect about 1.33, so the frame and picture would line up differently during the slide.
- **Y conversion:** I took the docs' statement that Babylon's y is measured from the bottom and converted it to top-origin pixels as (1 − 0.1 − 0.3) × 500 = 300. I did not check this against the implementation.

Running both functions once in the app with that viewport would settle both points.
