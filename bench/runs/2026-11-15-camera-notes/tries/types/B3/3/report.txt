I wrote `src/fly-controls.ts`, which wraps the library's `attachConfigurableFreeControl` with Space as up, PageDown as down, and Shift (left or right) as a 3x speed key. I overrode the default down keys because they include both Shift keys, which would have made Shift sink the camera. I couldn't run or type-check it, and I'm not sure of a few things:

- The declarations don't show the package's root export, so I assumed `attachConfigurableFreeControl` is exported from `"@babylonjs/lite"`.
- The defaults also bind PageUp to rise. I removed that, since you only asked for Space, so PageUp does nothing now. Add it back to `upKeys` if you want it.
- I assumed the key names match `KeyboardEvent.code` and that the returned cleanup removes everything, as the declaration comments say. Neither is verified without the library.
