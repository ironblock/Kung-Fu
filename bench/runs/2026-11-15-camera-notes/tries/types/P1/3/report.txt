I wrote `notes/preview-controls.json` with `{ "drag": false, "wheel": false, "pinch": true }`. The declarations say mouse drag and wheel only feed inertia offsets, which are applied from the scene's render loop, so without a scene they don't move `alpha`, `beta` or `radius`. Pinch is documented as a direct zoom with no inertia, so it still changes `radius`.
