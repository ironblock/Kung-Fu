I wrote `src/center-on.ts` in `/home/dev/ec8852/app`. It builds the lat/lon frame at a fresh camera's default centre, using the camera's own yaw (0 = north, π/2 = east) to fix north and east, then moves the centre with `setGeospatialOrientation`, which leaves zoom, yaw and pitch unchanged. I haven't compiled or run it, because the library isn't on this machine.

Things I'm not sure of:
- I assumed `@babylonjs/lite` re-exports `computeLookAtFromYawPitch`, `createGeospatialCamera` and `setGeospatialOrientation` from the root. I couldn't check the package index.
- I assumed `computeLookAtFromYawPitch` returns the viewing direction (so pitch π/2 gives a horizontal look along the yaw), and that a new camera's `center` is the lat 0, lon 0 point, as your spec says.
- I used a structural `{x, y, z}` type rather than the library's `Vec3`, in case `Vec3` is not exported.
