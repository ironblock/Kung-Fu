I wrote `/home/dev/f078b8/app/src/focus.ts`. `focusOn` sets `camera.target` to `point` by writing its x, y and z in place, leaving alpha, beta and radius alone, so the camera keeps its angles and distance and looks at the new point. It also zeroes the camera's pending inertia offsets so leftover drag or zoom momentum can't pull the camera off `point`.
