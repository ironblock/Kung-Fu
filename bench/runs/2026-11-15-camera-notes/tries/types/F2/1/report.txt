I wrote `src/walkthrough.ts`. It creates a free camera with `createFreeCamera`, sets it as `scene.camera`, and attaches the library's `attachFreeControl`. `jumpTo` detaches the controls, moves the camera's position and target, and reattaches them. `dispose` detaches them and makes later `jumpTo` calls no-ops. I could not compile or run it because the library isn't on this machine.

I'm not sure of these points:
- **Look direction after `jumpTo`:** the declarations don't say how the controls track look direction. `_yaw`/`_pitch` are internal, and I didn't write them because I couldn't confirm their conventions. If the controls keep those values and ignore `target`, W and mouse drag may still use the old direction after a jump.
- **Imports and types:** I assumed `createFreeCamera`, `attachFreeControl`, `FreeCamera` and `SceneContext` are exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` index, that `scene.camera` accepts a `FreeCamera`, and that `position` and `target` can be set field by field.
