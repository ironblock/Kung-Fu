I wrote `src/spin-around.ts`. It turns the heading in four quarter-turn flights through `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync`, keeping centre, pitch and radius fixed, then restores the exact starting yaw. Nothing has been compiled or run, since the library isn't on this machine.

Things I'm not sure of:
- **Render-loop hook:** the declarations have no public per-frame callback, so I used the flight API, which runs on the scene's loop. I'm assuming it is exported from the package root.
- **Interruption:** if a segment doesn't land on its target heading (user input, a newer flight, or yaw limits), the spin stops and the promise resolves early. The check also assumes a flight lands exactly on its target yaw.
- **Easing:** I used linear easing so the speed stays constant across quarter-turn boundaries. Ease-in-out would pause at each quarter.
- **Short durations:** I didn't test the 1-second end, where each flight lasts about 250 ms.
