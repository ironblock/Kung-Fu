I wrote `app/src/spin-around.ts`. It eases a full eastward yaw turn (always increasing), writes the start yaw back exactly at the end, leaves center, pitch and radius alone, and resolves when done. Nothing has been compiled or run, since the library isn't on this machine.

Things I'm not sure of:

- **The frame driver is not the scene's render loop.** The provided type files don't declare a per-frame hook on `SceneContext`, and the notes only mention an internal `scene._beforeRender` without its shape. So the turn is driven by `requestAnimationFrame`. To use the scene's loop, swap the `requestAnimationFrame` calls in `step` for that hook.
- **Yaw limits are lifted during the spin.** If `camera.limits.yawMin`/`yawMax` are set, they would clamp the turn, so I set them to ±Infinity and restore them afterwards.
- **Concurrent input will fight the spin.** Geospatial controls that rotate yaw will be overwritten each frame, and in a background tab the turn may jump to its end.
