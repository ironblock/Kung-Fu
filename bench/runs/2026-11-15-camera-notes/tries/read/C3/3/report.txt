I wrote `notes/arrow-keys.json` with `rotateFraction` 1.00, `altFraction` 1.00 and `ctrlSameDistance` false, but these are inferences from the notes and type declarations, not measurements, since I couldn't run the library. The notes say Arrows rotate, Ctrl+arrows pan and Alt+Up/Down zoom, but they don't describe how simultaneous keys combine or what Alt+Right does. The 1.00 values assume each key contributes independently, and the false assumes Right and Up pan on separate axes, so a diagonal move is about √2 times the single-key distance. Please check the first two against a real run before relying on them.
