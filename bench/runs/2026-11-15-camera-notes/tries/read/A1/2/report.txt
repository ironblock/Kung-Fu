Things I was not sure of:

- **Same-frame update:** I rely on note 3 (writing `target.x/y/z` marks the camera dirty) for the view to update in the same frame. I could not check this against the source.
- **Type of `target`:** the declarations type `target` as `Vec3`, so I used per-component writes. I did not check whether `ObservableVec3` also exposes `set()`.
