I wrote `app/src/center-on.ts`. `centerOnLatLon` converts latitude and longitude to a point on the sphere of `camera.limits.planetRadius`, then calls `setGeospatialOrientation` with that point as the centre and the camera's current yaw, pitch and radius, so zoom, heading and tilt are unchanged.

I'm not sure of the east direction. The notes say yaw 0 is north (+Z) and yaw π/2 is east, but not which world axis east is at the default centre. I assumed a right-handed east-north-up frame, which puts east at +Y for longitude 0. If the camera's yaw turns the other way, positive longitudes will point the wrong way, and the fix is to negate the `y` term. I haven't compiled or run this, since the library isn't on the machine.
