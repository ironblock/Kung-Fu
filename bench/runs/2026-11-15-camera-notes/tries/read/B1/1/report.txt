I wrote `src/limited-orbit.ts`. It sets the radius bounds with `setCameraLimits`, which clamps on every radius write, then calls `attachControl` with the scene so the orbit, wheel and pinch controls actually move the camera. The returned function removes the controls, stops the clamping, and restores the camera's previous radius limits.
