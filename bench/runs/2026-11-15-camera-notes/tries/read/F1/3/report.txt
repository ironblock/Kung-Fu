I wrote `app/src/big-world-fly.ts`. It wraps `attachConfigurableFreeControl` with every movement key in `fastKeys` and `fastMultiplier: 20`, so mouse look is untouched and the returned disposer removes everything. I haven't compiled or run it, since the library isn't on this machine.

I'm not certain of a few things:
- **Mouse look:** The notes document `attachFreeControl`'s drag behaviour but not `attachConfigurableFreeControl`'s. I assumed they match. If they don't, the drag turn will differ from the default.
- **Multiplier semantics:** The notes don't say whether `fastMultiplier` scales each key's per-frame increment before inertia. That's the assumption behind the 20x result.
- **Key names:** I used `KeyboardEvent.code` values such as `KeyW`, `ShiftLeft` and `Space`. The notes say matching is by code, but I haven't confirmed the exact string format the option expects.
- **Import path:** I assumed `attachConfigurableFreeControl` is exported from the package root. I couldn't check that.
