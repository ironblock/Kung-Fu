The frame and the picture will not line up during the slide as things stand. `resolveCameraViewport` clamps the viewport to the canvas, so the frame is 200×150 px (aspect 1.33). `getEffectiveAspectRatio` uses the unclamped normalized width and height, so it gives 2.0. You will need to draw the inset at the clamped size or otherwise reconcile the two.

I'm not sure of the exact clamping rule, since I only have your notes and not the source. I took the clamp to cut the viewport at the canvas's right edge, which gives a width of 200. I also worked out the y-flip from the note that `camera.viewport` y is measured from the bottom.
