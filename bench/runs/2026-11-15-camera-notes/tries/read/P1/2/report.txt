I wrote `notes/preview-controls.json` in the app with `{ "drag": false, "wheel": false, "pinch": true }`. This comes from the camera notes (items 2, 27, 33 and 34), since the library isn't on this machine to check against.

What I'm not sure of:
- The answers rest entirely on the notes, which I could not check against the library source. The `.d.ts` file only gives signatures.
- The notes say drag and wheel move the camera only from `scene._beforeRender`. With no scene, they only fill inertial offsets. I took that to mean `alpha`, `beta` and `radius` stay unchanged for drag and wheel.
- Pinch is the exception and sets `radius` directly. I'm reading that as true on a tablet, but QA should confirm it on a real device.
- On a laptop trackpad, a pinch usually arrives as wheel events, which the notes say do nothing here. QA may see no zoom there, which is expected.
