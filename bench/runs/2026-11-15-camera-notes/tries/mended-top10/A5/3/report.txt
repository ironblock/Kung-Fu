I could not get the real values, so `notes/inset-slide.json` holds `null` placeholders for all five fields. The declarations give only signatures, and the notes don't cover viewport resolution, so I didn't want to guess what `resolveCameraViewport` or `getEffectiveAspectRatio` return. Running them with the inset's viewport at 1000 × 500 will give the real numbers. Before you animate differently, check whether `resolveCameraViewport` clamps the 0.8 + 0.3 = 1.1 overflow to the canvas edge, and whether `getEffectiveAspectRatio` reflects that clipping or just returns the canvas ratio.
