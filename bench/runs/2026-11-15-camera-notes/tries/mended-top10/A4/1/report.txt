I wrote `notes/over-the-top.json` with `eyeX` ≈ -4.794, `eyeY` ≈ 8.776, `upright: true`, and `sameAsOtherSide: true`. That is the only file I changed. I worked it out by hand, since the library isn't on this machine and I could not run anything.

Things I'm not sure of:
- **No source was available.** I assumed the standard Babylon arc-rotate placement: `x = r·cos(alpha)·sin(beta)`, `y = r·cos(beta)`, `z = r·sin(alpha)·sin(beta)`, with a fixed world-up. The type declarations support the fixed up, since `ArcRotateCamera` has no `upVector` while `BankedFreeCamera` does. The notes don't cover beta, so I also assumed nothing clamps beta when no limits are set.
- **`sameAsOtherSide` holds in exact arithmetic, not bit-for-bit.** The camera lands on the same point as `createArcRotateCamera(Math.PI, 0.5, 10, …)`, but floating-point `sin(Math.PI)` leaves that camera's z about 6e-16 off, where mine is exactly 0.
