I added `src/orbit-camera.ts` to the app. `createOrbitCamera` creates an ArcRotate camera at radius 10 looking at the origin, calls `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls()`, attaches the library's controls with the scene and `keyboard: true`, and returns the camera.

Things I'm not sure of:
- **Imports:** I took `createArcRotateCamera`, `attachControl`, `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls`, `ArcRotateCamera` and `SceneContext` to be exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` root. The declarations only show internal module paths, so I couldn't confirm the package exports them under those names.
- **Angles:** the alpha and beta values (`-π/2` and `π/2.5`) are my choice, since you didn't specify a starting view.
- **Dispose:** `attachControl` returns a dispose function, which I discard because the function signature only returns the camera. Nothing in the app will be able to detach the controls.
- **Untested:** I couldn't run or type-check this, since the library isn't installed here.
