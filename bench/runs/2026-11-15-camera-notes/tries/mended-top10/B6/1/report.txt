I wrote `src/chase-camera.ts` in `/home/dev/29e2df/app`. It builds the camera with `createBankedFreeCamera`, which has a writable `upVector`. Each `bank(up)` call projects the car's up onto the plane perpendicular to the line of sight and writes that into `upVector`. If the car's up is along the line of sight, it changes nothing.
