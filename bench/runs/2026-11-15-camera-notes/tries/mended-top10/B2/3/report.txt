I wrote `src/fast-orbit.ts`. It attaches the library's own `attachControl` and halves `camera.angularSensibility` while attached, which should make drags and the glide turn twice as far. The returned function calls the library's detach and restores the original value. I could not verify this, so please check it before relying on it.

The main uncertainty is direction. I'm assuming `angularSensibility` is a divisor (higher = slower, as in Babylon.js), and your notes and typings don't say so. If it is a multiplier, this halves the speed instead, and the fix is to multiply rather than divide. I also assumed `attachControl` is exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` root, and that pinch zoom doesn't read `angularSensibility`. A quick test would settle it: move the mouse a known distance and check that alpha changes by twice the default amount.
