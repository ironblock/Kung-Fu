I wrote `src/walkthrough.ts` in `/home/dev/aa21a3/app`. `createWalkthrough` builds a free camera at `eye` looking at `target`, sets it as `scene.camera`, and attaches the library's `attachFreeControl`. `jumpTo` writes the new position and target in place and copies `_yaw`/`_pitch` from a throwaway camera built from the new eye and target, so the controls aim the right way without my guessing the yaw/pitch convention. `dispose` calls the controls' own disposer.
