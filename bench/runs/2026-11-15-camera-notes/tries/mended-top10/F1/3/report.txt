I wrote `/home/dev/de9792/app/src/big-world-fly.ts`. It attaches the library's `attachFreeControl` and multiplies `camera.speed` by 20 before attaching, so keyboard movement is 20× while mouse look (which uses `angularSensitivity`) is untouched. The returned function detaches the controls, restores the original speed, and is safe to call twice. Nothing runs per frame.

I'm not sure of several things, since the library isn't here and the notes don't cover the free-camera code:
- I haven't checked that `attachFreeControl` and `SceneContext` are exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` root. If they aren't, the import line needs changing.
- The 20× result assumes keyboard movement scales linearly with `speed` and that no other code in the library reads `speed` differently. I couldn't confirm either.
- The module is unbuilt and untested.
