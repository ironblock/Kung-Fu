I wrote `src/focus.ts` at `/home/dev/63f3bd/app/src/focus.ts`. `focusOn` writes `point` into `camera.target` in place, then nudges radius (or alpha or beta, if radius won't move) by a tiny amount and restores it. The notes say the target change only takes effect on an angle or distance write that changes value, so the nudge makes the new pose show in the same frame while the angles and distance stay the same.

I haven't compiled or run this, since the library isn't on the machine. I'm not sure of: whether the library's dirty tracking works the way the notes describe, and whether clamping or a nudge of 1e-9 could cause trouble. The function also doesn't clear any pending inertial offsets, so a drag in progress can still move the camera after the call.
