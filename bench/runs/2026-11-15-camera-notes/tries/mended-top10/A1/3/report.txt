I wrote `/home/dev/045fd9/app/src/focus.ts`. `focusOn` writes the new point into `camera.target` in place and leaves `alpha`, `beta` and `radius` alone, so the camera moves by the same offset as the target and keeps its angles and distance. I also zero the pending drag, wheel and pan inertia fields so they can't pull the camera off the point on the next render. I couldn't run it because the library isn't on this machine. The notes say an in-place target write refreshes `worldMatrix` at once, but I couldn't confirm that, so check the label position right after a call. Note also that the inertia reset is my addition, and a running `interpolateArcRotateCamera` will reject with "interrupted" when the target changes.
