I wrote `src/focus.ts` in `/home/dev/b7ac13/app/src/focus.ts`. `focusOn` sets `camera.target` to `point` by editing its x, y and z in place, which moves the camera by the same offset and keeps its angles and distance. It then makes the view matrix rebuild in the same call by nudging alpha, beta or radius by 1e-9 and restoring the original value, and reads `worldMatrix`.
