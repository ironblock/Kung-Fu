I read both files and wrote `{ "drag": false, "wheel": false, "pinch": true }` to `/home/dev/a249c4/app/notes/preview-controls.json`. According to notes 1 and 30, drag and wheel only add to the inertial offsets, and those are applied by a per-frame callback that never runs without a scene. A two-finger pinch sets `radius` directly, with or without a scene.

This comes only from the notes, since I couldn't run the library. One thing to warn QA about: on a laptop, a trackpad pinch usually reaches the browser as wheel events, not two touch pointers. So I'd expect it to do nothing there, while a touchscreen pinch on the tablet should zoom.
