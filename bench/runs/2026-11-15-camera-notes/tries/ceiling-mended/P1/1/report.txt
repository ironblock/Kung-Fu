I wrote `notes/preview-controls.json` with `{ "drag": false, "wheel": false, "pinch": true }`. Your notes say that without a scene, drags and the wheel only add to the camera's inertial offsets, and nothing ever applies those to `alpha`, `beta` or `radius` because there's no render loop (notes 1 and 13). A two-finger touch pinch sets `radius` straight away and doesn't need a frame (notes 1 and 27).

I couldn't run anything to check this, since it rests only on your notes. One thing I'm unsure of: on a laptop, a trackpad pinch usually reaches the page as wheel events (with Ctrl held), not as two touch points. So the "pinch changes the camera" result may only hold on the tablet, and a laptop trackpad pinch may do nothing, like the wheel.
