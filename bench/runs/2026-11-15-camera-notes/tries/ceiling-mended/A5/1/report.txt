I worked out both values by hand from the notes and wrote them to `/home/dev/d2b352/app/notes/inset-slide.json` as `{ "x": 800, "y": 300, "width": 200, "height": 150, "aspect": 2 }`. They won't line up during the slide. Note 25 says `resolveCameraViewport` cuts the frame down at the canvas edge, to 200 × 150 (about 1.33:1). But `getEffectiveAspectRatio` is worked out from the full viewport, which gives 1000/500 × 0.3/0.3 = 2.00.

What I wasn't sure of: I couldn't run the library, so these come from the notes, not from real output. Cutting at the edge gives 300 → 200 for the width. The notes don't say whether the result is rounded, so the library may return something like 199.99999999999997 instead of exactly 200. Nothing I have says how the GPU viewport (`_applyCameraViewport`) deals with the part past the edge. So I can't tell whether the picture is squeezed into the 200 px or just cut off. Either way it won't match a 200 × 150 frame, so you'll probably need to animate the inset differently, for example by keeping the viewport inside the canvas.
