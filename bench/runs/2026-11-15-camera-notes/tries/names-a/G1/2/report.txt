I wrote `src/mode-limits.ts` in `/home/dev/0fb4f3/app`. `useModeLimits` sets `camera.limits` to the mode's object. It also replaces `radius` and `yaw` with accessors that clamp to that mode's `radiusMin`/`radiusMax` and `yawMin`/`yawMax`, and wraps `_setOrientation` so its arguments are clamped too. Switching modes updates the stored limits and re-clamps the current values.

- **Yaw is clamped, not wrapped.** I treated `yawMin`/`yawMax` as a plain range. If the library expects wrap-around headings, this needs changing.
- **`setGeospatialOrientation` and other internal writes may bypass the guards.** The declarations don't show whether they go through `_setOrientation` or write private fields.
- **I did not use `clampZoomDistance`.** Its exact semantics aren't in the declarations, so the radius clamp is a plain min/max.
