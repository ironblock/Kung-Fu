I wrote `src/mode-limits.ts` in `/home/dev/44136a/app`. `useModeLimits` installs accessors on the camera's `radius` and `yaw` that clamp every write to the active mode's `radiusMin..radiusMax` and `yawMin..yawMax`, wraps `_setOrientation` the same way, sets `camera.limits` to the mode's object, and re-clamps the current values when the mode changes. The `limits` object is only read, never modified.
