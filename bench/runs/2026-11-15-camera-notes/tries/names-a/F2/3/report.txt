I wrote `app/src/walkthrough.ts`. Each `jumpTo` builds a fresh `FreeCamera` with `createFreeCamera(eye, target)`, sets it as `scene.camera`, and reattaches `attachFreeControl` to it. I did this because the declarations show `_yaw`/`_pitch` as internal state, so writing `position`/`target` onto the existing camera would likely leave W and mouse drag pointing the wrong way. I have not compiled or run it, and the library isn't here to check against. Things I'm unsure of:

- I couldn't see `SceneContext` or `ObservableVec3`, so I'm assuming `scene.camera` is writable and that `createFreeCamera` and `attachFreeControl` are exported from the package root.
- I'm also assuming that `createFreeCamera` derives the yaw/pitch from the target, which I haven't confirmed.
- `attachFreeControl` is the standard control I used; if your "usual" controls are a different attach function, that line needs changing.
