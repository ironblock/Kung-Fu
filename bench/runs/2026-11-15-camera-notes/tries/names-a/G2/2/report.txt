I wrote `/home/dev/7bf77c/app/src/center-on.ts`. It converts latitude and longitude to a point on the planet sphere (radius from `camera.limits.planetRadius`) and sets the camera's centre there with `setGeospatialOrientation`, which leaves yaw, pitch and radius alone.

I'm not sure of the library's axis convention. The declarations don't give the default centre, the up axis, or how yaw maps to east and north, and I couldn't check the library. I assumed a Y-up planet with lat 0, lon 0 on +Z, and longitude increasing toward +X. If `createGeospatialCamera`'s default centre isn't on +Z, or the east and north axes differ, the mapping in the file needs changing. I also haven't confirmed that the package root exports `setGeospatialOrientation`; if it doesn't, the import will fail to compile.
