I wrote `/home/dev/cee56d/app/src/center-on.ts`. `centerOnLatLon` sets `camera.center` to the surface point at the given latitude and longitude, on a sphere of `camera.limits.planetRadius`, and leaves yaw, pitch and radius alone.

I'm not sure of three things, and I couldn't check any of them because the declarations don't cover them and the library isn't here:

- **Axis convention.** I assumed the north pole is on +Y, latitude 0 and longitude 0 are on +Z, and longitude increases toward +X. If the library uses a different convention, the point will be wrong. The change is confined to the three `camera.center` assignments.
- **East and heading.** I couldn't confirm that a heading of 90° looks toward positive longitude under the library's yaw convention.
- **Cache and default centre.** I assigned `center` directly, without checking whether the library caches the look-at vector (`_lookAt`) and needs an update call, and I couldn't confirm that a new camera's default centre is latitude 0, longitude 0. I also haven't type-checked or run the file.
