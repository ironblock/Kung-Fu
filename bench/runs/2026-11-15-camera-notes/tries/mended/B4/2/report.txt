I wrote `/home/dev/4b3268/app/src/globe-tilt.ts`. It turns off the camera's radius-dependent pitch cap (`limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale = null`), reassigns `center` to a copy of its current values to apply that change, then sets `camera.pitch`. Zoom, heading and centre are never written. I could not run or type-check it because the library isn't on this machine.

I'm not sure of these points:
- Turning off the pitch cap is the only way I found to hold a tilt at the default far zoom. The cap is what forces pitch toward 0 there. As a side effect, this camera's pitch is no longer limited by zoom at all, including close in.
- If the centre sits exactly on a pole, the library moves it off the pole. In that case the centre would change.
- I took the library's behaviour from `camera-notes.md` (notes 5, 15, 24 and 10) and the `.d.ts` types. I did not confirm either against the real library.
