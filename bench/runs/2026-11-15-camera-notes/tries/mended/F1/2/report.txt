I wrote `/home/dev/0bb797/app/src/big-world-fly.ts`. It calls the library's `attachFreeControl` unchanged, so mouse look and key handling stay the library's own. Each animation frame it measures how far the library moved the camera and adds 19 more times that distance to the position and target, which makes movement 20 times as far. I left `speed` alone because note 33 ties the look cutoff to it. The returned function removes the key and blur listeners, cancels the frame loop, and calls the library's dispose.
