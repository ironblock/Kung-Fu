I wrote `/home/dev/13e102/app/src/focus.ts`. `focusOn` sets `target` to `point` by editing its x, y and z in place, so alpha, beta and radius stay the same and the camera moves by the same offset. It also zeroes the pending pan inertia so the orbit centre doesn't drift off `point`. Then it nudges alpha by 1e-7 and back, because note 5 says an in-place target edit only shows after an alpha, beta or radius change.

I could not compile or run this: the library isn't on the machine, and I only had the type declarations and the notes. The nudge trick is based on my reading of note 5, and I haven't confirmed it. Two other things to know: zeroing the pan inertia is my choice, not something you asked for, and calling `focusOn` while an `interpolateArcRotateCamera` is running will make that promise reject, per note 7.
