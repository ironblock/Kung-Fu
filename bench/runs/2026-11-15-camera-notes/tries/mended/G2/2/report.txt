I created `app/src/center-on.ts`. It sets `camera.center` to a new object at the surface point for the given latitude and longitude, on a sphere of radius `camera.limits.planetRadius`, and leaves `yaw`, `pitch` and `radius` alone. I haven't compiled or run it, since the library isn't on this machine.

I'm not sure about the axis convention. I took it from note 16 in `camera-notes.md`, which I could only confirm at a centre on +X, so the east direction at other centres is an assumption. If east is wrong there, longitude will come out mirrored.
