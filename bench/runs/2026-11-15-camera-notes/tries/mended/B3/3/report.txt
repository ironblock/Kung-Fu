I wrote `src/fly-controls.ts`, which wraps `attachConfigurableFreeControl` with Space as up, PageDown as down, and Shift (left or right) as a 3× speed key. I used the configurable variant because the stock `attachFreeControl` binds Shift to "down" (note 31), which would sink the camera when Shift is held. I could not run or type-check it, and I'm not sure of three things:

- **The import:** the declarations are in `configurable-free-camera-controls.d.ts`, but I don't know whether `attachConfigurableFreeControl` is exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` root. If it isn't, the import needs changing.
- **Default movement and look keys:** I assumed the configurable variant keeps WASD, the arrows, and mouse drag as defaults, since the notes don't say. I haven't confirmed that.
- **Cleanup:** the notes don't say whether the returned disposer removes the per-frame hook for free-camera controls. I'm relying on it doing so.
