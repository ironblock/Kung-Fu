Things I was not sure of:

- **Repeat calls:** I don't know whether `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls()` is safe to call more than once. Calling `createOrbitCamera` twice would call it twice.
- **Starting angles:** I chose alpha −π/2 and beta π/2 for the starting view, since you didn't specify any.
