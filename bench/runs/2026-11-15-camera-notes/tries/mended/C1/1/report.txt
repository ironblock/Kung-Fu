I wrote `src/steady-zoom.ts` in `/home/dev/b8903c/app/src/`. It calls the library's `attachControl` for drag, pan and pinch, and replaces only the wheel. The distance per notch is fixed when `attachSteadyZoom` is called, from the camera's radius and `wheelPrecision` at that moment. Each notch adds that same amount to `inertialRadiusOffset`, so the library's inertia still applies. The returned function removes our wheel listener and calls the library's dispose. I haven't run or type-checked it, since the library isn't on this machine.

Things I'm not sure of:

- **Wheel listener target:** I assumed the library's wheel handler is on the canvas, and I rely on our capture-phase listener running first to suppress it. If the library listens elsewhere, wheel zoom would double up.
- **Inertia changes:** the distance is fixed for the radius and `wheelPrecision` at attach time, but the library's inertia is read at each frame. If the app changes `inertia` while attached, the total distance per notch changes too.
- **Leftover offset:** a notch's pending `inertialRadiusOffset` may still be on the camera after detach, because the library's dispose doesn't clear it.
