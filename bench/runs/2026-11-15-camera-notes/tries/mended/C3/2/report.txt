I wrote `notes/arrow-keys.json` with `rotateFraction` 0.71, `altFraction` null, and `ctrlSameDistance` true. These come from your `camera-notes.md`, not from running the library, since it isn't on this machine. I left `altFraction` as null because the notes don't cover Alt, so I couldn't derive a value.
