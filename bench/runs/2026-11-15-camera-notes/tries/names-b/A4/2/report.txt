I wrote `notes/over-the-top.json` with `eyeX` -4.794, `eyeY` 8.776, `upright` true, and `sameAsOtherSide` true. Nothing else in the app changed.

The `.d.ts` file only has signatures, not the position formula or the beta clamping logic, so these values rest on the standard arc-rotate convention (as in Babylon.js), which I could not check against Babylon Lite 1.39.0. The main risk is if the library clamps `beta` when it is assigned or when limits are unset. In that case the camera would sit near the top pole and the answers would change. A quick check would be to read `camera.beta` and the camera position after the assignment in your running app.
