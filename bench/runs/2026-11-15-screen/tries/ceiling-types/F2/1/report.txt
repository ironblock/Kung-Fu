I wrote `/home/dev/eb7374/app/src/walkthrough.ts`. It creates the camera with `createFreeCamera`, makes it `scene.camera` and attaches the library's standard `attachFreeControl`. `jumpTo` removes the controls, moves the camera to the new view and attaches fresh controls, so no leftover movement or old state carries over. `dispose` removes the controls for good; after that, `jumpTo` only moves the camera.

**What I'm not sure of:** the type declarations don't say whether the controls steer by the camera's public `target` or by its internal look angles (`_yaw`/`_pitch`). So `jumpTo` sets the angles too, copying the values the library gives a brand-new camera with the same eye and target. That relies on internal fields, so a library update could break it, and I couldn't check that the angles really line up. Nothing has been compiled or run, because the library isn't on this machine. The first thing to try in the browser is a jump, then W and a small mouse drag. One side effect: restarting the controls forgets a key held during the jump, though the key's auto-repeat should pick it up again.
