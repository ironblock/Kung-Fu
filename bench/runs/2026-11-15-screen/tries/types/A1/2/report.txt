I wrote `src/focus.ts` in the app folder. `focusOn` clears the camera's pending inertia offsets and then sets `camera.target` to `point`, writing x, y and z in place. Alpha, beta and radius are untouched, so the camera keeps its angles and distance and moves by the same offset as the target.

I'm not sure of three things:
- The declarations type `target` as a plain `Vec3`, but a comment says it is an `ObservableVec3`. I relied on in-place field writes to trigger the dirty tracking; if it is actually a plain object, the view matrix could go stale after the call.
- Clearing the inertia offsets stops any coast from a previous drag, which is a behaviour change you may not want.
- I had no way to run or type-check the code here, since the library isn't installed and I had no shell.
