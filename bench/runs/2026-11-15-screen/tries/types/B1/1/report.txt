The returned function detaches the controls and the limits. I could not compile or run it, since the library isn't on this machine.

Things I'm not sure of:

- **Limits after cleanup:** the declarations say the disposer removes the clamp hook, but not whether it also clears the stored radius bounds. I didn't clear them explicitly, so the camera may keep its radius bounds after detach.
- **Inertia writes:** I'm relying on the declarations' claim that inertia and pinch writes go through the radius setter. I couldn't check that in the library source.
- **Bad input:** the function throws a `RangeError` if the bounds are non-finite, negative, or inverted. You didn't ask for that check.
