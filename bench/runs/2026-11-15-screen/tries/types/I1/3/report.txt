The one thing I'm least sure of is (a): the same comment says the first frame discards leftover inertia, which is why I expect the coast to be cleared and the transition to finish. I couldn't check the implementation, so it's worth a quick test on a real flick. For (b) and (c), `goHome()` needs a catch around the `await`, or the rejection will surface as an unhandled promise rejection.
