I wrote `notes/pointer-lock.json` inside the app folder with `lockedNoButton` true, `lockedButtonHeld` true, `rightDrag` true, and `touchDrag` false. Only `lockedNoButton` is supported by the declarations. The `attachFreeControl` doc comment says "Mouse drag or pointer-lock: look around (yaw/pitch)", which covers locked motion with no button. The other three are my guesses, because the declarations don't mention which button drives a drag or whether touch is handled.

- **`lockedButtonHeld` (true):** Not stated. I assumed the lock path doesn't check buttons. Test it with a left-button drag while locked.
- **`rightDrag` (true):** The doc says "mouse drag" without naming a button. The right-button pan mapping in the file belongs to ArcRotate, not the free camera.
- **`touchDrag` (false):** The free-camera doc doesn't mention touch at all. The geospatial controls do document touch, so the absence is a weak signal.

Because the library isn't on this machine, these three need a quick check in a running build before you rely on them. If you decide to write your own mouse-look, that check will also tell you whether the built-in one is enough. I also created the `notes/` directory, since it didn't exist yet.
