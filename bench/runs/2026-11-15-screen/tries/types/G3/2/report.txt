I wrote `app/src/fly-to-place.ts`. `flyToPlace` wraps `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` and resolves `true` only if the camera's final yaw, pitch, radius and centre match the place within a tiny tolerance. Otherwise it resolves `false`, and it never rejects.

- **Limits:** the pose check assumes the library doesn't clamp the place. You said places are always within limits, so this should hold.
