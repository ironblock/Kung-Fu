I wrote `src/chase-camera.ts` in `/home/dev/d5710b/app`. It uses `createBankedFreeCamera` from the declarations, since plain `createFreeCamera` can never roll. Each `bank(up)` call projects `up` onto the plane perpendicular to the view direction and writes that into the camera's `upVector`. When `up` is along the line of sight, it leaves the roll unchanged.

I could not compile or run it, because the library is not on this machine. Things I'm not sure of:

- The "parallel" cutoff is 1e-6 on the perpendicular component. A car up that is within about that angle of the line of sight will keep the previous roll.
- `createChaseCamera` throws if `eye` and `target` are the same point. If the initial view is straight up or down, the starting roll uses +Z instead of +Y.
