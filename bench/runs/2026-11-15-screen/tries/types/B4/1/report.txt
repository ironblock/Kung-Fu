I wrote `app/src/globe-tilt.ts`. `tiltGlobeCamera` clears the camera's `limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale`, which otherwise forces pitch down toward `pitchMin` when zoomed out, then calls `setGeospatialOrientation(camera, { pitch })` so only the pitch changes. It throws a `RangeError` for non-finite or out-of-limits pitch values.

I haven't compiled or run it, because the library isn't available here. I'm not certain that `setGeospatialOrientation` is exported from the package root, that the default `pitchMax` is at least 1.2, or that the radius is left unchanged when the camera starts at its maximum zoom. Clearing `pitchDisabledRadiusScale` also changes that camera's limits for good, so pitch will no longer reduce automatically when zoomed out.
