I added `src/orbit-camera.ts`, which exports `createOrbitCamera(canvas, scene)`. It creates an ArcRotate camera at radius 10 looking at the origin, calls `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls()`, attaches the default controls with `keyboard: true`, and returns the camera.

Things I could not confirm:
- **Imports:** I assumed `attachControl`, `createArcRotateCamera`, `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls`, and the `ArcRotateCamera` and `SceneContext` types are all exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` root. The declarations only show their internal module paths, and the library source isn't here to check.
- **Target shape:** I passed the target as a plain `{ x, y, z }` object, assuming `Vec3` has that shape.
- **Starting angles:** alpha is -π/2 and beta is π/3. The declarations don't specify defaults, so I picked these.
- **Dispose:** `attachControl` returns a dispose function, which I discarded. Keep it if you need to detach the controls later.
