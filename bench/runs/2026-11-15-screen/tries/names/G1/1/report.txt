I wrote `/home/dev/1483ff/app/src/mode-limits.ts`. `useModeLimits` sets `camera.limits` to the mode's object and wraps `camera.radius`, `camera.yaw` and `camera._setOrientation` so writes are clamped to that mode's `radiusMin`/`radiusMax` and `yawMin`/`yawMax`. Calling it again with another mode's limits switches the ranges, and it never modifies the limits objects.
