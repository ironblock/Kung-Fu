I wrote `notes/over-the-top.json` with `eyeX` -4.794, `eyeY` 8.776, `upright` true, and `sameAsOtherSide` true. I only created that file, and nothing else in the app changed.

I'm not certain of these values. `camera-api.d.ts` only declares types and signatures, with no position formula, view-matrix code, or up-vector handling. I derived the answers from the standard Babylon ArcRotate math, which places the eye at `target + radius·(cos α·sin β, cos β, sin α·sin β)` and uses a fixed world up of +Y. Under that math, `beta = -0.5` at `alpha = 0` lands on the same point as `alpha = π, beta = 0.5`, so the two views match. Before relying on this for your over-the-top behavior, confirm the formula and up handling in Babylon Lite's source, or check the result in a quick test once the library is available.
