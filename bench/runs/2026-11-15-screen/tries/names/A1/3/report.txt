I wrote `/home/dev/577d0b/app/src/focus.ts`. `focusOn` sets `camera.target` to a fresh copy of `point`; since the camera's position is derived from the target plus its angles and radius, it keeps those and moves by the same offset. It also zeroes `inertialPanningX/Y` so leftover pan momentum can't pull the target off the point.
