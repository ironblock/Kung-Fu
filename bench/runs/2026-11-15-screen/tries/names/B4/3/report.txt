I wrote `app/src/globe-tilt.ts`. `tiltGlobeCamera` widens the camera's pitch limits so the requested tilt isn't clamped at the current zoom or later, then re-applies the orientation with the existing yaw, radius and centre, and throws if the pitch didn't take. Nothing outside `app/src/globe-tilt.ts` was changed.
