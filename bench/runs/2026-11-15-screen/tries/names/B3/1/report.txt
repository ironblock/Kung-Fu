I wrote `src/fly-controls.ts`, which wraps the library's `attachConfigurableFreeControl`. It sets Space and PageDown for up and down, Shift for a 3× speed boost, and returns the library's own disposer. Shift is kept out of the up and down key lists so it can't move the camera vertically. I haven't compiled or run it because the library isn't on this machine.

Things I couldn't verify:
- **Key names:** I listed both `code` and `key` forms (e.g. `"ShiftLeft"` and `"Shift"`, `"Space"` and `" "`) in case the library matches on either. Check which one it uses.
- **WASD and arrow keys:** I didn't pass move keys, so these depend on the library's defaults and are unconfirmed. The declarations don't show what the defaults are.
- **Shift behaviour and cleanup:** I'm relying on the library not binding Shift to vertical movement by default, and on its disposer removing all listeners and per-frame hooks. Neither is shown in the declarations.
- **Import:** I assumed `attachConfigurableFreeControl` is exported from the package root. The declarations don't show the index, so that's unconfirmed.
