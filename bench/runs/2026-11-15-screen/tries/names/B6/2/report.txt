I wrote `src/chase-camera.ts` in the app. It builds on `createBankedFreeCamera` from your declarations, and `bank(up)` projects the car's up onto the plane perpendicular to the line of sight and writes that into the camera's `upVector`. When `up` is along the line of sight, it returns without changing anything, so the previous roll is kept. I have not compiled or run it, since the library isn't on this machine.
