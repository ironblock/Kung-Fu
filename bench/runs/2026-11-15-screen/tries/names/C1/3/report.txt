I wrote `app/src/steady-zoom.ts`, but I have not run or type-checked it, because the library isn't on this machine.

It attaches the library's `attachControl` for orbit, pan and pinch. It takes over wheel events on the canvas and moves the radius by a fixed step per notch. The step is measured once, when `attachSteadyZoom` is called, by sending a synthetic wheel event through the library's own handler and then restoring the camera. The returned function removes the window listener and calls the library's dispose.

Things I'm not sure of:
- **The step measurement is a guess at the library's internals.** The declarations don't say how a notch maps to radius. I assume the zoom shows up in `radius` or `inertialRadiusOffset`, in radius units. If it doesn't, the function throws instead of guessing.
- **`attachControl` may not be exported from `"@babylonjs/lite"`.** The declarations only show it in the controls module, so the import path may need changing.
- **Each wheel event counts as one notch.** Trackpads send many small events, so they will zoom in many small steps.
- **Radius limits are applied directly.** Inertia is bypassed for wheel zoom.
