That would make it the alpha = π view rolled 180°. I assumed Lite does the same, but I couldn't check. Lite's camera type has no `upVector` or `allowUpsideDown` setting, so it may be a simplified version that always keeps the world's up direction pointing up. In that case both values would be `true`, and nothing in your code would need flipping.

To check it once in a browser, set `beta = -0.5`, call `getViewMatrix(camera)`, and look at how the world up direction (0, 1, 0) comes out in camera space. If it points down, the guess is right.
