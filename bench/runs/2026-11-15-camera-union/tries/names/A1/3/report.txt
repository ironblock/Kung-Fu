I wrote `/home/dev/dbb435/app/src/focus.ts`. `focusOn` sets `camera.target` to a copy of `point` and clears the pending pan inertia (`inertialPanningX/Y`), since that would otherwise move the target off the point on the next update. I left `alpha`, `beta` and `radius` alone, so the camera moves by the same offset as the centre and keeps orbiting the point.
