I wrote `src/focus.ts`, which sets `camera.target` to a copy of `point` and zeroes the pending pan inertia so it can't drag the new centre away on the next frame. Alpha, beta and radius are left alone, so the camera keeps its angles and distance and orbits `point` from then on. I haven't compiled or run it, since the library isn't on this machine. I'm not sure the same-frame requirement holds: the declarations don't say whether the view or position caches update when `target` is assigned directly, and no method in them forces a refresh. I also haven't confirmed that `Vec3` is just `{x, y, z}`, which the object literal assumes.
