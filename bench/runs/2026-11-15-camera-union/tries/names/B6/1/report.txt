I wrote `src/chase-camera.ts` in `/home/dev/310947/app`. It builds on `createBankedFreeCamera` from your type declarations, and each `bank(up)` call projects `up` onto the plane perpendicular to the fixed line of sight and sets the camera's up vector to that. If `up` is parallel to the line of sight, it keeps the previous roll. I haven't compiled or run it, since the library isn't available here.
