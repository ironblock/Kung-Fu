I wrote `app/src/big-world-fly.ts`. It sets `camera.speed` to 20× its original value, attaches the library's `attachFreeControl` on the canvas, and the returned function detaches the controls and restores the original speed. Mouse look is untouched because it depends on `angularSensitivity`, not `speed`, and the wrapper adds no per-frame work.

I could not verify any of this against the library, so please check these before relying on it:
- **Speed scaling:** I assumed the library's keyboard movement, including inertia, is linear in `camera.speed` and reads it during the session. If it isn't, the 20× factor will be wrong.
- **Root export:** I assumed `attachFreeControl` is exported from `"@babylonjs/lite"`. The declarations don't show the package index.
- **Speed changes while attached:** Calling the returned function restores the speed captured at attach time, so any speed change made while attached will be overwritten.
- **Not compiled or run:** I did not type-check or test the file.
