I wrote `src/globe-tilt.ts`. It widens `camera.limits` so the requested pitch isn't clamped at the current zoom, disables the radius-dependent pitch cap (`pitchDisabledRadiusScale = null`), then calls `setGeospatialOrientation` with the existing yaw, radius and centre. It throws if the resulting pitch, radius or centre differs from what was requested.

I could not compile or run it, because the library isn't on this machine and the project has no `package.json` dependencies or tests for it. I'm not sure of these points:

- **Pitch convention:** the declarations don't say whether the library's `pitch` is 0 when looking straight down. I assumed it matches your convention.
- **Root exports:** I import `getEffectivePitchMax` and `setGeospatialOrientation` from `"@babylonjs/lite"`, but the declarations only show per-file modules. If they aren't re-exported from the root, the import will fail.
- **Disabling the radius cap:** I don't know what `pitchDisabledRadiusScale` controls. Setting it to `null` is my guess at removing the zoom-dependent limit, and it may change other behaviour.
