I wrote `notes/tighter-zoom.json` inside the app with `{ "asIs": 4000, "radiusSelf": 4000, "centerSelf": 4000, "emptyOrientation": 2000 }`, but these are inferences, not verified results. The declarations in `camera-api.d.ts` give the types and function names only. They don't show whether assigning `radius` or `center` clamps against `limits`, or what `setGeospatialOrientation` does internally. The one clamping helper they expose, `clampZoomDistance`, is used for zoom input, so I expect a bare `radius` write to leave the value at 4000. I'm less sure about `setGeospatialOrientation`: I guessed it routes through the internal `_setOrientation` and clamps to the new limit, which would give 2000. Before you rely on it, run the four cases against the real library, or check the implementation of `setGeospatialOrientation` and the `radius` setter. If you need the camera pulled in right after the limit change, `setGeospatialOrientation(camera, {})` is the candidate to verify first.
