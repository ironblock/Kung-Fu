I wrote `notes/over-the-top.json` in the app with `eyeX` ≈ -4.794, `eyeY` ≈ 8.776, `upright` true, and `sameAsOtherSide` true. The `.d.ts` file only declares the camera's shape, not its math, so these values come from the standard orbit formula (position = target + r·(cos α·sin β, cos β, sin α·sin β)), not from the library's code.

The main thing I'm not sure of is whether Babylon Lite clamps `beta` internally when no limits are set. If it does, the eye position and the "same as other side" result would differ. I also couldn't confirm that the view is built from a world-up lookAt. Once the library is available, a quick check of `camera.beta` and the position after setting `-0.5` would settle it.
