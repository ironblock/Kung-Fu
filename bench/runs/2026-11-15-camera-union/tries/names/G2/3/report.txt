I wrote `src/center-on.ts`, but I could not confirm it matches the library, so please check it before relying on it.

The function converts latitude and longitude to a point on a sphere of `camera.limits.planetRadius` and sets the camera centre through `setGeospatialOrientation(camera, { center })`, which leaves yaw, pitch and radius alone. I assumed Y-up, with the pole at +Y, east at +X and lat 0 / lon 0 at (0, 0, R). The declarations don't give the frame, the yaw convention, the default centre, or whether `setGeospatialOrientation` is exported from the package root, and the library isn't here to check. If any of those differ, the `surfacePoint` helper is the one place to change, but the heading-90° and default-centre requirements are unverified.
