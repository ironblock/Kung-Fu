Things I'm not sure of:
- **Imports:** I assumed `getViewMatrix`, `getViewProjectionMatrix` and `projectWorldToScreen` are exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` root. Your declarations show them only in internal modules, so check that these imports resolve.
- **Floating origin:** I did not pass `worldOrigin`. If the camera uses a floating origin, the shift will be wrong unless the app supplies the same origin the renderer uses.
- **Matrix copy:** I used `.slice()` to copy the matrices and cast the result back to the library's matrix type. This assumes the type supports `slice()`.
- **Canvas resize:** a resize between a mark and a shift will count as movement, because the marked matrices keep the old aspect ratio.
