Things I'm not sure of:

- **Limits disposer:** I assumed the disposer returned by `setCameraLimits` removes its per-frame clamp, which is what the cleanup relies on.
- **Wheel offset at the limit:** the notes don't say whether the wheel's inertial offset keeps building up while the radius is clamped. If it does, a zoom reversal could feel delayed.
