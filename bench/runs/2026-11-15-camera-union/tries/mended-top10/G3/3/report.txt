I wrote `app/src/fly-to-place.ts`, which calls the library's `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` and resolves `true` only if the camera's final yaw, pitch, radius and center match the place within small tolerances. Nothing was compiled or run, since the library isn't available here.

Things I'm not sure of:
- **Cancellation behaviour:** the notes don't say whether a cancelled fly rejects or resolves, or whether its promise settles at all. The code handles rejection as `false` and checks the pose after resolution, so a superseded flight should still give `false`. If the promise never settles on cancel, `flyToPlace` will hang.
- **Root import:** I assumed `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` is exported from `"@babylonjs/lite"`. The declarations are per-module, so this needs checking.
- **Pitch clamping:** if the library writes radius after pitch during the flight, the default pitch clamp at large radius could leave the camera short of a valid place, which would return `false` wrongly.
- **Tolerances:** the 1e-4 angle and relative tolerances are my guesses.
