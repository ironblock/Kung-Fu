I wrote `src/center-on.ts` in `/home/dev/bd2295/app`. It converts latitude and longitude to a point on the planet's sphere and moves the camera there with `setGeospatialOrientation`, passing back the current yaw, pitch and radius so zoom, heading and tilt are unchanged.

I'm not sure of the following, and I could not test any of it because the library isn't on this machine:
- **Planet axes:** The type declarations and notes don't say which world axis is the pole or where longitude 0 lies. I assumed north is +Y and lat 0, lon 0 is +Z. If the library differs, only `surfacePoint` needs changing.
- **North and east:** I didn't confirm that the library's yaw of 90° points toward positive longitude, so the "camera's own north and east" requirement is unverified.
- **Import:** I assumed `setGeospatialOrientation` and `GeospatialCamera` are exported from `"@babylonjs/lite"`. I only saw the internal module declarations, not the package's index.
