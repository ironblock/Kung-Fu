I wrote `notes/preview-controls.json` with `{ "drag": false, "wheel": false, "pinch": true }`. The reasoning comes from note 1 in `camera-notes.md`: drag and wheel only add to inertial offsets that are applied in a `scene._beforeRender` callback, which never runs without a scene, so they don't move the camera. A two-finger pinch writes `radius` directly, so it does change the camera.

I could not check this against the library itself, since it isn't on the machine. The answer rests entirely on the notes file, and the `.d.ts` file doesn't confirm the pinch behaviour or the no-scene behaviour. I also haven't verified that nothing else applies the inertial offsets when there is no scene. QA should confirm on the laptop and tablet that the drag and wheel really do nothing on the static page.
