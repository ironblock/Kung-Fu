I wrote `src/big-world-fly.ts`. It attaches the library's `attachFreeControl` and multiplies `camera.speed` by 20 before attaching, so the keyboard movement scales while mouse look (driven by `angularSensitivity` and inertia) is untouched. The returned function detaches the controls and restores the original speed; it registers no per-frame work of its own.

I could not verify it. The library isn't on the machine, and the notes don't say how the free-camera controls read `speed`. I'm assuming:
- All movement keys, including rise and sink, scale linearly with `camera.speed`.
- `attachFreeControl` is exported from the `"@babylonjs/lite"` root.

If vertical movement uses a separate speed, or the controls cache speed differently, the change will not give 20× for every key.
