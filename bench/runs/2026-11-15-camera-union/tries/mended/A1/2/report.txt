I wrote `/home/dev/7aef90/app/src/focus.ts`. `focusOn` writes the new point into `camera.target` in place, so the eye moves by the same offset while alpha, beta and radius stay as they are. It then nudges one pose value away and back, because the notes say a target change only takes effect on the next write that actually changes alpha, beta or radius. Nothing was run, since the library isn't on this machine.

I'm not sure of three things:
- The nudge depends on the library treating a change of about 1e-4 (scaled to the value) as a real change. If it has a larger equality threshold, the label code could still read the old view in the same frame.
- The claim that the eye is derived from target plus angles and radius is the standard arc-rotate model. The declarations don't spell that out, so I haven't confirmed it.
