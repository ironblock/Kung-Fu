I added `src/focus.ts` with `focusOn`. It writes the point into `camera.target` in place and leaves alpha, beta and radius alone, so the camera moves by the same offset and looks straight at the point. It also zeroes `inertialPanningX` and `inertialPanningY`, so a pending pan can't drift the camera off the point on the next frame; that's my addition, not something you asked for.

I couldn't compile or run it because the library isn't on this machine. The notes say an in-place target write is the supported way to move the target, but they don't say outright that `worldMatrix` and the view update in the same frame. That is the one thing to check in your label code.
