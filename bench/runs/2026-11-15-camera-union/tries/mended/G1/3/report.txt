I wrote `src/mode-limits.ts` in `/home/dev/e43f9e/app`. `useModeLimits` wraps the camera's `yaw` and `radius` setters and `_setOrientation` so each write is clamped against `camera.limits` at that moment. It then sets `camera.limits` to the mode's object and clamps the current pose once, since the notes say changing limits doesn't re-clamp on its own. The mode's limits object is only read, never changed.
