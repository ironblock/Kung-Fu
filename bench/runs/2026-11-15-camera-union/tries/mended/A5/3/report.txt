I wrote `notes/inset-slide.json` in the app with `{ "x": 800, "y": 300, "width": 200, "height": 150, "aspect": 1.33 }`. I couldn't run the library, so these are hand-derived from the notes, not measured.

- **Viewport (fairly solid):** Note 15 says the viewport's y is counted from the bottom but returned from the top, and edges are clamped to the canvas. The x range 0.8–1.1 clamps to 800–1000 px, which gives x 800 and width 200. The y range 0.1–0.4 from the bottom gives 300–150 px from the top, so y 300 and height 150.
- **Aspect (uncertain):** The `.d.ts` only gives the signature, and the notes don't describe how `getEffectiveAspectRatio` computes its value. I took 1.33 (200 ÷ 150), which uses the clamped rectangle, so it matches the frame. If the function uses the unclamped viewport, it would be 2.00 instead. Please check that against the library source or a real call before you rely on the aspect.
