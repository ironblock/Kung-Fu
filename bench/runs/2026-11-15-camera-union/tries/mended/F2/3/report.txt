I wrote `/home/dev/eceb51/app/src/walkthrough.ts`. `createWalkthrough` makes a free camera at `eye` looking at `target`, sets it as `scene.camera`, and attaches the library's free-camera controls. `jumpTo` copies the angles that `createFreeCamera` computes for the new view onto the camera (the controls steer by those angles), then moves the position and target. `dispose` only calls the controls' own disposer.

I could not compile or run this, since the library isn't here, so it is untested. I'm not sure of these points:
- I assumed `createFreeCamera`, `attachFreeControl` and `scene.camera` (writable) are all available from `@babylonjs/lite`'s root. The declarations only show the module files, so the root exports may differ.
- I used a throwaway `createFreeCamera` to get the angles, because I couldn't confirm the sign convention for `_pitch` from the notes. This avoids the guess, but it depends on `createFreeCamera` having no side effects beyond its return value.
- Any drag or key momentum still coasting at the moment of `jumpTo` is not cleared, because the public types don't expose the internal velocity fields.
- I assumed writing `position` and `target` in place updates the camera's world matrix.
