I wrote `/home/dev/6be6f0/app/src/center-on.ts`. It sets `camera.center` to the surface point at the given latitude and longitude on the camera's planet radius, and leaves radius, yaw and pitch alone. I could not compile it or run it: I only had file read and write access, and the library isn't on this machine.

I'm not sure of the east direction. The notes say north is +Z and the centre at longitude 0 is on +X, but nothing I have says whether longitude increases toward +Y or -Y. I chose -Y, reasoning that Babylon renders left-handed, so east is on the right for an outside viewer with north up. If a camera with heading 90° doesn't face increasing longitude, flip `EAST_Y_SIGN` at the top of the file. Checking it needs the library or a run.
