I added `src/center-on.ts` in the app, exporting `centerOnLatLon`. It places the camera's centre on the sphere at the given latitude and longitude, using `limits.planetRadius`, and leaves radius, yaw and pitch alone. I could not run it or type-check it, since the library isn't on this machine. The sign of the longitude axis is the part I'm least sure of: I took it from note 16 (east is -Y at lon 0), and it is what makes heading 90° look toward positive longitude.
