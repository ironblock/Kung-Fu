I wrote `src/chase-camera.ts` in `/home/dev/2d7b14/app`. It uses `createBankedFreeCamera` from the type declarations, starts with world +Y made perpendicular to the line of sight, and on each `bank(up)` call writes the projected, normalised `up` into `upVector`. When `up` is along the line of sight (projection under 1e-6), it leaves `upVector` alone, so the previous roll holds. I could not run or type-check it because the library isn't on this machine.
