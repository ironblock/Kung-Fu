I created `src/fly-controls.ts`. It wraps the library's `attachConfigurableFreeControl` with Space for up, PageDown for down, Shift left or right as a 3× fast key, and returns that function's disposer. I had no way to compile or run it, so it is untested.

Things I'm not sure of:
- **Import path:** I assumed `attachConfigurableFreeControl` is exported from the package root. The `.d.ts` file only shows internal module paths.
- **`downKeys` replacing the defaults:** the notes say `upKeys` replaces the default Space/PageUp rather than adding to them. I assumed `downKeys` works the same way, which is what keeps Shift from moving the camera down. If it adds to the defaults, Shift will still sink the camera.
- **Mouse and WASD in the configurable variant:** the notes describe WASD, arrows and drag-to-look for `attachFreeControl`, not for `attachConfigurableFreeControl`. I assumed they match.
- **Disposer:** I trusted the library to remove its canvas listeners and per-frame work. The notes don't confirm that for this variant.
