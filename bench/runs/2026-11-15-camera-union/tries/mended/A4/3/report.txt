I wrote `notes/over-the-top.json` in the app with `eyeX` -4.794255, `eyeY` 8.775826, `upright` true, and `sameAsOtherSide` true. The library isn't on this machine, so I worked these out by hand from the standard arc-rotate convention rather than running anything. The position formula, the (0,1,0) up vector, and the claim that a direct `beta` write updates the position straight away are assumptions. The declarations and notes don't state them, though note 7 suggests the write does take effect at once. The camera ends at alpha 0, beta -0.5, which is the same point as alpha π, beta 0.5. The up direction stays upright because the camera is not directly overhead. The only difference from the fresh camera is a float rounding error of about 6e-17 in z, from `sin(Math.PI)`.
