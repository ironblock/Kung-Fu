I wrote `/home/dev/60e447/app/notes/preview-controls.json` with `{ "drag": false, "wheel": false, "pinch": true }`. Per your notes (items 1 and 27), a mouse drag or wheel only adds to inertial offsets that are applied on the scene's render hook, which never runs on this page, so those inputs leave `alpha`, `beta` and `radius` unchanged; a two-finger pinch sets `radius` directly. I haven't verified this against the library source, since it isn't on the machine. Also, on a laptop trackpad, a pinch often arrives as a wheel event with `ctrlKey` set, and per the notes that would not change the camera; QA should expect pinch to work on touch screens and not on the trackpad gesture.
