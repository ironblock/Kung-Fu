I wrote `notes/preview-controls.json` with `{ "drag": false, "wheel": false, "pinch": true }`. Per `camera-notes.md`, drag and wheel only feed the inertia offsets, which need the scene's `_beforeRender` to apply, while a pinch writes `radius` directly and needs no scene.

I could not confirm any of this against the library itself, since it isn't on this machine. The answers rest entirely on your notes (items 1, 13, 39 and 41), and `camera-api.d.ts` doesn't declare the pinch behaviour. QA should also know that a laptop trackpad's pinch gesture usually arrives as a wheel event with `ctrlKey`, not as a two-finger touch pinch, so the laptop result for pinch may differ from the tablet result.
