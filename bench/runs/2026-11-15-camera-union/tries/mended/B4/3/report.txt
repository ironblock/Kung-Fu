I wrote `app/src/globe-tilt.ts`. It sets `limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale` to `null`, raises `limits.pitchMax` to at least the requested pitch, then calls `setGeospatialOrientation` with the current radius, so yaw, zoom and centre stay put. It throws if the pitch doesn't take. I haven't run it, since the library isn't on this machine.

I'm not sure of three things:
- The notes don't say what `pitchDisabledRadiusScale` means, so setting it to `null` as "no radius-based pitch restriction" is an inference.
- I assumed the package root exports `setGeospatialOrientation`. The declarations show it in the geospatial camera module, but not that it is re-exported.
- The tilt holding at other zoom levels rests on the same inference. The function only checks the pitch it just set, not the pitch after later zoom changes.
