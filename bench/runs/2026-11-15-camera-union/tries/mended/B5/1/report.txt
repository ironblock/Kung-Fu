I wrote `src/label-position.ts`. It projects the point with the library's `getViewMatrix`, `getViewProjectionMatrix` and `projectWorldToScreen`. It sets `visible` from `behindCamera`, `clipW > 0`, and whether the CSS position falls inside the canvas, ignoring the far-plane `clipped` flag so distant points still count as visible.

I could not compile or run this because the library isn't on the machine. Things I'm not sure of:
- I assumed `getViewMatrix`, `getViewProjectionMatrix`, `getEffectiveAspectRatio` and `projectWorldToScreen` are exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` root. The `.d.ts` only shows their module paths.
- I assumed `cssWidth`/`cssHeight` make `cssX`/`cssY` come out in CSS pixels, and that `behindCamera` is the library's own front/back test. The declarations don't say either.
- I ignore `camera.viewport` on the basis of your statement that the camera renders to the whole canvas.
- A canvas with zero CSS or backing size returns `{ x: 0, y: 0, visible: false }`.
- I don't handle floating origin or the `worldOrigin` option.
