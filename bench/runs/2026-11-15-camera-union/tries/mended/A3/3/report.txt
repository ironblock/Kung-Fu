I wrote `app/src/screen-shift.ts`. `mark()` and the constructor copy the camera's view and view-projection matrices, and `shift()` projects the point through the marked and current matrices, then returns the CSS-pixel difference. Copying matters because the library overwrites its cached matrices on the next change (note 11). The projection uses the same aspect the renderer uses (note 16), so a field-of-view change shows up in the matrices.
