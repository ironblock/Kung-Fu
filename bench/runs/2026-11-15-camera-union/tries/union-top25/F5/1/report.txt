I wrote `/home/dev/c639b7/app/notes/pointer-lock.json` with `lockedNoButton: false`, `lockedButtonHeld: false`, `rightDrag: false`, and `touchDrag: true`. The declarations and notes don't settle most of these. The only direct statement is in note 25: a left drag and a middle drag turn the free camera. Nothing covers pointer lock, `movementX`/`movementY`, right-button drags, or touch for `attachFreeControl`, so those four values are my inferences, not confirmed behaviour. Only `lockedNoButton` (no drag without a held button) and `rightDrag` (right button not listed) are fairly well supported. `lockedButtonHeld` is the one that matters most for your decision. If the library measures drags from `clientX`/`clientY`, it won't turn under lock, but the notes don't say which it uses. Check the implementation of `attachFreeControl` before relying on any of these.
