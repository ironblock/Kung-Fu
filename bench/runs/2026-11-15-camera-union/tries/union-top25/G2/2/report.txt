I wrote `/home/dev/a77b28/app/src/center-on.ts`. It converts latitude and longitude to a point on the planet sphere, using `camera.limits.planetRadius`, and assigns that as the new `camera.center` without touching yaw, pitch or radius.

I'm not sure of the axis convention. The declarations and notes don't say which world axis is north, or where lat 0, lon 0 sits, and the library isn't on this machine. I assumed north is +Y, lat 0 lon 0 is +Z, and east is +X. If the library differs, latitude and longitude will be wrong, so check it against the library before relying on this.
