I wrote `src/label-position.ts` in `/home/dev/5e9665/app`. It projects the point with the library's `projectWorldToScreen` and sets `visible` to "not behind the camera and not offscreen", ignoring the `clipped` flag so points past the far plane still count. I have not compiled or run it, since the library isn't on this machine.

Things I'm not sure of:
- I assumed `@babylonjs/lite` exports these functions from its root. The declarations only show per-module files, so the import path may need changing.
- `offscreen` is trusted to be false for points beyond the far plane and to mean "outside the picture". Note 24 supports the first part, but I haven't seen the code.
- I don't know how points between the eye and the near plane are classed. I haven't handled them specially.
- I passed no `worldOrigin`, which may matter if the camera uses a floating origin.
- `cssWidth` and `cssHeight` come from `canvas.clientWidth` and `clientHeight`, which exclude borders.
