I wrote `notes/inset-slide.json` with `x` 800, `y` 300, `width` 200, `height` 150, but `aspect` is `null` because I couldn't determine it.

The `x`, `y`, `width` and `height` values are my calculation from note 13 in `camera-notes.md`, not output from the library. The inset's right edge would be at 1100 px, so it is clamped to the 1000 px canvas edge, which leaves a width of 200. The bottom-origin `y` of 0.1 becomes a top-origin `y` of 300.

I didn't compute `aspect` because neither the declarations nor the notes say how `getEffectiveAspectRatio` handles viewports that extend past the canvas. Whether it uses the clamped 200 × 150 (about 1.33) or the unclamped 300 × 150 (2.0) matters for your alignment question. Check the library source for that function, or run it on this camera, before relying on the inset's aspect.
