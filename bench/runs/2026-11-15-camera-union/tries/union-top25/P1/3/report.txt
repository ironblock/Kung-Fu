I wrote `/home/dev/f2e94a/app/notes/preview-controls.json` with `{ "drag": false, "wheel": false, "pinch": true }`. Mouse drag and the wheel only add to inertial offsets that are applied on the scene's render hook, and the preview has no scene, so they don't change alpha, beta or radius. A two-finger pinch writes radius directly, so it does change the camera.

I couldn't check this against the library source; the answer rests on `camera-notes.md` (notes 1, 18 and 22), and the `.d.ts` only confirms the signatures. The pinch result is the one I'd test first on the tablet.
