I wrote `/home/dev/5f4650/app/notes/go-home.json` with `a: "resolves"`, `b: "rejects"`, and `c: "rejects"`. Those answers come from note 6 in `camera-notes.md`, which says `interpolateArcRotateCamera` rejects when alpha, beta, radius or target change between its frames by anything other than itself, including a second call on the camera. A change made before its first frame becomes the start, and that first frame zeroes inertia. The two-call case in (c) is that rule applied to the first call. I did not verify this against the library source, since it isn't on this machine.

What I'm not sure of:
- The notes are secondhand. I have no source or runtime to check them, and I didn't check the exact error type on rejection.
- For (a), I'm relying on the coasting being absorbed before the first frame. That depends on the ordering of the attachControl hook and the interpolation frame within a single frame, which the notes don't spell out. Either order should still resolve, but I haven't confirmed it.
- For (b), I'm assuming that with the scene attached, drag input only feeds the inertial offsets and the per-frame hook applies them to alpha/radius. That is what note 1 implies.

Since (b) and (c) reject, `goHome()` will need a try/catch around the `await` if those cases can occur. The notes also suggest that passing an `AbortSignal` (the optional `signal` argument) would let you cancel the first call deliberately; I haven't looked into that.
