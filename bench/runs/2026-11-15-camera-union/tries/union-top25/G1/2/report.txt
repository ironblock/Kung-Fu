I wrote `/home/dev/41c54a/app/src/mode-limits.ts`. `useModeLimits` sets `camera.limits` to the given mode's object, pulls the current radius and yaw into that mode's range, and then intercepts later writes to `radius` and `yaw` on that camera so they are clamped to the active mode's `radiusMin`/`radiusMax` and `yawMin`/`yawMax`. Calling it again with another mode's limits swaps the active ranges, and the limits objects are never modified.
