I wrote `notes/over-the-top.json` in the app with `eyeX` -4.794, `eyeY` 8.776, `upright` true, and `sameAsOtherSide` true. I could not run Babylon Lite, so these values come from the standard arc-rotate formula and the camera notes, not from the library's code.

Not sure about:
- **The position formula.** The type declarations don't include it. I assumed the usual `target + radius·(cos α·sin β, cos β, sin α·sin β)`.
- **The view.** I assumed it is built with a look-at toward the target using world up (0,1,0). If so, "upright" and "same view" depend only on position.
- **Lazy updates.** The world matrix might only refresh on a render or a read, so `eyeX`/`eyeY` could be stale until a frame runs. The notes don't say.
- **Exactness.** The other-side camera's z comes out about 6e-16 off from the z of the over-the-top camera, so "exactly the same" holds only up to floating-point error.
