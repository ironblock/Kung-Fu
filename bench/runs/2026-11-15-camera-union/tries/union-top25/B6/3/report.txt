I wrote `/home/dev/dd9d5f/app/src/chase-camera.ts`. It creates a `BankedFreeCamera` aimed from `eye` at `target`, and each `bank(up)` call projects `up` onto the plane perpendicular to the line of sight and writes the result to `camera.upVector`. When `up` is parallel to the line of sight, it writes nothing, so the previous roll stays.

- **`upVector` behaviour:** I assumed the camera's view uses `upVector` and that in-place writes to its x/y/z are seen. The `ObservableVec3` type isn't in the declarations, so I could not confirm a `set()` method or how writes are tracked.
