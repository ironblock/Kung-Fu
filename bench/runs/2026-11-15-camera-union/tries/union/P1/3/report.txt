I wrote `/home/dev/dd2c52/app/notes/preview-controls.json` with `{ "drag": false, "wheel": false, "pinch": true }`. Mouse drag and the wheel only add to inertial offsets, which are applied from `scene._beforeRender`, and the preview page has no scene or render loop, so they leave `alpha`, `beta` and `radius` unchanged. A two-finger pinch writes `radius` directly, so it does change the camera.

I'm not certain of this because I only had the type declarations and the notes, not the library source or a way to run it. The answer relies on the notes' claims about 1.39.0 (notes 1 and 22 mainly). One thing QA should check on a laptop: a trackpad pinch often arrives as wheel events with `ctrlKey`, and the notes don't say how those are handled, so that gesture may not zoom even though a touch pinch does.
