I wrote `/home/dev/ed51c5/app/src/turntable.ts`. `spinAround` splits the spin into segments of at most π/2 and runs them one after another through the library's `interpolateArcRotateCamera`. I did this because the notes say alpha takes the shortest arc modulo 2π (note 56), so a single goal would either do nothing or turn the wrong way. I have not run it, since the library isn't on this machine.

Things I'm not sure of:
- **Pauses at segment joins:** the library's easing decays to rest within each segment, so the camera may visibly settle at every join. That may break your "glide" and "no jump" requirements. A single call can't avoid this, and I can't reshape the easing without the library's scene API, which I don't have.
- **Root export:** I assumed `interpolateArcRotateCamera` is exported from `"@babylonjs/lite"`. I only saw its declaration file, not the package entry point.
- **Alpha limits:** if the camera has alpha limits, the goals will be clamped and the spin won't reach `start + 2π·turns`.
- **Negative turns:** these throw a `RangeError`.
