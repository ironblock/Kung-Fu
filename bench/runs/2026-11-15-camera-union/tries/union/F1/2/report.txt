I wrote `/home/dev/3e002f/app/src/big-world-fly.ts`. It calls the library's `attachFreeControl` and then, each frame, scales the camera's translation by 20 by adding 19 times the frame's displacement to both position and target. It never touches `speed`, sensitivity, or yaw/pitch, so mouse look stays the library's own. The returned function calls the library's detach, removes the canvas listeners and the per-frame hook.

I could not compile or run it, because the library isn't on this machine and the declarations don't include `SceneContext`. Three things I could not confirm:
- **The per-frame hook:** I assumed `scene._beforeRender` is an array of `(deltaMs) => void` functions that I can push to and splice from. If it is a Set or has another shape, registration will fail, so check this first.
- **The import:** I assumed `attachFreeControl` is exported from the package root.
- **External position writes:** the scaling can't tell library movement from your own `camera.position` writes. Writes made while a movement key is held, or while the library's motion is still decaying after release, will also be scaled 20 times.
