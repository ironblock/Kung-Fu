I wrote `notes/over-the-top.json` in the app with eye position (-4.794, 8.776), upright true, and same-as-other-side true. The camera stays at beta = -0.5 because it has no beta limits and nothing clamps direct writes (note 8). The eye lands on the far side at the same height as the `(PI, 0.5)` camera. I could not confirm this from the provided files, since neither the type declarations nor the notes give the arc-rotate position or up-vector formula. The values come from the standard Babylon arc-rotate formula, and the library isn't on this machine to check.

Two things I'm unsure of:
- **`sameAsOtherSide`** is true in what the screen shows, but not bit-for-bit. Under that formula, the z component differs by about 6e-16 (`sin(PI)` isn't exactly 0). If you need a strict "exactly", set it to false.
- **`upright`** assumes the view uses a fixed world-up vector, as Babylon's arc-rotate does. If Babylon Lite flips its up vector past the pole, this would be wrong.
