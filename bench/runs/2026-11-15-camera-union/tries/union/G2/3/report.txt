I wrote `src/center-on.ts` (`/home/dev/3f6f04/app/src/center-on.ts`). It sets `camera.center` to the surface point at the given latitude and longitude on a sphere of `camera.limits.planetRadius`, and leaves yaw, pitch and radius alone. I could not run it, because the library isn't on this machine.

I was not sure of the following:
- **East direction:** I took the axes from note 54 of `camera-notes.md`: at lat 0, lon 0 the point is (R, 0, 0), north is +Z, and east is -Y. Note 77 says a right-handed build flips the sign of yaw, so if your build is right-handed, longitude may come out mirrored. A quick check is to centre on lat 0, lon 0, set yaw to π/2, and confirm the view heads toward positive longitude.
- **Vec3 shape:** I assumed `Vec3` is a plain `{ x, y, z }` object, so the literal assignment type-checks.
