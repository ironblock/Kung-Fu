I wrote `notes/same-frame.json` with `rejects: false`, `finalRadius: 7`, and `finalTargetX: 5`. The `camera.radius = 7` line is safe because a change before the first frame becomes the start (camera-notes item 6). The `spot.x = 5` line does not cause a rejection, but it changes where the flight ends, so it should move if you want the first transition to finish at x = 1.

I was not fully sure of two things, both from the notes rather than the library:
- The target goal is inferred to be read on every frame (from items 46 and 56), which is why `finalTargetX` is 5 and not 1. The notes don't state this directly.
- Item 58 lists alpha, beta and radius as snapping exactly to the goal at the end, but doesn't mention target. If target doesn't snap, the settled X would be close to 5 but not exactly 5.
