I wrote `src/fly-controls.ts`. It calls the library's `attachConfigurableFreeControl` rather than `attachFreeControl`, because the notes say the default free-camera controls map Shift to "down". The configurable version lets me set Space to rise, PageDown to sink, and Shift to a 3× speed multiplier, so Shift never moves the camera vertically. The returned function is the library's own disposer, and my wrapper adds no listeners or per-frame work.

Two things I could not verify, since the library isn't on this machine:
- I imported `attachConfigurableFreeControl` from the package root `"@babylonjs/lite"`. The `.d.ts` file only shows the module where it is declared, so the root export is an assumption. The tsconfig's path alias points at `../babylon-lite`, which is also not present here.
- I'm relying on the notes for the key-list and multiplier behaviour, and on the assumption that this variant includes mouse look and that its dispose removes everything it adds. I have not run or type-checked the file.
