I wrote `src/limited-orbit.ts` in `/home/dev/a85c9c/app`. It sets the radius bounds with the library's `setCameraLimits`, which clamps the current distance right away and every later wheel or pinch write, and attaches the standard controls with `attachControl`, passing the scene in so drag and wheel move the camera. The returned function detaches the controls, releases the limits, and restores the radius limit fields it found. I couldn't compile or run this, since the library isn't on the machine.

- The notes don't say whether `attachControl`'s returned function also removes its per-frame hook from the scene. I assumed it does.
