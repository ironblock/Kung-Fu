I wrote `src/limited-orbit.ts` in the app folder. `attachLimitedOrbit` calls the library's `attachControl` with the scene, so drag and wheel move the camera each frame. It also calls `setCameraLimits` on the radius only, so the library clamps the starting radius and every later write, including wheel, pinch, and drag offsets, to `[minDistance, maxDistance]`. The returned function stops both and restores the camera's previous radius limit fields.

I could not run or type-check this: the library isn't on the machine, so the behaviour rests on your notes and the declarations.

- I assumed the package root exports `attachControl` and `setCameraLimits`. The declarations only show their module paths.
- I added a guard that throws a `RangeError` unless `0 < minDistance <= maxDistance` and both are finite.
- Keyboard controls are left off, so Alt+arrow zoom can't bypass the range.
