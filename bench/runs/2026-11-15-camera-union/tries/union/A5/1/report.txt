I wrote `notes/inset-slide.json` with `x` 800, `y` 300, `width` 200, `height` 150, `aspect` 2.0. The first four come from the notes' description of `resolveCameraViewport`: 0.8×1000 = 800, 0.3×1000 = 300 is clipped to the 200 px left before the right edge, and the bottom-origin y of 0.1×500 + 0.3×500 = 200 becomes y = 500 − 200 = 300 from the top. The `aspect` value is the uncertain one. The notes don't cover `getEffectiveAspectRatio`, so I took 2.0 from the unclipped viewport (300 / 150). If the function uses the clipped width, it would be 200 / 150 ≈ 1.33. Because the inset is clipped, that gap matters: if the frame is 200×150 but the picture is drawn at aspect 2.0, the picture will be stretched. Please check `getEffectiveAspectRatio` against the real library before relying on this file.
