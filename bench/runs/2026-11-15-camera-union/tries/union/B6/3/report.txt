I wrote `src/chase-camera.ts` in `/home/dev/bd9803/app`. It builds the camera with `createBankedFreeCamera` (upright, world +Y), and each `bank(up)` projects `up` onto the plane perpendicular to the line of sight and writes that into `camera.upVector`. When `up` is along the line of sight, it returns without writing, so the previous roll stays.
