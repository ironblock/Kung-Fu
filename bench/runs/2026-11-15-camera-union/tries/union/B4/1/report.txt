I wrote `src/globe-tilt.ts`. `tiltGlobeCamera` clears `camera.limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale`, which lifts the library's rule that forces pitch down at large radii, then sets `camera.pitch` and leaves yaw, radius and centre alone. Nothing was run, since the library isn't on this machine.

I'm not fully sure of two things:
- The library applies a limits change on the next yaw, pitch or radius write that changes a value. I'm relying on the pitch write itself doing that. If the library doesn't, the tilt may be clamped to 0.001.
- Clearing the restriction is camera-wide, so the user's own drag or wheel controls can also reach any tilt at any zoom.
