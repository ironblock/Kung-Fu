I wrote `app/src/globe-tilt.ts`. `tiltGlobeCamera` sets `limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale` to `null`, which removes the library's radius-based pitch restriction, then writes `camera.pitch`. It leaves radius, yaw and centre alone, and throws if the library clamps the pitch away from the value you asked for.

I have not run this, because the library isn't on the machine. The approach relies on the notes: note 45 says pitch is locked near 0 at the default start radius, and note 37 says `pitchDisabledRadiusScale = null` frees it. I also couldn't confirm that `setGeospatialOrientation` is exported from the package root, so I used direct property writes instead. Two things to check: that a pitch write leaves the centre unchanged, which note 11 suggests, and that `limits` isn't shared with other cameras in your app.
