I wrote `notes/go-home.json` in the app with `a: "resolves"`, `b: "rejects"`, and `c: "rejects"`. I based this only on the rules in `camera-notes.md` (notes 6, 30, 32, 34), since the library isn't on the machine to test against.

I'm not fully sure of (a). The notes say the first interpolation frame zeroes inertia, which should stop the coast, and a coast that is already under way counts as the start pose. But the notes don't say whether attachControl's per-frame offset step runs before or after the interpolation's frame. If it runs after, the coast could still change alpha once in that frame, and the promise would reject. Also, the notes don't say whether "zeroes inertia" means only the offsets or also the inertia factor. For (c), the notes explicitly say a second call on the camera counts as an outside change, so the first call rejects.
