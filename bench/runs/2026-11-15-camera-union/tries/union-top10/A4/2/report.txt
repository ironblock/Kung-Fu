I wrote `notes/over-the-top.json` in the app with `eyeX` -4.794255386, `eyeY` 8.775825619, `upright` true and `sameAsOtherSide` true. The library isn't on this machine, so I couldn't run anything. The answer comes from the type declarations and your notes.

- **Beta stays at -0.5.** No limits are set and no controls are attached, so nothing clamps the write (notes 2 and 8).
- **Position is an assumption.** The declarations don't include the position formula. I used Babylon.js's convention, `x = r·cos(α)·sin(β)`, `y = r·cos(β)`, `z = r·sin(α)·sin(β)`, which gives the values above. Please check this against Lite's source.
- **Upright is an assumption.** I assumed the view is a look-at toward the target with world up (0,1,0). That is not in the declarations either.
- **Same view is only true up to rounding.** The fresh camera at α=π gets a z of about 6e-16 because `Math.sin(Math.PI)` isn't exactly zero, while the β=-0.5 camera gets exactly 0. Visually they are identical, but they are not bit-for-bit equal.
