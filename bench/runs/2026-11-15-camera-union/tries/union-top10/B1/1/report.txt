I wrote `src/limited-orbit.ts`. It calls the library's `setCameraLimits` with `lowerRadiusLimit`/`upperRadiusLimit` set to your min and max, then `attachControl` with the scene passed in. The returned function calls both disposers once.

I couldn't run or type-check it, since the library isn't on this machine. Things I'm not sure of:

- I'm relying on the notes saying every radius write is clamped once the limits are set. That should include the inertia offset applied each frame from drags and the wheel, but I couldn't confirm it.
- I added a guard that throws unless `0 < minDistance <= maxDistance` and both are finite. Zero is excluded because a zero radius puts the camera on its target.
