I'm not sure of these points:
- **Internal writes bypass the guard.** I can only intercept property writes and `_setOrientation`. The notes don't say whether the library's own controls, `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync`, or the module-level `setGeospatialOrientation` write fields directly.
- **Radius semantics.** I clamp to `radiusMin`/`radiusMax` directly and did not use `clampZoomDistance`, because its signature doesn't say whether its argument is an absolute radius or a delta.
- **Yaw wrapping.** If the library normalises yaw (`normalizeRadians`) and the mode's yaw range doesn't line up with that, clamped values could behave oddly.
- **Data vs accessor fields.** I handle `radius`/`yaw` being either plain data or accessors, but I could not confirm which the library uses.
