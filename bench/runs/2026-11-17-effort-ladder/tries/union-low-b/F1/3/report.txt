I used `attachConfigurableFreeControl` with every movement key listed in `fastKeys` and `fastMultiplier: 20`, which makes each held-key frame add 20× the default per-frame distance without touching `camera.speed` or the look-inertia threshold, so mouse drags turn the camera by exactly the same total amount as the stock controls. The only thing I was not fully certain of is whether `fastMultiplier` is implemented as a pure per-frame input multiplier (which I inferred from note 16) versus some internal change to `camera.speed`; if it were the latter the coasting threshold would shift, but the note's wording ("fastKeys alone do not speed anything up") strongly indicates it is a per-key movement factor that leaves the camera's own speed field untouched.
