I created `src/mode-limits.ts` which assigns the mode's limits object to `camera.limits` (so all future radius/yaw writes are clamped by the camera per note 37) and immediately clamps the current `radius` and `yaw` into the new bounds so the camera is in-range the instant the mode is switched. I was not sure whether the yaw clamping should account for the `[-PI, PI)` wrap in a special way (e.g. when the range straddles the ±PI boundary), but since the limits are presumably author-defined within that range, a plain min/max clamp should be correct.
