I created `src/mode-limits.ts` which assigns the mode's limits object to `camera.limits` (so the library's built-in clamping on subsequent writes uses the correct bounds, per note 37) and immediately clamps the current `radius` and `yaw` into the new range so the camera pose is valid even if it was outside the newly-applied limits. I was not sure whether the yaw limits could represent a wrap-around range (e.g. `yawMin > yawMax` straddling the ±π boundary); I assumed they are a simple contiguous interval within [−π, π), which is the natural case for heading constraints in a map viewer.
