I wrote `src/fly-to-place.ts` which delegates to the library's `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` and, once that promise resolves, checks whether the camera's yaw, pitch, radius, and center match the target place (with angle wrapping for yaw); if they do it resolves `true`, otherwise `false`. The only thing I was slightly unsure of is the exact epsilon values — I used 1e-4 for angles and 1e-3 for distances, which should be tight enough to reject an interrupted flight yet loose enough to absorb floating-point drift over a multi-frame interpolation; if your places can be very close together you might want to tighten them.
