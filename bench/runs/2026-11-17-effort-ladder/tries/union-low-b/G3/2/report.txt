I wrote `src/fly-to-place.ts` which delegates to the library's `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` and, once that promise resolves, checks whether the camera's yaw, pitch, radius, and center actually match the target place (using a small epsilon and normalising yaw to [−π, π) for the comparison). I'm not 100 % certain that `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` is re-exported from the top-level `@babylonjs/lite` entry point rather than a subpath — if your build needs a subpath import instead, that's the one line to adjust.
