I wrote `src/fly-to-place.ts` which delegates to the library's `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` and, once that promise resolves, compares the camera's live yaw/pitch/radius/center against the target place (using a shortest-arc comparison for yaw to handle the ±π wrap) to decide whether the flight completed or was cut short. The one thing I'm not fully certain of is whether the library snaps the camera *exactly* to the goal on completion (the notes say `interpolateArcRotateCamera` does, and I'm assuming `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` behaves the same); if it ever leaves a residual larger than 1e-4 on a completed flight, the epsilon could be loosened.
