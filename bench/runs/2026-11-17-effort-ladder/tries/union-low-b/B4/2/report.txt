I disabled the radius-based pitch clamping by setting `limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale` to `null` (which, per note 37, allows full pitch at any radius), then applied the desired pitch via `setGeospatialOrientation` with only the `pitch` field so that yaw, radius, and centre are left untouched. The null scale also ensures the tilt persists when the app later changes the radius.

I'm not 100% certain that `setGeospatialOrientation` with a partial orientation object truly leaves the omitted fields (yaw, radius, centre) untouched rather than resetting them to defaults, but the optional-field interface strongly implies it only writes what is provided.
