I disabled the radius-based pitch clamping by setting `pitchDisabledRadiusScale` to `null`, forced that limits change to take effect via a self-assignment of `center` (note 37), then set the pitch through `setGeospatialOrientation` which preserves yaw, radius, and center. The camera will hold the tilt through any later zoom because the radius→pitch clamp is gone. I was not fully certain whether `camera.center = camera.center` could have any side-effect on the orbit beyond applying limits, but note 11 and 37 together indicate it re-orbits around the same point (a no-op visually) while flushing the limits.
