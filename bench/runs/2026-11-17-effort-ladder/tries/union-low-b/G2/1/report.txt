I wrote `src/center-on.ts` which converts the latitude/longitude to a point on the planet sphere using the coordinate convention from the notes (north = +Z, east at the prime meridian = −Y, so the longitude sign is negated in the Y component), then assigns a new object to `camera.center` so the camera re-orbits immediately while preserving its current yaw, pitch, and radius. I was not sure whether assigning `center` re-applies the pitch clamp at the current radius (note 37 mentions center assignment triggers limits re-evaluation), but since the stored pitch value itself shouldn't change and only the *effective* rendering pitch is clamped, the tilt should remain visually unchanged.
