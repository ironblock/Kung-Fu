I wrote `src/screen-shift.ts` which snapshots the camera's view matrix, view-projection matrix, pixel viewport, and canvas dimensions on each `mark()` (and once at creation), then on `shift` projects the point with both the stored and current matrices via `projectWorldToScreen` and returns the difference in `cssX`/`cssY`. I'm not 100% sure whether `canvas.clientWidth`/`clientHeight` is the intended source for "CSS pixels" in your setup (vs. computed style or a custom sizing scheme), but it is the standard layout-size accessor and matches the spec's "CSS pixels" phrasing.
