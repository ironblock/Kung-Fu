I computed the pixel viewport by converting the normalized viewport to pixels (accounting for the y-from-bottom → y-from-top flip per note 13) and clamping the right edge to the canvas boundary, and derived the effective aspect ratio from the viewport's pixel dimensions (300 × 150). I'm fairly confident in the clamping logic based on the worked example in note 13; the one small uncertainty is whether `getEffectiveAspectRatio` uses the unclamped viewport dimensions or the clamped ones — I assumed unclamped (i.e., the viewport as the camera sees it), which is the natural reading of "the aspect to match what is rendered" since the GPU render pass uses the viewport scissor before clamping affects the frame overlay.
