I created `src/chase-camera.ts` which uses `createBankedFreeCamera` (the only constructor that exposes a writable `upVector`, per note 28) and implements `bank` by projecting the car's up vector onto the plane perpendicular to the look direction, then writing the normalised result into `camera.upVector` in place. When the projection length is negligible (the loop case where up is parallel or anti-parallel to the line of sight) it simply returns, preserving the previous roll. I was not 100% sure whether `createBankedFreeCamera` is exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` package root or a sub-path, but since `FreeCamera` is imported from the root in your spec I assumed the same applies.
