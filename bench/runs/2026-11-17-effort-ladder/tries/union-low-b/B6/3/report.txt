I created `src/chase-camera.ts` which builds a `BankedFreeCamera` (which extends `FreeCamera` and exposes a writable `upVector`) at the given eye/target, and whose `bank` method projects the car's up onto the plane perpendicular to the current look direction and writes the normalized result into `upVector`, rolling the camera to match; when the projection is near-zero (up parallel or anti-parallel to the line of sight) it leaves the roll untouched. The one thing I'm not fully certain of is whether writing `upVector` components triggers an immediate view-matrix rebuild or defers to the next frame tick — the notes say "writing it rolls the camera" but don't specify the timing, so if the render loop requires a frame boundary the caller may need to ensure a frame runs after each `bank` call.
