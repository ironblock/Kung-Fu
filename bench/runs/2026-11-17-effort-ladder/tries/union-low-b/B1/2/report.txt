I composed the function from the library's own `attachControl` (standard orbit/zoom/pinch controls) and `setCameraLimits` (which clamps the radius on every write, clamps the initial pose immediately, and zeroes the inertial offset when a bound is hit), returning a teardown that calls both disposers so no canvas listeners or per-frame hooks remain. The only thing I was slightly unsure of is whether `attachControl` and `setCameraLimits` are re-exported from the package root `@babylonjs/lite` versus a sub-path like `@babylonjs/lite/camera`; I assumed root since your type import already pulls from there.
