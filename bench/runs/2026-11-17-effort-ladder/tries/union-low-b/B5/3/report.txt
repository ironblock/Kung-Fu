I wrote `src/label-position.ts` which projects the world point through the camera's view-projection matrix via `projectWorldToScreen`, returns `cssX`/`cssY` for the screen position, and computes `visible` as "not behind the camera and not offscreen" (so far-plane and near-plane clipping don't affect visibility, per your spec). I was not sure whether a point between the camera and the near plane should count as visible; I treated it as visible since it is geometrically in front of the camera and inside the frustum, consistent with the "however far away" language applying symmetrically to near-side proximity.
