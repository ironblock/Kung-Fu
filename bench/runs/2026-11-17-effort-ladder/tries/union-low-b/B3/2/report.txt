I used the library's `attachConfigurableFreeControl` with `upKeys` restricted to Space, `downKeys` restricted to PageDown, and Shift mapped as a `fastKeys` entry with a 3× multiplier — this gives you the WASD/arrow/mouse/Space/PageDown layout you described while keeping Shift purely a speed modifier. I'm confident in the key mappings based on the notes; the one thing I didn't verify (since the library isn't on this machine) is whether `fastKeys` is evaluated independently of `upKeys`/`downKeys` in a way that could let Shift also trigger a vertical move when combined with Space or PageDown — but note 16's phrasing that `fastMultiplier` only scales speed and the separation of the option fields strongly implies it doesn't.
