I wrote `src/turntable.ts` implementing `spinAround` with a `requestAnimationFrame`-driven loop that applies the library's exponential easing formula (`1 − 2^(−dt/0.1)` of the remaining distance per frame), zeroes the inertial offsets on the first frame, and snaps `alpha` exactly to `startAlpha + 2π·turns` before resolving. I wasn't sure whether the scene exposes a public per-frame hook I should use instead of `requestAnimationFrame` (the `SceneContext` type isn't in the provided declarations), but since the scene's render loop picks up camera state each frame, driving the update from `rAF` keeps them in sync without any jump.
