I wrote `src/turntable.ts` which delegates to the library's `interpolateArcRotateCamera`, passing only an `alpha` goal of `startAlpha + 2π·turns` so that beta, radius, and target are untouched and the library's exponential easing glides the camera the full unwrapped distance in the direction of increasing alpha.
