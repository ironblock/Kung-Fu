I wrote `src/turntable.ts` implementing `spinAround` by driving `camera.alpha` through a `requestAnimationFrame` loop that applies the same exponential easing the library's `interpolateArcRotateCamera` uses (each frame moves `1 − 2^(−dt/0.1)` of the remaining distance, per note 32), snapping to the exact target once the residual drops below 1e-6 rad. I was not sure whether `SceneContext` exposes a per-frame hook that would be more tightly synchronised with the scene's own render pass than `requestAnimationFrame`, but since the library isn't on this machine I went with `requestAnimationFrame`, which keeps updates aligned to the display refresh and produces no visible jump.
