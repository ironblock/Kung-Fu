I wrote `src/focus.ts` which sets the camera's target in place (so subsequent angle/distance changes orbit the new point) and then nudges alpha by a tiny amount and restores it, forcing the library to rebuild its cached view and world matrices immediately with the new target while preserving the original angles and radius. The only thing I was slightly unsure of is whether the 1e-6 nudge could theoretically be swallowed if the camera's alpha happens to sit within 1e-6 radians of a hard limit set via `setCameraLimits`; in practice for a product viewer the camera will not be that close to a boundary, but if you ever see it not updating, bumping the nudge to `1` (radian) would be a safe fallback since the intermediate state is never rendered.
