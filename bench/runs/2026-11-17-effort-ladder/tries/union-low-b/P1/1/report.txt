I read the type declarations and the notes, then wrote the answer to `notes/preview-controls.json`. Per note 1, `attachControl` without a scene makes drags and wheel only accumulate inertial offsets that are applied in a `_beforeRender` hook (which never fires without a render loop), so `alpha`, `beta`, and `radius` stay put; a two-finger pinch, however, writes `radius` directly at once (confirmed by note 22), so it does change the camera. I was not unsure of anything — the notes are explicit on all three cases.
