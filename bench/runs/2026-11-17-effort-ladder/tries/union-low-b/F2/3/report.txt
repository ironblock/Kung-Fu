I created `src/walkthrough.ts` which builds a free camera at the given eye/target, attaches the standard free-control to the canvas (giving the usual WASD/arrows + drag-to-look behaviour), and exposes `jumpTo` that repositions the camera and syncs the internal `_yaw`/`_pitch` so the controls carry on from the new orientation, plus `dispose` which calls the disposer returned by `attachFreeControl` to remove all canvas listeners and the per-frame hook. I was not entirely sure whether `scene.camera` is a settable property on `SceneContext` (its full declaration wasn't in the provided files), but the requirement states the camera should be `scene.camera`, so I assign it directly.
