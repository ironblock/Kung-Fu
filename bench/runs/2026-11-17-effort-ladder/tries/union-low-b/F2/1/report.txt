I created `src/walkthrough.ts` which builds a `FreeCamera` at the given eye/target, attaches the default free-camera controls (WASD/arrows for movement, drag to look) via `attachFreeControl` with the scene so motion is applied each frame, and exposes `jumpTo` that repositions the camera and updates `_yaw`/`_pitch` so the controls continue from the new orientation. I'm not entirely sure about the sign convention for `_pitch` (whether positive means looking up or down in this library's build); I assumed positive pitch = looking up (toward +Y), consistent with the `atan2(dy, horizontalMagnitude)` formula and the note that pitch is clamped to ±(π/2 − 0.01).
