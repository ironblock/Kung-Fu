I wrote `notes/tighter-zoom.json` with the four radius values based on the behavior described in the notes: a limits change only takes effect on a value-changing yaw/pitch/radius write or any center assignment, so `asIs` and `radiusSelf` leave the radius at 4000 while `centerSelf` and `emptyOrientation` (which internally assigns center via `setGeospatialOrientation`) clamp it to 2000. I was confident in all four cases; the one nuance worth flagging is that `setGeospatialOrientation(camera, {})` with no fields specified still performs an internal center assignment (per note 11), which is what triggers the clamp.
