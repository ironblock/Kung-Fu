I wrote `src/label-position.ts` projecting a world point to CSS-pixel coordinates using `resolveCameraViewport` for the viewport, copying the view/view-projection matrices (they're in-place caches), passing both `cssWidth`/`cssHeight` and reading `cssX`/`cssY`, and defining `visible` as `!behindCamera && !offscreen` so distant in-frame points count as visible while behind and offside points don't. I was not fully sure whether your camera ever sets `_useFloatingOrigin` (I handle it defensively via `getCameraPosition`), and I assumed `canvas.clientWidth`/`clientHeight` are the correct CSS dimensions to scale into — if your canvas is sized differently (e.g., CSS-scaled with a devicePixelRatio mismatch), the CSS mapping may need adjusting.
