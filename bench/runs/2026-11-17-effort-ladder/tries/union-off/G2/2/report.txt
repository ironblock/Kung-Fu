I wrote `src/center-on.ts` exporting `centerOnLatLon`, which converts the latitude/longitude to a point on the planet's surface using the camera's own frame (north = +Z, east = −Y, with lat 0/lon 0 at (R, 0, 0)) and assigns a fresh object to `camera.center` so the camera re-orbits immediately while leaving yaw, pitch, and radius untouched. I was not certain of the exact shape of the `Vec3` type since `math/types.js` wasn't provided, but its `{x, y, z}` structure is clear from the rest of the declarations and notes, so I assigned a plain object literal.
