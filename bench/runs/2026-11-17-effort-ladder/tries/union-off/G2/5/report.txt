I wrote `src/center-on.ts` exporting `centerOnLatLon`. It converts the latitude/longitude to a point on the planet's surface using the camera's `limits.planetRadius`, following the axis convention from your notes (centre at lat/lon 0 is +X, north is +Z, east is −Y), and assigns a whole new object to `camera.center` so the camera re-orbits immediately while leaving yaw, pitch and radius (heading, tilt, zoom) untouched.

The one thing I was not fully sure of: whether you'd prefer `setGeospatialOrientation` (the purpose-built API) over a direct `center` assignment — both re-orbit at once per note 11, but I chose the direct assignment because it changes only the centre and guarantees the heading/tilt/zoom are byte-for-byte unchanged rather than re-running the orientation/clamp logic.
