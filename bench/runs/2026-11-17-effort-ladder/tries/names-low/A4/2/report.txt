I computed the arc-rotate camera's world position using the standard spherical-coordinate formula (the same one Babylon's full library uses), confirmed the up-vector stays upright for a sub-horizon camera, and verified the antipodal camera at `alpha=π, beta=0.5` produces a different view. I'm confident in the position math and the upright answer; the one thing I'm slightly less certain about is whether Babylon Lite's internal `worldMatrix` computation applies any extra transformation (e.g., a different convention for the up vector at extreme betas), but based on the type declarations the interface is identical to standard Babylon and no such special-casing is exposed.
