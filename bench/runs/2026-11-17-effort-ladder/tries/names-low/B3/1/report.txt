I wrote `src/fly-controls.ts` which delegates to the library's `attachConfigurableFreeControl`, configuring Space/PageDown as the vertical keys and Shift as the 3× fast key. The "Shift never moves the camera by itself" requirement is satisfied structurally: Shift is only listed in `fastKeys` (a multiplier), not in `upKeys` or `downKeys`, so it can only amplify other movement. I'm not sure whether the library's default key list for horizontal movement already includes both WASD and arrow keys or only one set—if it only includes one, you may want to confirm the defaults match your spec.
