I attached the library's standard arc-rotate pointer controls via `attachControl` (giving drag-to-orbit, wheel and pinch-to-zoom) and registered per-frame radius clamping via `setCameraLimits` with the supplied min/max; the camera is also clamped immediately if it starts out of range, and the returned cleanup detaches both. I'm not 100% certain that `attachControl` and `setCameraLimits` are re-exported from the `"@babylonjs/lite"` package root (the `.d.ts` files are internal module paths), but given the user's existing import style they almost certainly are.
