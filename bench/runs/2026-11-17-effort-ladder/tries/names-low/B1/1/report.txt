I wrote `src/limited-orbit.ts` which clamps the initial radius, uses the library's `setCameraLimits` to enforce `lowerRadiusLimit`/`upperRadiusLimit` every frame through the scene, and attaches the standard orbit controls via `attachControl`. I'm not 100% certain that `setCameraLimits` clamps *after* the controls apply their per-frame changes (as opposed to before), but given that it accepts a `SceneContext` and the camera exposes `_clampToLimits`, it is the library's purpose-built mechanism for this and should guarantee the radius stays in bounds on every rendered frame.
