I wrote `src/focus.ts` which re-aims the arc-rotate camera's orbit centre by copying `point` into `camera.target`. Because an arc-rotate camera derives its position from `target + radius · direction(alpha, beta)`, changing the target shifts the camera by exactly the old-target-to-point offset while preserving angles and distance, and the camera naturally looks at the new target. I'm not sure whether the lite build requires any explicit cache-invalidation call (e.g. bumping `_viewVer`) for the view matrix to reflect the change within the same frame — if the library doesn't dirty-check `target` mutations, you may need to additionally call an internal invalidate or recompute; but given the public API surface shown, a plain property write is the standard contract.
