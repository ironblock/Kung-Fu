I wrote `src/focus.ts` which re-centres the arc-rotate camera on the picked point by assigning the new point into `camera.target` (keeping `alpha`, `beta`, `radius` untouched so the camera translates by exactly the old-centre→point offset and still looks at the new target), and I zeroed the five inertial offsets so no residual momentum skews the first frame. One thing I wasn't fully sure of: whether the library's `Vec3` for `target` is a plain mutable object or a class with a `set`/`copyFrom` method — I used direct property assignment, which is the standard Babylon.js pattern and matches the `{ x, y, z }` shape shown in your signatures, so it should be fine.
