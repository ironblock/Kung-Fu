I wrote the answer to `notes/same-frame.json`. The two "layout" lines are safe in the sense that they don't cause a rejection or corrupt the radius, but `spot.x = 5` silently retargets the in-flight interpolation (the goal holds a live reference to the scratch vector), so the camera ends up looking at x=5 rather than x=1 — if that's not intended, the mutation must move to after `done` settles. I was not 100% certain the interpolation reads the goal's `target` reference every frame versus snapshotting it at call time, but the Babylon.js convention (and the fact that the goal field is typed as `Vec3` rather than `{x,y,z}` primitives) strongly indicates a live reference is held.
