I wrote `src/label-position.ts` which uses the library's `projectWorldToScreen` helper (along with `getViewMatrix`, `getViewProjectionMatrix`, and `getEffectiveAspectRatio`) to project the world point into CSS-pixel coordinates, then reports `visible` as true when the point is neither behind the camera nor offscreen — deliberately ignoring the `clipped` flag so that points beyond the far plane still count as visible per your spec. I'm not 100% certain whether `offscreen` in the library's result refers to the NDC frustum bounds or the pixel-rectangle bounds, but for a full-canvas viewport those are equivalent, so either interpretation gives the correct answer here.
