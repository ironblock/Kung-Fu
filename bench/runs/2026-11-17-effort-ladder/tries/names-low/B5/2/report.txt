I wrote `src/label-position.ts` which uses the library's `projectWorldToScreen` helper (along with `getViewMatrix`, `getViewProjectionMatrix`, `getEffectiveAspectRatio`, and `resolveCameraViewport`) to project the world point into CSS-pixel screen coordinates. A point is considered visible when it is neither behind the camera nor offscreen; I deliberately ignore the `clipped` flag so that points beyond the far plane still count as visible, per your spec. I'm not 100% certain that `offscreen` in the library's result excludes points behind the camera (it might), but since I also check `behindCamera` independently, the combined condition should be correct either way.
