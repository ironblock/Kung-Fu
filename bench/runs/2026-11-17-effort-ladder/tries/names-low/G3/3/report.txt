I created `src/fly-to-place.ts` which delegates to the library's `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` and, once that promise resolves, checks whether the camera's current yaw/pitch/radius/center match the target within a small epsilon (0.001, matching the library's `GEO_EPSILON`); if they do it resolves `true`, otherwise `false`. A `.catch(() => false)` ensures the returned promise never rejects. The one thing I'm not certain of is whether `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` is re-exported from the top-level `"@babylonjs/lite"` entry point or only from a sub-path — I assumed top-level since you indicated that's how the library is imported.
