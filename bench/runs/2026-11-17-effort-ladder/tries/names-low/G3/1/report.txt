I wrote `src/fly-to-place.ts` which delegates to the library's `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` and, once that promise resolves, checks whether the camera's yaw, pitch, radius, and center match the target place within a small epsilon (0.001, matching the library's `GEO_EPSILON`). If they match the flight completed successfully and the promise resolves with `true`; if they don't (meaning the flight was cancelled partway, e.g. by a newer fly-to), it resolves with `false`. The promise never rejects because `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` returns `Promise<void>` and I only chain a `.then` on it.

The one thing I was not sure of: whether `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` is exported from the package root (`@babylonjs/lite`) or requires a sub-path import (e.g. `@babylonjs/lite/camera`). I assumed it's available at the root since your existing import of `GeospatialCamera` and `SceneContext` comes from there. If it's not, the import line just needs a path adjustment.
