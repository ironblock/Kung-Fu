I wrote `src/turntable.ts` which delegates to the library's `interpolateArcRotateCamera`, passing a goal that sets only `alpha` to the starting value plus `2π·turns`, so beta, radius, and target are left untouched and the library's built-in easing handles the glide. The one thing I'm not sure of is whether the package re-exports `interpolateArcRotateCamera` from the bare `@babylonjs/lite` entry point or from a sub-path (e.g. `@babylonjs/lite/camera/arc-rotate-interpolate`); if the import fails at build time, that's the place to adjust.
