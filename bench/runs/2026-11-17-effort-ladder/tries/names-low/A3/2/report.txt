I wrote `src/screen-shift.ts` which captures the camera's view/projection matrices, viewport, and canvas dimensions at mark-time (copying the matrices to avoid mutations of internal caches), then on `shift` projects the point through both the stored and current camera states via `projectWorldToScreenToRef` and returns the CSS-pixel delta. The one thing I wasn't sure of is whether `Mat4`, `PixelViewport`, `ScreenProjectionOptions`, and `ScreenProjectionResult` are re-exported from the `"@babylonjs/lite"` entry point or only from internal sub-paths — if they aren't, the type imports at the top would need to be adjusted to the correct sub-module paths.
