I wrote `src/mode-limits.ts` which assigns the mode's `GeospatialLimits` to `camera.limits` and then replaces the `radius` and `yaw` properties with clamping accessors (via `Object.defineProperty`) so that any subsequent write is bounded by that mode's `radiusMin`/`radiusMax` and `yawMin`/`yawMax`; on a later mode switch the current values are also clamped into the new range before new accessors are installed. I'm not 100 % certain that the camera's internal `_setOrientation` or rendering path goes through the `radius`/`yaw` property setters rather than touching a private backing field directly — if it does, the clamping would not intercept those internal writes.
