I wrote the four answers to `notes/tighter-zoom.json`. The key distinction is that only `setGeospatialOrientation` (even with an empty object) routes through `_setOrientation`, which is where clamping against `limits` happens; direct property writes bypass that path entirely. I'm confident in the reasoning from the type declarations, though I can't be 100% certain `_setOrientation` clamps radius against `radiusMax` without seeing the implementation — it's the standard pattern in Babylon's camera architecture, and the presence of `GeospatialLimits` on the camera plus the dedicated `_setOrientation` method strongly implies it.
