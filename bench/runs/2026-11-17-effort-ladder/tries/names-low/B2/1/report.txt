I doubled the camera's `angularSensibility` before attaching the library's standard `attachControl`, which makes both the live drag and the post-release inertia rotate at twice the default rate while leaving wheel zoom (governed by `wheelPrecision`) and panning (governed by `panningSensibility`) untouched; the returned cleanup calls the library's detach and restores the original sensitivity. The one thing I'm not fully certain of is whether `attachControl` is exported at the top level of `@babylonjs/lite` or lives under a sub-path (e.g. `@babylonjs/lite/camera`), since the declaration file doesn't show the package's `exports` map — if it's not a top-level export, the import would need to be adjusted.
