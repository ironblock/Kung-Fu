I doubled `camera.angularSensibility` before handing the camera to the library's standard `attachControl`, which makes both the drag rotation and the resulting inertia glide exactly twice as far, while leaving wheel zoom (governed by `wheelPrecision`) untouched; the returned cleanup calls the library's detach and restores the original sensitivity. The one thing I'm not fully certain of is whether `attachControl` reads `angularSensibility` dynamically each frame or snapshots it at attach time — given it's a public mutable property on the camera, dynamic reading is almost certainly the case, but I couldn't verify against the implementation.
