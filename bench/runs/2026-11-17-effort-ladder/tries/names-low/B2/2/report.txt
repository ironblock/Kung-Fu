I doubled the camera's `angularSensibility` before calling the library's standard `attachControl`, which makes both the live drag and the post-release inertial glide rotate twice as far per pixel of movement, while leaving wheel zoom (governed by `wheelPrecision`) untouched; the returned cleanup restores the original sensitivity and calls the library's detach. I'm not 100% certain that `attachControl` is re-exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` package root (the declarations show it in an internal `arc-rotate-controls` module), so if your build complains you may need to adjust the import path to wherever your package re-exports it.
