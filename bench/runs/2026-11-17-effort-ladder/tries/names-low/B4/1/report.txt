I wrote `src/globe-tilt.ts` which temporarily widens the camera's pitch limits, calls `setGeospatialOrientation` with only the `pitch` field (so yaw, radius, and center are untouched), then restores the limits. I'm not 100% certain that `setGeospatialOrientation` preserves the other fields when they are omitted — the type marks them optional, which strongly implies it, but I can't verify the runtime behavior without the library. I also can't be fully certain that restoring the limits won't cause a subsequent zoom interaction to re-clamp the pitch via `getEffectivePitchMax`; if that turns out to be a problem in practice, the fix would be to leave the widened limits in place or to re-assert the pitch after each zoom change.
