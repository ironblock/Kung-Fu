I wrote `src/center-on.ts` which converts the given latitude/longitude to a point on the planet's surface sphere (Y-up, lat 0/lon 0 at `(R, 0, 0)`, north pole at `(0, R, 0)`) and sets the camera's centre to that point via `setGeospatialOrientation`, leaving yaw, pitch, and radius untouched. I'm fairly confident in the coordinate convention given the heading constraint you specified; the one thing I wasn't independently able to verify is whether `setGeospatialOrientation` is re-exported from the top-level `@babylonjs/lite` entry point or only from a sub-path — if it isn't, you'd need to import it from the appropriate sub-module.
