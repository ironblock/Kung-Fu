I wrote `src/center-on.ts` which converts the given latitude/longitude (degrees) to a point on the sphere of radius `camera.limits.planetRadius` centred at the origin, using the mapping x = R·cos(lat)·cos(lon), y = R·sin(lat), z = −R·cos(lat)·sin(lon), and assigns it to `camera.center` — leaving yaw, pitch, and radius untouched. I was not sure whether Babylon's geospatial camera internally expects the center to be passed through `clampCenterFromPoles` or `setGeospatialOrientation` for bookkeeping, but since the task only asks to move the centre point and the latitudes are bounded to ±80°, a direct assignment to `camera.center` is the straightforward and correct approach.
