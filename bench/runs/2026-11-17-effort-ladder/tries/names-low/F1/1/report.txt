I scaled `camera.speed` by 20 before handing the camera to the library's `attachFreeControl`, which gives you the standard keyboard + mouse fly controls with all movement keys (W/A/S/D, arrows, rise/sink) travelling 20× farther while leaving `angularSensitivity` untouched so mouse look is identical to default. The returned function calls the library's detach (removing all canvas listeners and per-frame work) and restores the original speed. I'm not certain whether `attachFreeControl` internally snapshots `speed` at attach time or reads it live each frame, but since the declaration exposes `speed` as a mutable property on `FreeCamera` and the control functions take the camera object itself, scaling it before attach is the natural and correct hook.
