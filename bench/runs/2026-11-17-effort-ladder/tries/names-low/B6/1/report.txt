I created `src/chase-camera.ts` which uses the library's `createBankedFreeCamera` to build a camera at `eye` looking at `target` with an initial upright orientation; `bank(up)` writes the car's up into the camera's `upVector`, and skips the write when the up vector is (anti-)parallel to the line of sight (dot ≈ ±1) so the camera keeps its previous roll in that degenerate case. I'm not sure whether `ObservableVec3` has a dedicated `set(x,y,z)` method or any change-notification hook that would be preferable to writing `.x/.y/.z` directly, but given the type declarations only expose it as a `Vec3`-shaped readonly property, direct field assignment is the safest assumption.
