I created `src/walkthrough.ts` which builds a free camera at the given eye/target, assigns it to `scene.camera`, attaches the standard free-camera controls via `attachFreeControl`, and returns a `Walkthrough` whose `jumpTo` sets `position` and `target` directly (so the controls' W-key fly and mouse-drag rotate continue from the new pose) and whose `dispose` calls the detach function returned by `attachFreeControl` to remove all canvas listeners and per-frame hooks.

The one thing I was not sure of: whether `SceneContext` exposes a settable `camera` property versus something like `activeCamera` — I followed your description ("is also `scene.camera`") and used `scene.camera = camera` directly. If the property is named differently in the runtime, that single assignment line would need adjusting.
