I created `src/orbit-camera.ts` which builds an `ArcRotateCamera` looking at the origin from 10 units away (top-down at β = π/2), then calls `attachControl` with `keyboard: true` to wire up both the pointer (mouse/touch) controls and the library's built-in arrow-key controls on the canvas in a single call.

The one thing I wasn't sure of is the exact import paths — I assumed `createArcRotateCamera`, `attachControl`, `ArcRotateCamera`, and `SceneContext` are all exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` package root. If the library uses subpath exports (e.g. `@babylonjs/lite/camera`), the import lines would need adjusting.
