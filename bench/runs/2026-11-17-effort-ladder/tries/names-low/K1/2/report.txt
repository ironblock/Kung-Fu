I created `src/orbit-camera.ts` which builds an `ArcRotateCamera` looking at the origin from 10 units away (alpha 0, beta π/2 for a side-on view), calls `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls()` to register the library's keyboard-control factory, and then attaches the standard pointer (mouse/touch) controls on the canvas with `keyboard: true` so arrow keys also drive the camera. The one thing I'm slightly unsure about is whether `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls()` is strictly required before `attachControl` accepts `keyboard: true`, or whether it's an independent global toggle — I included it to be safe since it appears to be a one-time install step in the type declarations.
