I created `src/orbit-camera.ts` which builds an `ArcRotateCamera` targeting the origin at radius 10, attaches the library's pointer controls to the canvas via `attachControl`, and enables the library's arrow-key control via `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls`. I'm not fully certain whether `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls()` alone is sufficient or whether `attachControl` also needs `keyboard: true` in its options to wire up per-camera keyboard input; the d.ts shows both mechanisms exist, so if the keys don't respond at runtime you may want to add `{ keyboard: true }` as the fourth argument to `attachControl`.
