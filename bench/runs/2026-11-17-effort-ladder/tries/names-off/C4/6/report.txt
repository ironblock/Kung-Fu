I wrote `src/turntable.ts` which captures the camera's starting `alpha`, computes the target as `startAlpha + 2π·turns`, and delegates to the library's `interpolateArcRotateCamera` with only the `alpha` goal so that beta, radius, and target remain untouched; the returned promise resolves when the camera arrives at the new alpha. I was not certain whether `interpolateArcRotateCamera` is re-exported from the `"@babylonjs/lite"` package root or requires a deeper sub-path import, but given that `ArcRotateCamera` and `SceneContext` are both imported from the root in your example, I assumed the same applies to the interpolation helper.
