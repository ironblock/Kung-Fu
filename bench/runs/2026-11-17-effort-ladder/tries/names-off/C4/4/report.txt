I wrote `src/turntable.ts` which exports `spinAround`, using the library's `interpolateArcRotateCamera` to smoothly animate the camera's `alpha` from its current value to `startAlpha + 2π·turns`, leaving `beta`, `radius`, and `target` untouched. I'm not entirely sure whether `interpolateArcRotateCamera` handles alpha wrapping when limits are set, but since the spec guarantees the final alpha equals start + 2π·turns and nothing else moves the camera, I assumed the interpolation works as intended without clamping interference.
