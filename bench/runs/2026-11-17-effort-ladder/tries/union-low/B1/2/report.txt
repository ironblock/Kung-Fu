I composed the two library calls that do the job: `attachControl` wires up the standard orbit/zoom pointer handlers on the canvas, and `setCameraLimits` clamps the radius to the given range both immediately (so an out-of-range starting pose is pulled in) and on every subsequent radius write (covering wheel zoom via the per-frame offset and pinch which writes radius directly).
