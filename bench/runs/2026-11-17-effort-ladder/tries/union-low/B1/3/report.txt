I composed the function from the library's own `attachControl` (for the standard drag/wheel/pinch orbit controls) and `setCameraLimits` (which clamps the radius to the given bounds on every write and snaps the pose inside immediately), returning a combined disposer that undoes both. I'm not sure whether `setCameraLimits` strictly requires the `scene` argument for its clamping hook to fire each frame or whether it works purely as an inline patch on the camera's radius setter; I passed it in to be safe since the controls themselves depend on the scene for their `_beforeRender` step.
