I wrote `src/turntable.ts` which captures the camera's current `alpha`, computes the goal as `startAlpha + 2π·turns`, and delegates to the library's `interpolateArcRotateCamera` with only the `alpha` field in the goal (so beta, radius, and target are untouched). The library's exponential easing handles the smooth glide, and per note 58 alpha is unwrapped so a goal two full turns ahead rotates the long way in the direction of increasing alpha. I was not sure whether `interpolateArcRotateCamera` is re-exported from the package root `@babylonjs/lite` or lives under a subpath — if your bundler complains, adjust the import specifier accordingly.
