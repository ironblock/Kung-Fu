I wrote `src/turntable.ts` which captures the camera's current `alpha`, computes the goal as `alpha + 2π·turns`, and delegates to the library's `interpolateArcRotateCamera` with only that alpha goal so beta, radius, and target are untouched. One thing I was not sure of: note 56 says alpha takes the shortest arc mod 2π, but note 58 explicitly states alpha is "unwrapped" and gives the example that a goal of 2π from 0 ends at 2π — I went with note 58's reading (full rotation) since it matches the requirement, but if the library actually wraps alpha to the shortest arc internally, a full turn (turns = 1) would resolve immediately without visible motion.
