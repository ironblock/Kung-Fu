I wrote `src/turntable.ts` which chains successive `interpolateArcRotateCamera` calls, each advancing `alpha` by at most π radians, so that the library's shortest-arc easing always travels in the direction of increasing alpha and the exponential ease-out gives the smooth glide with no frame-to-frame jump. The only thing I was not fully certain of is the exact behavior at the π boundary (where both arcs are equal length); I assumed the implementation resolves in the direction of the goal, which is the natural reading of the notes.
