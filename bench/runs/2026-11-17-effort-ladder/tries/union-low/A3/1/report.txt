I wrote `src/screen-shift.ts` which captures the camera's view matrix, view-projection matrix, viewport, canvas dimensions, and (if floating-origin is active) the camera position at mark time, then computes the CSS-pixel screen positions of the point under both the stored and current camera states via `projectWorldToScreen` and returns the delta. I'm not 100% certain that `Mat4` and `ScreenProjectionOptions` are re-exported from the `"@babylonjs/lite"` package root versus a sub-path — if they aren't, the type imports would need adjusting to the sub-path that provides them.
