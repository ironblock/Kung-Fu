I wrote `src/fly-controls.ts` as a thin wrapper around the library's `attachConfigurableFreeControl`, restricting up to Space, down to PageDown, and designating both Shift keys as the fast key with a 3× multiplier — which removes Shift from the default down-keys so it can never move the camera vertically. I'm confident in the mapping based on the notes (especially #16 and #55); the only thing I'm slightly unsure of is whether `attachConfigurableFreeControl` also respects the `fastKeys`/`fastMultiplier` option for the drag-to-look speed or only for key-driven movement, but since the requirement is about keyboard movement speed, it should be fine either way.
