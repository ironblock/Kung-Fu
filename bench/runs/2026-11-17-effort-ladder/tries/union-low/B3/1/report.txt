I used `attachConfigurableFreeControl` with `upKeys` narrowed to `["Space"]` and `downKeys` to `["PageDown"]` (which drops the default Shift/PageUp entries so Shift can never raise or sink the camera), and `fastKeys` set to both Shift keys with `fastMultiplier: 3` so holding Shift triples movement speed. The returned dispose function is the one `attachConfigurableFreeControl` gives back, which removes the canvas listeners and the per-frame hook. I'm confident in this; the only minor assumption is that `attachConfigurableFreeControl` keeps the default WASD/arrow movement keys and mouse-drag look when only `upKeys`/`downKeys`/`fastKeys`/`fastMultiplier` are overridden, which the notes confirm.
