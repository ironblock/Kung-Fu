I wrote `src/fly-to-place.ts` which delegates the flight to the library's `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` (which per your notes always resolves, never rejects, whether the flight completes or is cut short), then on resolution compares the camera's final yaw/pitch/radius/center against the target place using a small epsilon (with shortest-arc handling for yaw) to decide whether the camera actually arrived. The only thing I was slightly unsure of is the exact epsilon magnitude — I used `1e-4`, which is well below the library's `GEO_EPSILON` of `0.001` and should be comfortably above floating-point noise for a completed flight, but if your app's flight ever lands a hair's-breadth short due to clamping edge cases you might want to tune it.
