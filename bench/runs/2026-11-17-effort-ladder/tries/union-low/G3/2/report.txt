I wrote `src/fly-to-place.ts` which delegates to the library's `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` (which drives the flight from the scene's `_beforeRender` loop and resolves without rejecting whether it completes or is cut short), then inspects the camera's final pose against the target place to resolve `true` or `false`. I'm slightly unsure whether the library snaps the camera *exactly* to the goal on the final frame (the notes confirm this for `interpolateArcRotateCamera` but not explicitly for the geospatial fly), so I used a 1e-6 tolerance to be safe — if the flight is genuinely cut short the camera will be far outside that epsilon anyway.
