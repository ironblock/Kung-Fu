I wrote `src/fly-to-place.ts` which delegates the animation to the library's `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` (driven by the scene's `_beforeRender` loop per the notes), then on resolution checks whether the camera's yaw/pitch/radius/center match the target place within `GEO_EPSILON` to distinguish a completed flight from one that was cut short. I'm not entirely certain the library snaps the camera to *exactly* the goal on the final frame (as opposed to getting very close), but the 0.001 tolerance should cover any residual floating-point drift; if in practice the final frame overshoots or undershoots by more than that, the epsilon could be widened.
