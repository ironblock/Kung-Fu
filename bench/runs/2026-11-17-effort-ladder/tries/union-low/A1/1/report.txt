I wrote `src/focus.ts` which sets the camera's target in place and then nudges `alpha` by a tiny epsilon and back to force the view-matrix cache to rebuild immediately (per note 3, target writes alone don't invalidate the camera). The only thing I'm not fully certain of is whether the 1e-7 nudge could be nullified if `alpha` happens to sit exactly on a limit boundary set by `setCameraLimits`; in that rare case the update would defer to the next user interaction, but in normal product-viewer use this won't occur.
