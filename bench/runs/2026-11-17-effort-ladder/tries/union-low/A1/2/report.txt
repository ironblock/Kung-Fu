I wrote `src/focus.ts` which sets the camera's target in place (as the notes require) and then nudges `alpha` by 1e-10 and restores it, which forces the view matrix to rebuild in the same frame so the new orbit centre is immediately visible. I'm confident in the approach; the only thing I'd flag is that if the camera has extremely tight alpha limits (smaller than 1e-10 in width) the nudge could theoretically be clamped, but that would be far outside any practical use.
