I halved `camera.angularSensibility` before calling the library's `attachControl`, which doubles the alpha/beta offset added per pixel of drag (including the inertial glide, since the starting offset is doubled and decays at the same rate); wheel zoom and panning are untouched because they use `wheelPrecision` and `panningSensibility` respectively. The only thing I was slightly unsure of is whether `attachControl` reads `angularSensibility` at event time versus caching it at attach time — the notes describe the per-event formula in terms of the camera's live property, so I'm confident it's read at event time, but if the library ever snapshots it at attach, this approach would need to be swapped for intercepting pointer events directly.
