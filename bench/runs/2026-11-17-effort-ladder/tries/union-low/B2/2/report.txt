I halved `camera.angularSensibility` before calling the library's `attachControl`, which doubles the per-pixel offset fed into `inertialAlphaOffset`/`inertialBetaOffset` (note 70), so every drag and the subsequent inertial glide rotate twice as far while wheel zoom is untouched; the returned cleanup calls the library's dispose and restores the original sensibility. I'm confident in this approach; the only thing I can't verify without the library source is whether `attachControl` reads `angularSensibility` dynamically on each pointer-move event versus capturing it once at attach time, but the notes describe it as a live camera property and standard Babylon controls work that way.
