I created `src/orbit-camera.ts` which builds an `ArcRotateCamera` at radius 10 looking at the origin (alpha 0, beta π/4 for a 3/4 view), calls `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls()` (required per note 9 before the keyboard option is honoured), then attaches the standard pointer/touch controls via `attachControl` with `keyboard: true` so arrow keys work on the focused canvas. The one thing I wasn't fully sure of is whether `enableArcRotateKeyboardControls()` is safe to call on every invocation of `createOrbitCamera` (it appears to install a factory, so repeated calls should be harmless, but I couldn't verify from the declarations alone).
