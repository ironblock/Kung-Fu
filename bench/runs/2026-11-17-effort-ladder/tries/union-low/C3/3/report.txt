I wrote `notes/arrow-keys.json` with the three answers derived from the "normalised" input-vector behaviour described in the notes. I'm confident in the Ctrl answer (the vector magnitudes work out identically), and in the two rotation fractions (standard normalisation of a two-key diagonal input). The one thing I'm slightly less certain about is whether the Alt modifier changes *which* keys contribute to the normalisation vector (i.e., whether Up is excluded from the rotation vector when Alt is held, which would make the Alt fraction 1.0 instead of 0.71) — but the most natural reading of the notes is that normalisation happens on the raw key-input vector before modifiers are applied, giving 0.71.
