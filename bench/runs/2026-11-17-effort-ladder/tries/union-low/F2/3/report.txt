I created `src/walkthrough.ts` which builds a free camera at the given eye/target, assigns it to `scene.camera`, attaches the standard free-camera controls (WASD/arrows/Space/Shift + mouse drag), and returns a `Walkthrough` whose `jumpTo` rewrites position, target, and the internal `_yaw`/`_pitch` so the controls resume from the new view, and whose `dispose` calls the control's teardown to remove canvas listeners and the per-frame hook.

The one thing I was not fully sure of is whether `ObservableVec3` exposes plain writable `x`/`y`/`z` properties (as opposed to requiring a setter method); given that the type declarations show it as the same shape as `Vec3` and the notes discuss in-place writes to camera vectors, I assumed direct property assignment is correct.
