I created `src/chase-camera.ts` which uses `createBankedFreeCamera` (the only factory that exposes a writable `upVector` for rolling) and sets that vector to the car's up each frame, with a guard that skips the write when the up vector is (anti)parallel to the view direction so the previous roll is preserved on loops. The one thing I wasn't fully sure of is the exact epsilon for "exactly along the line of sight" — I used a perpendicular-magnitude threshold of 1e-4, which should be tight enough for the geometric degenerate case while tolerating floating-point noise; if your simulation produces values closer to the singularity you may want to tune it.
