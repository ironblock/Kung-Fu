I created `src/chase-camera.ts` which builds a `BankedFreeCamera` (the only camera type in Babylon Lite that exposes a writable `upVector`) at the given eye/target with world-up, and whose `bank` method projects the car's up onto the plane perpendicular to the look direction, normalises it, and writes it to `upVector`—skipping the write when the projection is degenerate (car up along the line of sight) so the previous roll is preserved. I was not sure whether `ObservableVec3` requires any special notification call after writing its components (as opposed to a plain `x/y/z` assignment), but note 28's "writing it rolls the camera" suggests a plain write is sufficient.
