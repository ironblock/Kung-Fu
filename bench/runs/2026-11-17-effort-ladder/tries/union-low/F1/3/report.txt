I multiplied `camera.speed` by 20 before attaching the library's `attachFreeControl` (which provides the standard WASD/arrow/Space/Shift keyboard movement and mouse-drag look), then restored it on dispose so no listeners or per-frame work remain. The one thing I'm not fully certain of: because the look-inertia stop threshold is `speed * 0.001`, a 20× speed makes the camera's post-drag coasting end slightly sooner for very large drags (the initial drag rotation itself is unchanged), so "exactly the same total amount once at rest" holds for all but the largest swipes.
