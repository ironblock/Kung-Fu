I assigned the mode's `GeospatialLimits` to `camera.limits` and then performed a no-op center assignment (`camera.center = camera.center`) to force the new limits to take effect immediately (per note 37), guaranteeing that any subsequent radius or yaw write is clamped to the current mode's ranges. I'm fairly confident in this approach; the one thing I'm slightly unsure of is whether the center-assignment trigger also clamps the *current* radius/yaw in place or merely arms the clamping for future writes—if the app ever needs the pose snapped into range on mode switch, an explicit clamp of `camera.radius` and `camera.yaw` would be an additional step.
