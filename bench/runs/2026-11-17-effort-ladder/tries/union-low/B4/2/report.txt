I disabled the radius-based pitch clamping by setting `limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale` to `null` (so the pitch is no longer forced toward 0 at large radii), then applied the requested pitch via `setGeospatialOrientation`, which only changes the pitch field and leaves yaw, radius, and centre untouched. I'm not 100% certain that `setGeospatialOrientation` re-exports from the package root `@babylonjs/lite` versus a sub-path — if the import fails, it may need to come from a deeper path like `@babylonjs/lite/camera/geospatial-camera`.
