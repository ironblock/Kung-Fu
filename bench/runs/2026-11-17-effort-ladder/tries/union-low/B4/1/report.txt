I disabled the radius-based pitch clamp by setting `limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale` to `null`, then set the pitch via `setGeospatialOrientation` (which preserves yaw, radius, and centre since only `pitch` is passed). Because the clamp is now gone, the pitch holds steady even when the app later changes the radius. I'm fairly confident in this, but the one thing I can't verify without the library source is whether `setGeospatialOrientation` truly preserves omitted fields rather than resetting them to defaults — the type signature (all fields optional) and the codebase's pattern (cf. `setCameraLimits`) strongly suggest it does, but I couldn't confirm it empirically.
