I added `src/restore-mask.ts`, which builds a fresh mask with `createAnimationGroupMask` from the saved names (copied) and mode, copies over `disabled`, and assigns it to `group.mask`. I went through the factory rather than assigning the plain object because the docs say the first factory call is what turns on the controller's masking path.
