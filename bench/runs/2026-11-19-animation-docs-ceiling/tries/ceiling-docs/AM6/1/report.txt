I read the doc's "glTF Skeleton Weight Mixing" section: translation uses weighted sums with no normalization and no blend toward rest pose, and rotation is accumulated then normalized. So A alone at 0.5 gives y = 1 and a full 90° turn, and the two-clip cases give 0.5·2 + 0.5·6 = 4 and 0.75·2 + 0.75·6 = 6. A lone clip therefore can't fade out to the rest pose, and you'd need an idle clip to cross-fade into.
