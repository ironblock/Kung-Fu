I wrote `restoreMask` to give the group a fresh copy of the saved mask, with a new `names` array. The library ignores in-place edits to `names` that keep the same length, and a fresh copy also keeps the clip from sharing the saved object with your settings or another clip. I didn't import anything at runtime, so it doesn't depend on which helpers `@babylonjs/lite` exports. I haven't run it, since the library isn't available here.

One assumption: I rely on the notes' statement that a glTF group reads `group.mask` on each tick and resets masked-out nodes to rest, so no extra call is needed.
