I answered this from the notes alone, since the library isn't here to run. Note 49 says a stopped glTF group gets its `currentTime` set, but the pose is only applied when an engine is passed. Note 26 says clips other than the first start stopped, so clip 1 counts as stopped until `playAnimation` clears that (note 53). That gives not posed with `currentTime` 30/60 = 0.5 for the never-played call, and not posed after `stopAnimation` plus `goToFrame`. It gives posed when you pass the engine. It also gives posed after `playAnimation` then `goToFrame`.

I'm least sure of `playThenSeekPosed`: the notes never say outright that `goToFrame` poses a non-stopped glTF group immediately. I inferred it from note 49 making the engine requirement specific to stopped groups. The 0.5 value also assumes clip 1 lasts longer than 0.5 s, because looping groups wrap the seek time.
