I did not write `notes/am6-weights.json`, because the notes don't say how weights mix on a skinned glTF character.

The mixing rules in the notes (12, 44, 62) are all about `enablePropertyAnimationBlending` and property groups. Under that mode, a lone group at 0.5 writes half its value, with no rest pose and no division by the total. Your setup uses `enableAnimationBlending` on glTF clips, and for that the notes only say two things:
- A glTF group whose weight is not 1 makes the update throw unless the manager has an engine (35).
- Even with an engine, it does not write node targets (35).

Nothing says whether skeletal blending divides by the total weight, mixes toward the rest pose, or just sums. Those choices give different numbers. A lone clip at 0.5 could hold y = 2 and the full 90° turn, or land at y = 3 and 45°. A lone clip can only fade out to the rest pose in the second case.

Any numbers I put in the file would be guesses, so I left it out. If you can send the notes or source for the glTF mixer, such as `weighted-gltf-mixer`, I can fill it in.
