I added `src/jump-to-frame.ts`. It calls `goToFrame`, which converts the frame to seconds at the clip's own frame rate and sets the time, then `playAnimation` to resume. Loop mode and `speedRatio` are left alone. Nothing was run, since the library isn't here.

I wasn't sure of three things:
- **Export:** I assumed `goToFrame` and `playAnimation` are exported from `@babylonjs/lite`. The notes don't list them as missing, but they don't confirm it either.
- **Property clips:** `goToFrame` clamps to the whole clip duration rather than the play range, so a frame outside `fromTime`/`toTime` is allowed. A non-looping clip jumped to its end will finish on the next update.
- **glTF clips:** I assumed `playAnimation` keeps the `currentTime` that `goToFrame` set.
