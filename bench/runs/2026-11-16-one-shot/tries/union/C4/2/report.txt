I wrote `spinAround` as a chain of `interpolateArcRotateCamera` calls, each covering less than π of alpha, because the shortest-arc rule in the notes makes one multi-turn call stop short or snap. I am not sure the chained calls will look smooth: each call's easing restarts at the chunk boundary, so there may be a visible speed pulse at each one. I also could not confirm that `interpolateArcRotateCamera` is exported from the package root, nor the exact snap behaviour at the end of each call, and I cleared the alpha limits during the spin on the assumption that the caller wants a full turn.
