I wrote `focusOn` to set the target's x, y and z in place and keep alpha, beta and radius unchanged, so the camera moves by the offset from the old centre to the point. It then nudges radius, or alpha or beta if radius cannot move, by a tiny step and restores it, so the view updates in the same frame. I could not check any of this: the library is not on this machine, and I have no source for the dirty-marking behaviour beyond your notes (3 and 11). The nudge is skipped only if all three channels are pinned at their limits, and in that case the view would not update until the next write. Pan inertia that is still pending from a drag will keep moving the target after the call; I left that alone because the spec does not cover it.
