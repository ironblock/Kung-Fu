I wrote this from the type declarations and the notes alone, and I have not compiled or run it. The notes say that writing `target` in place does not mark the camera dirty (note 3), and I rely on that. The two-write alpha trick assumes the library marks dirty on each changing write rather than comparing the final value with the last rendered one; I could not confirm which. If it is the latter, the view will not update until the next frame, and the fix is to use a real 1e-9 offset and accept that alpha is off by that amount.

Other things to check: an `interpolateArcRotateCamera` run in progress will reject its promise when the target changes (note 6), and inertial offsets from a drag still in progress will keep moving the camera after the call. I also assumed `getCameraPosition` (via `worldMatrix`) refreshes through the same dirty path as the view matrix.
