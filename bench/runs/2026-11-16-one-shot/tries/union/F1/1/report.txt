I could not write this into /home/dev/461a4d/app from here, so the file above is for you to save at `app/src/big-world-fly.ts`. I chose `attachConfigurableFreeControl` with `fastKeys` set to every movement key and `fastMultiplier: 20`, because it is the only control in the declarations that scales key movement without touching `camera.speed`. Changing `speed` would also change the look-glide cutoff (note 65), which would alter mouse look.

Things I am not sure of:
- I have no library source, so I don't know whether `attachConfigurableFreeControl` is exported from the package root, or whether its mouse look is identical to `attachFreeControl`. Neither can be checked here.
- I haven't confirmed that `fastMultiplier` scales only translation. If it also scales the look-glide cutoff, a drag made while a move key is held would coast less and turn the camera a smaller total than the default. The fix would be a dedicated key handler, which I have not written.
- I assumed the library's default up/down keys are Space/PageUp and ShiftLeft/ShiftRight/PageDown, taken from note 55, and I listed them explicitly so they don't depend on the defaults.
- The 20x multiplier also applies to the decay tail after a key is released, not only while it is held.
