I built this on `attachConfigurableFreeControl`, not `attachFreeControl`, because the configurable version can scale movement keys by a multiplier and `attachFreeControl` cannot. The result is a thin wrapper that adds no listeners of its own.

I'm not sure of several things:
- The notes don't say whether the configurable control's mouse look matches `attachFreeControl` exactly. Your requirement on mouse look should be checked by comparing a drag against the stock control.
- I don't know whether `attachConfigurableFreeControl` is exported from the package root `"@babylonjs/lite"`. It may need a subpath import.
- I don't know if the configurable control's default key sets match `attachFreeControl`'s. I passed the stock rise/sink keys explicitly so this shouldn't matter.
- I haven't confirmed that the multiplier scales the whole per-frame movement, including inertia decay, by exactly 20.
