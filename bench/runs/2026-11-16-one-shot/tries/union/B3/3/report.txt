I wrote a thin wrapper around the library's configurable free-camera controls, passing Space and PageDown as up and down and Shift as the speed key with a multiplier of 3, so Shift never moves the camera vertically. The returned function is the library's own disposer, so the wrapper adds nothing per frame or on the canvas.

Things I'm not sure of:
- I haven't confirmed that `@babylonjs/lite` exports `attachConfigurableFreeControl` from its root. If it doesn't, the import path needs changing.
- I'm relying on the notes for the key behaviour: that `upKeys`/`downKeys` replace the defaults (note 16), that `fastMultiplier` applies to WASD and the arrows as well as vertical movement, and that the returned disposer also removes the per-frame hook.
- The library only hears keys while the canvas has focus.
- My working directory is /tmp/one-shot-lfom8bk7, not /home/dev/0e8ee1/app, so I haven't checked those files directly.
