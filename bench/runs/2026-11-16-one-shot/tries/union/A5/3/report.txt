`x`, `y`, `width` and `height` come from note 13. The viewport is 800 px from the left, and the 300 px width is clipped to the canvas edge at 1000 px, which leaves 200 px. Vertically, the bottom-origin y of 0.1 × 500 = 50 px with height 150 px gives a top-origin y of 500 − 50 − 150 = 300. `aspect` is null because neither the declarations nor the notes say how `getEffectiveAspectRatio` treats the viewport. It could return 2.0 (the unclipped 300 × 150 region, or the full 1000 / 500 canvas if it ignores the viewport), or about 1.33 (the clipped 200 × 150 region). Note 36 suggests it matches what is rendered, so check that against the library before using it. If it returns 2.0, the inset will be drawn 2:1 into a 200 × 150 visible frame and will look squashed during the slide.
