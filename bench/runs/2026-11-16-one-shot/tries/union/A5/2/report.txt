The `resolveCameraViewport` values follow from note 13. The viewport's x is 0.8 × 1000 = 800 and its right edge is at 1100, so note 13's edge clamping cuts the width to 1000 − 800 = 200. The y edges are 50 and 200 px above the bottom, which is 300 px from the top, and the height is 0.3 × 500 = 150. For `aspect`, I took the unclamped viewport ratio, (0.3 × 1000) / (0.3 × 500) = 2.00, because the notes don't say whether `getEffectiveAspectRatio` clamps. That means during the slide the picture is drawn 2.00 wide to 1 high, but the frame is 200 × 150 (about 1.33), so the frame and picture won't line up unless the picture is clipped or scaled to the frame.

I'm not sure of two things. First, if `getEffectiveAspectRatio` uses the clamped pixel size, `aspect` should be 1.33 instead of 2.00. Second, I applied the clamping to the vertical axis too, but it has no effect here because the y edges are inside the canvas.
