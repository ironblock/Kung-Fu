The frame rectangle comes from note 13. The viewport's x (0.8 × 1000 = 800) is clamped at the right canvas edge, so its width is trimmed from 300 to 200. Its y is counted from the bottom and returned from the top, giving 500 − (0.1 × 500 + 0.3 × 500) = 300. The aspect of 2.00 is my inference from the unclamped viewport (0.3 × 1000 ÷ 0.3 × 500). The notes don't say how `getEffectiveAspectRatio` handles clamping. If it uses the clamped rectangle, the aspect would be 200 ÷ 150 ≈ 1.33. In that case the picture and the frame would only match with the clamped value, so please check that against the library source.
