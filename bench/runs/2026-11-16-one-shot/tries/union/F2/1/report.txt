I wrote this from the notes and declarations pasted in your message, not from files on disk. Things I am not sure of:

- **Pitch sign:** I assumed `_pitch` is positive when looking up, because the notes do not say. If the library uses the opposite sign, the first W press or drag after a jump will tilt the view.
- **Root exports:** I assumed `createFreeCamera` and `attachFreeControl` are exported from the package root. The declarations only show their module paths.
- **`scene.camera`:** I assumed `SceneContext.camera` is assignable with a `FreeCamera`. I did not see that type.
- **Disposal:** I assumed the function returned by `attachFreeControl` removes its canvas listeners and its per-frame hook. The notes only say this for `attachControl`.
- **Re-attaching on `jumpTo`:** I chose this to reset coasting turn velocity. It is a design choice, not something the notes require.
