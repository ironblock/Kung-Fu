I answered the question from camera-notes.md items 37 and 11. Plain `camera.radius = camera.radius` doesn't trigger the new limit, since the notes say a limits change applies only at a write that changes the value, or at a center assignment. Adding `camera.center = camera.center;` after the limit line pulls the radius to 2000, and it's the safest option.

I'm least sure of the `emptyOrientation` value. I assumed `setGeospatialOrientation` assigns a new center object internally, which would trigger the same clamp, but the declaration doesn't show that. You can confirm it by checking the function body or by running the case once with the real library.
