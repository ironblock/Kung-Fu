I changed the camera's limits in place rather than replacing the object, so if the same limits object is shared with another camera, that camera's pitch behaviour changes too. The clamp removal is permanent, because restoring the default would bring back the forced pitch at long range. I'm not sure whether `camera.center = camera.center` is needed or harmless in the library, though the notes say it is a supported way to apply limits. I didn't use `setGeospatialOrientation` because I can't confirm it is exported from the package root. Also, the notes say `attachGeospatialControls` can move the centre after a pan or zoom, which could change the tilt during interactive use; I couldn't check that from here.
