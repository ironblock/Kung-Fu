Things I'm not sure of:
- **Exports:** `createBankedFreeCamera` is the only camera factory that has `upVector`, and the `.d.ts` files show internal paths, so I can't confirm it is exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` entry point.
- **In-place writes:** I assumed writing `upVector.x/y/z` in place is observed and rolls the camera, since the notes say writing `upVector` rolls it but don't say which form of write.
- **Vertical views:** The notes document the free camera's behavior when looking straight up or down, not the banked camera's, so I'm not sure how the banked camera behaves there.
- **Threshold:** The 1e-6 cutoff for "along the line of sight" is my choice. Values just above it give a noisy roll direction.
