The answers come from note 6 of camera-notes.md. In (a), the coasting happens before the first frame, so it becomes the start pose, and that frame zeroes the inertia, so nothing changes between frames afterward. In (b), the drag's offsets get applied to alpha, beta or radius between frames, which the notes say rejects the promise. In (c), the second call counts as "anything else" changing the camera, so the first call rejects.

I'm not fully sure of two things. First, I'm reading "that frame zeroes inertia" as clearing the coasting offsets, and I don't know whether the attachControl hook can still apply a leftover offset later in the same frame. Second, I haven't checked the second call's own result, since you only asked about the first call's await. Since (b) and (c) reject, a try/catch around `goHome()` should treat a rejection as "superseded or interrupted," not as a failure.
