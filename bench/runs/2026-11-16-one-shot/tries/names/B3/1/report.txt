I worked from the declarations in your request, since I could not confirm the file on disk or compile against the library. Unverified points:

- I assumed the library's own WASD and arrow-key handling is the default and needs no configuration.
- I'm relying on the returned disposer to remove all canvas listeners and per-frame work, which I could not check.
