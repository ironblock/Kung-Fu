I wrote `src/center-on.ts` to move the camera centre using the library's own east, north and up directions at lat 0, lon 0, and kept yaw, pitch and radius as they were. I have not run or type-checked anything, and I could not check the library itself. Things I'm not sure of:

- I assumed `computeLocalBasis` writes east, north and up into its last three arguments. Its declaration is `void`, so that is the most likely reading, but I haven't confirmed it.
- I assumed `@babylonjs/lite` exports `computeLocalBasis`, `createGeospatialCamera` and `setGeospatialOrientation`, and that `Vec3` is a plain `{x, y, z}`. The declarations you gave don't show the package's index file.
- I did not check that a yaw of 90° means "looking east" in this library. The code doesn't change yaw, so that behaviour comes from the library and is untested here.
- Each call creates a throwaway camera to read the frame. This is cheap but could be cached if it matters.
