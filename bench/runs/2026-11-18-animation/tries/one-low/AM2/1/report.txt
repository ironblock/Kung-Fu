I set the weight directly so it is correct immediately, then called `fadeAnimationWeight` with `from` and `to` both equal to `share` and zero duration; per the notes this replaces any in-flight fade for the group, and because `from === to` the replacement fade can never move the weight away from `share`, while a later real fade will simply replace it again and behave normally. The only thing I wasn't fully sure of is whether the library guards against `durationMs: 0` producing a `NaN` on a zero-delta update, but even in that case the weight stays correct because `from === to` makes the interpolated value `share` regardless of the progress factor.
