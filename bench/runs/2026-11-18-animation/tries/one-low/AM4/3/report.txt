I created `src/mix-all.ts` which builds two separate `AnimationManager` instances—one for the character glTF groups with `enableAnimationBlending` (so bone weights are respected, and the engine is supplied to avoid the throw in note 37) and one for the HUD property groups with `enablePropertyAnimationBlending` (so property weights are respected)—then `step` advances both managers by the given delta. I was not fully certain that `enableAnimationBlending` still produces correct weighted bone matrices when weights differ from 1 (note 37 says node targets are skipped, but I assumed the skeletal path is unaffected); if that turns out not to be the case, the character's bones would need a different approach.
