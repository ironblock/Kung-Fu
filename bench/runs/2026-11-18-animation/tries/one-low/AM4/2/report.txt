I created `src/mix-all.ts` which sets up two separate `AnimationManager` instances—one with `enableAnimationBlending` for the character's glTF groups (so their weights produce the library's own weighted bone mix) and one with `enablePropertyAnimationBlending` for the HUD's property groups (so their weights produce the library's own weighted property mix)—then advances both via `updateAnimationManager` on each `step` call. I was not sure whether the HUD manager strictly needs the `engine` option (property groups don't appear to require it per the notes), but I pass it for symmetry and in case future use cases need it.
