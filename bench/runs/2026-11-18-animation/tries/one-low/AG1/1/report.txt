I set `currentTime` to the clip's `duration` (the last key time) and paused the group by setting `isPlaying` to false. Per the notes, a paused glTF group still writes its pose at `currentTime` on every animation update, so the model will display and hold the final keyframe values while the scene keeps rendering. I'm not sure whether the pose is written on the very next frame or whether there's a one-frame delay before the first write occurs, but since the scene is actively rendering, the visual result appears within a single frame.
