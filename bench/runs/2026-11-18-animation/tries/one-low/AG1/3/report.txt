I paused the group first (so it can't advance and wrap while still looping), then set `currentTime` to `duration`. Per the notes, a paused glTF group still writes its pose at `currentTime` on every subsequent update, so the scene will keep rendering the final keyframe values while the group stays stopped.

I'm confident in this approach based on the notes; the one thing I'm slightly unsure of is whether any consumer in your app reads `isPlaying` to decide whether to show the model at all—if so, you may want to verify the visual result, since the group will report `isPlaying === false` even though the pose is being written each frame.
