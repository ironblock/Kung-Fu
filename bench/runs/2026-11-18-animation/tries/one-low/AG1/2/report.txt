I set `currentTime` to `group.duration` (the end of the clip) and paused the group by setting `isPlaying = false`. Per the notes, a paused glTF group still writes its pose at `currentTime` on every update, so the model will hold the final keyframe values while the scene keeps rendering. I was not sure whether `pauseAnimation` would be preferable to a direct `isPlaying = false` assignment (it might do extra bookkeeping I can't verify without the library), but for a glTF group the direct assignment achieves the stated requirements.
