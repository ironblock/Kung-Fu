I wrote `src/release.ts` which resolves the dotted path to its leaf target and leaf property, then iterates every group on the manager and filters out any `_propertyMixer` tracks whose `mixTarget()` and `mixProperty` match, replacing the group's mixer with a new tuple that excludes them. I was not sure whether the `_propertyMixer` tuple's trailing numeric fields (indices 1–4) encode anything that depends on the track count (e.g., a pre-computed stride sum), but given they appear to be static parameters set at group creation time and the tracks array is what gets iterated at update time, I'm confident the replacement is safe.
