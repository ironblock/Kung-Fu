I wrote `src/beacon.ts` using a custom `AnimationTask` that tracks elapsed time, computes the flash envelope (linear rise over 0.1 s, linear fall over 0.1 s, then zero) scaled by a mutable strength variable, and writes to `lamp.intensity` each update. The returned closure simply reassigns that variable, so a new strength takes effect on the next update without touching the clock. I was not sure whether the "more than 0" lower bound on strength meant I should reject or clamp zero specifically, so I clamped to [0, 1] as the safe range.
