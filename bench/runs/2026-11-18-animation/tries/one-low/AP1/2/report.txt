I wrote `src/fade-in.ts` which builds a two-key linear clip (0 → 1 over `durationMs/1000` seconds) on the `alpha` path and creates a non-looping property animation group on the given manager; with `loop: false` the group clamps at the end, writes 1, and sets `isPlaying` to false, so the panel stays at 1 until you change it yourself. I was not sure whether you expected `panel.alpha` to be snapped to 0 synchronously at call time (the library defers the first write to the next `updateAnimationManager` call), but given that you drive the manager yourself each frame that first write happens on your very next tick.
