I created a property animation clip with three linear keys (1 → 1.5 → 1 over 0.4 s), wrapped it in a non-looping group that is paused at creation so nothing moves before the first press, and returned a press handler that resets `currentTime` to 0 and calls `playAnimation` — which restarts the pulse from the beginning whether the group is mid-flight or already finished. I'm not sure whether `playAnimation` on an already-playing group is a strict no-op or re-asserts `isPlaying`, but either way the `currentTime = 0` assignment (note 11) guarantees the next update begins from the start.
