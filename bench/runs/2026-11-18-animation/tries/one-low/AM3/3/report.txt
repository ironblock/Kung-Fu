I created `src/skip-ahead.ts` which advances the manager by exactly `ms` milliseconds: for fixed-step managers it runs `Math.floor(ms / fixedDeltaMs)` full steps, then temporarily clears `fixedDeltaMs` to handle the sub-step remainder and restores it; for non-fixed managers it simply calls `updateAnimationManager` once with the full `ms`. I'm not sure whether any edge case exists where temporarily zeroing `fixedDeltaMs` mid-call could interact badly with a task that reads it during its update, but based on the notes the fixed step is only consulted at the top of `updateAnimationManager` to choose the effective delta, so it should be safe.
