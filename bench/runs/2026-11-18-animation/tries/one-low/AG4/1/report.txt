I wrote `playAgain` as `stopAnimation` followed by `playAnimation`. For property groups this resets `currentTime` to `fromTime` and restarts playback (note 8 explicitly says to do this rather than calling `playAnimation` alone, which would leave `currentTime` at `toTime`). For glTF groups, `stopAnimation` resets `currentTime` to 0 and sets `isPlaying` false, then `playAnimation` resumes from there; `speedRatio` and `loopAnimation` are untouched, so the clip replays at its own speed and stops at the end again. I'm not aware of any uncertainty — the notes cover both group kinds and the sequence is the one they prescribe.
