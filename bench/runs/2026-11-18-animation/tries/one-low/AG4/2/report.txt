I implemented `playAgain` by calling `stopAnimation` (which resets `currentTime` to the group's `fromTime` and halts playback) followed by `playAnimation` (which resumes from that point), so the clip replays from its first frame at its existing `speedRatio` and runs to its end once more, for both property and glTF groups. I'm confident in this based on the notes; the one small uncertainty is whether `stopAnimation` on a glTF group that is still flagged `isPlaying` (per note 31) behaves identically to the property-group case, but the API contract and note 7's description of `stopAnimation` suggest it uniformly resets time and stops, making the subsequent `playAnimation` a clean restart.
