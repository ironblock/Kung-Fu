I wrote `notes/am6-weights.json` with the blended values derived from the additive-around-rest skeletal blending model that `enableAnimationBlending` uses. I'm confident in the translation math; the one thing I'd flag is that I inferred the quaternion blend uses slerp (giving 45°) rather than linear interpolation of components (which would give a different angle) — the `AnimationPropertyMixStrategy` doesn't expose the quaternion path explicitly, but slerp is the standard for rotation blending in skeletal animators and is consistent with the `quaternion: boolean` flag on runtime tracks.
