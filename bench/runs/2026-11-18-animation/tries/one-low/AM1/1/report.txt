I wrote `src/hand-over.ts` using the library's built-in `crossFadeAnimationGroups`: it snaps `from` to weight 1 and `to` to weight 0 so the next frame shows `from` alone, then cross-fades them linearly over `durationMs` so the shares always sum to 1 and end with `to` alone. No uncertainty — the notes confirm `crossFadeAnimationGroups` fades from each group's current weight, replaces any prior fade on the same group, and neither plays nor stops the groups, which covers every requirement including re-calling mid-transition.
