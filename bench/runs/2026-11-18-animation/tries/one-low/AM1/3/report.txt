I set the weights to the correct starting state (`from` at 1, `to` at 0) and then delegate to `crossFadeAnimationGroups`, which fades `from` linearly to 0 and `to` linearly to 1 over `durationMs`, replacing any in-progress fade on either group so a reverse call mid-transition works cleanly. I was not unsure of anything — the library's cross-fade primitive matches the requirements exactly once the starting weights are pinned.
