I created `src/jump-to-frame.ts` which calls `goToFrame` to seek the group to the requested frame (which sets `currentTime` to `frame / group.frameRate` seconds and pauses it), then immediately calls `playAnimation` to resume playback from that point. Because neither call touches `speedRatio` or `loopAnimation`, the group keeps its own speed and loop behavior. I'm confident this is correct for both property groups and glTF groups that are already playing; the one thing I didn't independently verify is the exact edge-case behavior if a caller passes a frame beyond the end of a non-looping clip (the notes say `goToFrame` clamps it, and `playAnimation` on a property group already at `toTime` would stop again on the next update per note 8, but the problem states the group is playing so I assumed a valid in-range frame).
