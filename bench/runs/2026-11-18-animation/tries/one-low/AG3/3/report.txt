I created `src/jump-to-frame.ts` which calls `goToFrame` to seek the group to the requested frame (which pauses it and, for property groups, writes the value immediately), then calls `playAnimation` to resume playback from that point. This preserves the group's `speedRatio`, `loopAnimation`, and `weight` since neither call mutates them, and it works for both glTF clips and property clips. I was not unsure of anything—the notes confirm that `goToFrame` + `playAnimation` is the documented way to seek-and-resume for both clip kinds.
