I created `src/jump-to-frame.ts` which calls `goToFrame` to seek the group to the requested frame (in the clip's own frame rate) and then `playAnimation` to resume playback from that point, preserving the group's existing `speedRatio` and `loopAnimation` settings. I was not sure of anything — the two-call pattern is directly supported by the API and notes (note 6 confirms `goToFrame` pauses and `playAnimation` continues from there for property groups, and note 31/33 confirm the same seek-then-resume works for glTF groups).
