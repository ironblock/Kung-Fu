I created `src/watched-manager.ts` which builds a standard manager via `createAnimationManager` and registers a task that, on every update, calls `onStep` with the effective step (respecting `fixedDeltaMs`). I was not sure whether the task's `deltaMs` parameter already reflects `fixedDeltaMs` internally, so I compute the effective step explicitly—this is correct whether the library passes the raw or the effective value.
