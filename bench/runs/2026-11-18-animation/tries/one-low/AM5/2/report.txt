I created `src/watched-manager.ts` which builds a standard `AnimationManager` and hooks into its `_preUpdate` field so that every call to `updateAnimationManager` on that manager fires `onStep` once with the effective step (honouring `fixedDeltaMs`). The one thing I was not fully sure of is whether `_preUpdate` is guaranteed to be invoked on every `updateAnimationManager` call in the implementation; it is declared on the interface, which strongly implies it, but I could not verify against the library source.
