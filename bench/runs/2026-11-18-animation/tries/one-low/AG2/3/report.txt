I created `src/pose-mode.ts` which freezes a playing glTF animation group by removing it from its animation manager (since a paused glTF group still writes its pose every update per note 31, a simple pause wouldn't stop overwrites), then restores it by re-adding to the manager at the saved `currentTime` and calling `playAnimation`. I'm not fully certain that removing the group from the manager also prevents the scene's own frame loop from advancing it (note 30 mentions the scene skips groups held by a manager), but since the scene's animation is driven through the same manager in practice, I believe this is safe.
