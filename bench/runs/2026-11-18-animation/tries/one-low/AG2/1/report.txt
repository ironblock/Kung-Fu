I created `src/pose-mode.ts` which freezes a playing `AnimationGroup` by removing it from its animation manager (this stops all updates and node writes for both glTF and property groups, per notes 31 and 46), and returns a `resume` closure that re-adds the group to the same manager and calls `playAnimation` so it continues from its saved `currentTime` at its own `speedRatio`. I'm fairly confident in this approach; the one thing I'm slightly unsure of is whether `removeAnimationGroup` clears `group._animationManager` to `undefined` — if it does, the re-add via `addAnimationGroup` should restore it, but I couldn't verify that detail from the declarations alone.
