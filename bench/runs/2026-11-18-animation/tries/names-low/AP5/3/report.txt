I created `src/beacon.ts` which registers an `AnimationTask` on the given manager; each update it advances an internal clock, computes the phase within the period, and writes the corresponding intensity (ramp up 0→strength over 100 ms, ramp down strength→0 over the next 100 ms, then 0) to `lamp.intensity`. The returned closure simply stores a clamped strength value that is read on the next tick, so changing it never perturbs the rhythm and multiple beacons can coexist on the same manager. I was not sure whether "more than 0" meant I should reject (throw on) invalid input or silently clamp — I chose to clamp to a tiny positive floor.
