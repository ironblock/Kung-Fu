I created `src/beacon.ts` which registers an animation task on the manager that tracks elapsed time, computes the triangular flash profile (0→strength over 0.1 s, strength→0 over the next 0.1 s, then 0 for the remainder of the period), and exposes a setter that stores a pending strength applied on the next tick so the rhythm is never shifted. The only thing I was slightly unsure of is whether the strength clamp should be strictly > 0 as the spec's wording suggests; I clamped to [0, 1] since a value of 0 simply means "no flash" and is a natural edge case.
