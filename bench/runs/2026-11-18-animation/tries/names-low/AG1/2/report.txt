I created `src/skip-to-end.ts` which calls `goToFrame` at the last frame to write the final keyframe pose onto the model, then sets `currentTime` to `duration` (so the progress bar reads 100%), and marks the group as stopped so the animation manager won't tick it further.

I'm not fully certain whether `_stopped = true` alone is sufficient to prevent the animation manager from ticking the group (versus also needing `stopAnimation` to deregister it from the manager's task list), and I defaulted `frameRate` to 30 when it's undefined, which may not match your assets' actual rate.
