I wrote `notes/seek-stopped.json` with the answers derived from the type declarations: `goToFrame` is a direct seek that sets `currentTime` and calls `_evaluate`, so it poses the node regardless of whether the group is playing, stopped, or never played. I'm confident in all five answers; the only minor uncertainty is whether `_evaluate` is guaranteed to be non-undefined at the moment of the call for a never-played group, but since it is populated from the glTF mixer data at `createAnimationGroups` time (not at first play), it should be present.
