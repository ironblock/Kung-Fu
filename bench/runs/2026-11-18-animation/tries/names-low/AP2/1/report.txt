I wrote `src/pulse.ts` using a custom `AnimationTask` on the shared manager: a per-button closure tracks elapsed time, and each press resets it to zero so the linear 1→1.5→1 ramp restarts immediately, while the task is a no-op until the first press. The only thing I was slightly unsure of is whether `deltaMs` is milliseconds vs. seconds — the parameter name says "ms" so I divided by 1000 to get seconds, but if your manager passes seconds directly you'd drop that division.
