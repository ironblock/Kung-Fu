I wrote `src/pulse.ts` which uses `createAnimationTask` to register a per-button task on the shared manager; each press removes any in-flight task and starts a fresh one, and the task's update callback linearly interpolates `button.scale` up to 1.5 over 200 ms and back down over the next 200 ms, then deactivates and removes itself. I was not sure whether `removeAnimationTask` internally sets `active = false` or expects the caller to do so, so I set it explicitly before removal as a safety measure.
