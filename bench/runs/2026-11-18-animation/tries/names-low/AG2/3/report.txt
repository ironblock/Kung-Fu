I created `src/pose-mode.ts` which pauses the group (freezing it at its current `currentTime`) and returns a `resume` closure that calls `playAnimation` to continue from where it left off at the group's own `speedRatio`. No state needs to be saved or restored manually because the group retains `currentTime` and `speedRatio` across pause/play. I'm not aware of anything I'm unsure of — the type declarations make `pauseAnimation`/`playAnimation` the clear fit for this.
