I created `src/pose-mode.ts` which pauses the animation group (stopping it from ticking and overwriting node transforms) and returns a closure that calls `playAnimation` to resume from the frozen `currentTime` at the group's own `speedRatio`. The only thing I was not sure of is whether `pauseAnimation` in Babylon Lite 1.39.0 leaves the last sampled values on the nodes (i.e., does not reset them to rest) — based on the standard Babylon.js semantics and the fact that `currentTime` is preserved on the group, I'm confident it does, but I couldn't verify against the actual runtime source.
