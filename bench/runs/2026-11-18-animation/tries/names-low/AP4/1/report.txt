I wrote `src/release.ts` which iterates every group on the manager, identifies groups that animate the given target+path (checking both `targetedAnimations` and `_propertyMixer` tracks), and either filters the offending tracks out of the group's mixer (stopping the group if none remain) or stops the group outright when no mixer exists. The one thing I was not sure of: for a non-blended (direct-write) group that animates *multiple* properties of the same target, stopping the whole group is the only option available through this API — there is no way to suppress a single track without a mixer — so such a group will be fully stopped even though it also animates other properties of that target.
