I created `src/hover.ts` which registers an animation task on the manager that linearly bobs `node.position.y` between rest and rest + 0.5·strength on a 1-second cycle (0.5 s up, 0.5 s down), and returns a setter for strength that takes effect on the next tick without resetting the phase. I'm confident in the logic; the only thing I couldn't verify is whether `createAnimationTask`/`addAnimationTask` are re-exported at the top level of `@babylonjs/lite` versus a sub-path, but given the type declarations live under the package's animation module that's the natural expectation.
