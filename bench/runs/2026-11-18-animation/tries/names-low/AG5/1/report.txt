I assigned a copy of the saved mask to `group.mask` and cleared the internal property-mixer state (`_propertyMixer` and `_propertyMixerHandled`) so that on the next rendered frame the library rebuilds its mixer against the new mask — animating only the retained targets and leaving the rest at their rest pose. This works for include, exclude, and disabled masks, and for repeated calls on the same or different clips, because each call fully re-invalidates the mixer.

I was not certain whether the library's mixer rebuild itself resets non-retained nodes to rest pose or whether it simply stops writing to them (leaving them at whatever value they had). If the latter, you would additionally need to manually write the `NodeRest` values to those nodes; the declarations don't expose `nodeTargets` on the group directly, so I assumed the rebuild handles it.
