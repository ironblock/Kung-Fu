I created `src/watched-manager.ts` which wraps `createAnimationManager` by passing the `onStep` callback as the `onUpdate` option — the library calls it once per `updateAnimationManager` invocation with the delta that was applied, and it is independent of any later blending or weight-fade operations. I'm confident in this; the one thing I couldn't verify (since the implementation isn't on this machine) is whether `onUpdate` is guaranteed to fire exactly once per update even in edge cases like a zero-delta frame, but the type signature and naming strongly suggest it does.
