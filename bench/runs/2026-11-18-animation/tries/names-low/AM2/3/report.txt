I created `src/set-share.ts` which sets the group's weight immediately, then probes every active task on the manager with a NaN sentinel to find and remove any fade task that is driving this group's weight, leaving other groups' fades untouched. I was not sure whether `removeAnimationTask` is safe to call on a task that may have already self-removed during the `_update` probe (e.g., a fade that finished on that call), but in practice it should be a no-op in that case since the task would no longer be in the array.
