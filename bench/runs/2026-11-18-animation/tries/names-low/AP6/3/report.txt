I built the function around Babylon Lite's property-animation helpers: the keys are mapped into `AnimationKeyframe` objects (using `frame` and a plain `number` value), a single linear-interpolation track is wrapped in a clip, and the clip is turned into a looping group on the given target/path, then immediately played. I'm fairly confident in the approach, but I was not 100% sure whether `createPropertyAnimationGroup` registers the group with the manager automatically or whether a separate `addAnimationGroup` call is needed — the function signature and the `loop` option suggest it does, but I couldn't verify the implementation.
