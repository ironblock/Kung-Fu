I created `src/hand-over.ts` which registers a short-lived `AnimationTask` on the manager that linearly shifts weight from `from` to `to` over `durationMs`, removing itself when the transition completes; a module-level `WeakMap` tracks the active task per manager so a subsequent call (even mid-transition) cancels the old one and starts fresh from full weight on the new `from`. I was not sure whether the library's `removeAnimationTask` also sets `active = false` internally, but since I rely on removal from the `animations` array (which is what the loop iterates), it should be safe either way.
