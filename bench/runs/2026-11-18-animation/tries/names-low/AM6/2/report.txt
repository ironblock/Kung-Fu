I wrote `notes/am6-weights.json` with the four values derived from the normalized weighted-average blending that `AnimationPropertyMixStrategy` implies: a lone clip at weight 0.5 still fully controls the bone (weight cancels), and two equal-weight clips produce a plain average regardless of the weight magnitude. I was not 100% certain that Babylon Lite's concrete `accumulate`/`finish` implementation is the standard multiply-then-divide (as opposed to some other normalization), but the interface signature and the parallel with full Babylon.js make that the overwhelmingly likely reading.
