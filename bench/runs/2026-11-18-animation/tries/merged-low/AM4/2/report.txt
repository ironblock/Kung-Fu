I created `src/mix-all.ts` which builds two separate managers — one for the character (with `enableAnimationBlending` and the engine, so weighted glTF/skeleton mixing works on bones) and one for the HUD (with `enablePropertyAnimationBlending`, so weighted property mixing works on the HUD object) — because the two blending modes occupy the same slot on a single manager (note 25). `step` advances both managers by the given delta each frame.

I was not sure whether the character groups need `playAnimation` called explicitly before being added (the prompt says they are "all playing"), but since `addAnimationGroups` merely attaches them and they are already in a playing state, I did not call it. If they turn out to need a nudge, adding `character.forEach(playAnimation)` before `addAnimationGroups` would cover it.
