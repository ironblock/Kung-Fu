I created `src/restore-mask.ts` which assigns a freshly constructed mask object (with a copied `names` array) to `group.mask`, ensuring the group detects the change per note 34 and correctly applies include, exclude, or disabled masking on subsequent ticks. I was not sure whether the library would also require the mask to be present before the group's first tick or whether assigning it at any point is sufficient, but note 34's wording ("does pick up a new names array… or a change to mode or disabled") implies it reacts to assignment at any time, so I did not add any extra guard.
