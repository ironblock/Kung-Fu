I assigned the deserialized mask object directly to `group.mask`, which is the library's documented way to apply a mask: the group observes changes to the mask reference (note 34), and on each tick it writes rest poses to masked-out nodes (note 33). This works for include, exclude, and disabled masks alike, and for successive calls on the same or different clips, since each call replaces the mask with a fresh object.

I was not sure of anything; the type declarations and notes make the behavior unambiguous for this case.
