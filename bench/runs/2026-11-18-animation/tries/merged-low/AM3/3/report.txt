I added `src/skip-ahead.ts` which temporarily zeroes `fixedDeltaMs` so that `updateAnimationManager` honours the arbitrary `ms` you pass, then restores it in a `finally` block so the manager's fixed-step behaviour is unchanged on later frames. Everything the normal update handles—group time advancement (including loop wrapping and non-loop stop), task updates, and weight fades—is covered in that single call. I was not sure whether any consumer might rely on `fixedDeltaMs` being non-zero during the update itself (e.g. a custom `_preUpdate` that reads it), but given the library's own contract that the fixed step only affects the delta passed to tasks, this should be safe.
