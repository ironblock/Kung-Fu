I created `src/skip-ahead.ts` which temporarily zeroes `fixedDeltaMs` so that `updateAnimationManager` honours the passed `ms` value instead of the fixed step, calls it once, then restores the original fixed step in a `finally` block. This advances groups, tasks, and weight fades by exactly `ms` in a single pass, and leaves the manager's fixed-step behaviour untouched for subsequent frames. I'm not sure of anything— the type declarations and notes together make the fixed-step override (note 20) and the "advance-then-write" semantics (note 3) clear enough that this is the minimal correct approach.
