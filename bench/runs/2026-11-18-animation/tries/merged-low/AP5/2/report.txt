I wrote `src/beacon.ts` using a custom `AnimationTask` that accumulates elapsed time on each update, computes the linear rise/fall/hold envelope within the current period via modulo, and writes `lamp.intensity` scaled by the mutable strength. The returned closure simply updates the strength variable, which takes effect on the next update without disturbing the rhythm. I was not sure whether "more than 0" in the strength contract meant I should reject or clamp zero — I chose to clamp to `[0, 1]` so a zero value simply turns the beacon off rather than throwing.
