I registered a custom `AnimationTask` on the manager that tracks elapsed time, computes the phase within each period, and writes the appropriate linear ramp (rise 0→1 over 0.1 s, fall 1→0 over the next 0.1 s, flat 0 for the remainder) scaled by the current strength. The returned closure simply updates the strength variable, which takes effect on the next update without touching the clock, so the rhythm is unaffected. I'm fairly confident in the approach; the one thing I wasn't 100 % sure of is whether the spec intends the strength setter to hard-clamp at 0 (making the beacon permanently off) or to reject/ignore 0 — I chose to clamp to [0, 1] as the most forgiving interpretation.
