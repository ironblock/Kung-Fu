I wrote `src/beacon.ts` using a custom `AnimationTask` that accumulates elapsed time, computes the flash envelope (linear rise over 0.1 s, linear fall over 0.1 s, zero for the remainder) modulo the period, and writes the result scaled by a mutable strength captured in the closure. I was not sure whether `createAnimationTask` sets `active` to true by default or only `addAnimationTask` does, but since note 66 states that `addAnimationTask` sets it, the explicit call covers both cases.
