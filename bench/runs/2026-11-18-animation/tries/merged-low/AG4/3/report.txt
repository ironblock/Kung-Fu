I wrote `src/play-again.ts` with a two-line body: `stopAnimation` resets `currentTime` to the group's start (fromTime for property groups, 0 for glTF groups) and marks it stopped, then `playAnimation` clears that stop and begins playback at the group's existing `speedRatio`. This works for both property clips (which set `isPlaying` false at their end) and glTF clips (which keep `isPlaying` true but sit at the end), and it is repeatable because each press re-runs the same reset-and-play sequence. I was not unsure of anything; the notes make the behavior of both group kinds at their end explicit.
