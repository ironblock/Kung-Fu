I created `src/play-again.ts` with a `playAgain` function that calls `stopAnimation` (which resets `currentTime` to the group's start and sets the stopped flag) followed by `playAnimation` (which clears the stopped flag and resumes playback). This works for both property groups—where reaching the end sets `isPlaying` to false—and glTF groups—where reaching the end leaves `isPlaying` true but `currentTime` at the end—because `stopAnimation` unconditionally resets the time and `playAnimation` unconditionally clears the stop. The group's `speedRatio` is untouched, so it replays at its own speed. I'm not sure of anything here; the notes (especially 4, 7, 27, 39, and 53) make the behavior clear for both kinds.
