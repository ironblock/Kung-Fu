I wrote `src/hover.ts` as a custom animation task that accumulates elapsed time, computes a triangular-wave offset (linear rise to 0.5 over 0.5 s, linear fall back over 0.5 s), multiplies it by the current strength, and writes the result onto `node.position.y` each update. I had no uncertainty—the task-based approach sidesteps the blending/weight complexities in the notes and keeps each pickup's state (rest height, strength, phase) in its own closure so many can share one manager safely.
