I wrote `src/hover.ts` using a custom `AnimationTask` that accumulates elapsed time, computes a linear triangle wave over a 1-second period (rising to `0.5 × strength` above rest in the first half, falling back in the second), and writes the result to `node.position.y` each update. The strength setter mutates a closure variable, so changes take effect on the next update without restarting the timer, and each call to `startHover` adds its own independent task to the shared manager.
