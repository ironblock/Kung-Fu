I created `src/pose-mode.ts` which freezes a playing animation group by detaching it from its animation manager (so no further writes reach the nodes), and resumes by re-attaching it and calling `playAnimation` so it continues from its preserved `currentTime` at its own `speedRatio`. I was not sure whether `playAnimation` is strictly necessary on resume (the group's `isPlaying` flag should survive the detach/re-attach), but I included it as a safety measure to guarantee a clean playing state in case any internal stop flag was set.
