I created `src/pose-mode.ts` using `stopAnimation` to halt the group's writes (a stopped glTF group writes nothing on updates, unlike a paused one which keeps writing its pose), preserving `currentTime` so that `playAnimation` on resume continues from the frozen moment at the group's own `speedRatio`. I was not sure whether any other part of the scene (e.g. a second manager or the scene's own asset-container path) could also be driving the same group concurrently, but per the notes a group held by a manager is skipped by the scene, so a single `stopAnimation`/`playAnimation` pair should be sufficient.
