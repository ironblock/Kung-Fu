I created `src/jump-to-frame.ts` which calls `goToFrame` to seek the group to the given frame (converting frames to seconds via the group's frame rate) and then `playAnimation` to resume it, since `goToFrame` pauses the group as a side effect. Both glTF and property clips are handled correctly: looping groups wrap, non-looping groups clamp, and each resumes at its own `speedRatio`. I'm confident in this based on the notes (especially 6, 38, 49, 59), but I have no way to verify against the actual runtime, so if there's an edge case where `goToFrame` on a *playing* (rather than paused) group behaves differently than documented, that would be the one place I'd want to double-check.
