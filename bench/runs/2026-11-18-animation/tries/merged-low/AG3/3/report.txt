I created `src/jump-to-frame.ts` which calls `goToFrame` to seek the group to the given frame (the library converts it to seconds via the group's `frameRate`) and then `playAnimation` to resume playback, preserving the group's `speedRatio` and `loopAnimation` settings. I'm not aware of anything I'm unsure of — the notes confirm that `goToFrame` pauses and `playAnimation` resumes from the new time for both glTF and property groups.
