I wrote `src/release.ts` which resolves the dotted path to its owning object and final segment, then iterates every group on the manager checking `targetedAnimations` for a matching `(target, path)` pair and calls `stopAnimation` on any that match — stopping the whole group so it no longer writes the property (including under blending, per note 32). The only thing I wasn't fully sure of is whether `stopAnimation` is safe to call on a group that is already stopped; the notes imply it just resets `currentTime` and sets `_stopped`, so I assumed idempotency, but I couldn't verify the exact behavior on a double-stop.
