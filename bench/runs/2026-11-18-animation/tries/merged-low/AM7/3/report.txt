I wrote the JSON with the four values derived from the blending rules in the notes: when all groups on a bone have weight 1 the last-added group wins outright, and when any group has weight ≠ 1 the result is the unnormalized sum of value × weight. The "B at half" case doesn't change Lower because B's mask excludes that bone entirely. I was not fully certain that the same mixing rules (notes 12/44) apply identically to skeletal bone blending as to property blending, but note 25 (same slot) and the shared `AnimationPropertyMixStrategy` interface make that the most reasonable reading.
