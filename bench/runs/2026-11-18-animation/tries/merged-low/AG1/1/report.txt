I created `src/skip-to-end.ts` which sets `currentTime` to `duration` (the end of the clip) and then pauses the group. A paused glTF group still writes its pose at `currentTime` on every update (per your notes), so the model holds the last keyframe values on each rendered frame while `currentTime` stays at the end for the progress bar. I was not sure whether the modulo-wrap that note 10 describes for looping groups could affect a paused group's evaluation at exactly `duration`, but note 42 makes clear that at the last key's time the last value is returned, and the wrap only applies when time is *advanced* past the end—which a paused group never does.
