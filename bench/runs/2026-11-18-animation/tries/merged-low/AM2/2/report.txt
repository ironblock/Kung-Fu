I set the weight directly so it takes effect immediately, then called `fadeAnimationWeight` with `from` and `to` both equal to `share` and a 1 ms duration — this replaces any in-progress fade on the group (per the notes, a new fade on the same group replaces the old one) while keeping the weight constant at `share` for every subsequent frame, and leaves other groups' fades untouched. I'm slightly unsure whether the library's fade implementation might behave unexpectedly with a very short duration, but since `from === to` the interpolated value is `share` at every step regardless, so it should be safe.
