I created `src/set-share.ts`, which sets the group's weight immediately and then calls `fadeAnimationWeight` with `from` and `to` both equal to `share` and a 1 ms duration; this replaces any in-flight fade on the group (per the "new fade replaces old" rule) while keeping the weight pinned at `share` on every subsequent update, and a future fade on the same group will simply replace this no-op task and behave normally. I used `durationMs: 1` rather than `0` to avoid a potential `0 * Infinity → NaN` in the linear interpolation if the next update carries a zero delta. I was not sure whether the library clamps fade progress to [0, 1] before interpolating, which is the only reason I avoided `durationMs: 0`; if it does clamp, `0` would also be safe.
