I created `src/hand-over.ts` which implements the hand-over by calling `fadeAnimationWeight` on both groups: snapping `from` to weight 1 and `to` to weight 0 immediately (so the next frame shows `from` alone), then linearly fading them toward 0 and 1 respectively over `durationMs`. Because `fadeAnimationWeight` replaces any prior fade on the same group, re-calling `handOver` in the opposite direction mid-transition simply replaces the in-progress fades with new ones. I was not unsure of anything; the library's own fade utility matches the required linear, weight-summing-to-one behaviour exactly.
