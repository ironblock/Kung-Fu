I used `fadeAnimationWeight` to immediately pin `from` at weight 1 and `to` at weight 0, then linearly cross the weights over `durationMs`; because the manager already has property blending enabled, the two weighted values sum to exactly one share and a second call simply replaces the in-flight fades on both groups. I was not sure whether `durationMs` could ever be zero in practice, but the two fades would complete instantly in that case, which is consistent with the stated contract.
