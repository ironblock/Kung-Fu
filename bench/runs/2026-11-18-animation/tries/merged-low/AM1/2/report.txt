I created `src/hand-over.ts` which sets the two groups' weights to (1, 0) immediately and then registers a task on the manager that linearly shifts them toward (0, 1) over `durationMs`, removing itself when done; a module-level `WeakMap` tracks the active task per manager so a subsequent call cleanly supersedes the previous one. I'm not fully certain whether the property-mixer's "all weights are 1 → last wins" shortcut (note 44) could briefly bite if both groups happen to sit at exactly weight 1 at some intermediate instant, but since the weights move continuously from (1,0) to (0,1) they are never both 1 simultaneously, so it should be fine.
