I created `src/watched-manager.ts` which builds a standard manager via `createAnimationManager` and sets its `_preUpdate` hook to call `onStep` with the effective step (respecting `fixedDeltaMs` when it is set), so every `updateAnimationManager` call reports exactly once without interfering with tasks, blending, or weight fades. I was not fully certain that `_preUpdate` fires in the degenerate case where the passed delta is negative or non-finite and no `fixedDeltaMs` is set (the notes say the update "does nothing" but don't confirm whether the pre-hook still runs); if it doesn't, `onStep` would be skipped in that edge case.
