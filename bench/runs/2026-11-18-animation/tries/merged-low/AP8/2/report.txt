I wrote `notes/backward-door.json` with the four computed values. The key takeaway: a fresh backward group stops immediately (it starts at `fromTime` and one step goes past it), so you must seek to the end before playing to get a smooth close. I'm confident in all four values based on the notes; the one thing worth flagging is that `isPlaying` remaining `true` on a stopped non-looping backward group is a quirk of this API (notes 10 and 51 state it explicitly), so you can't rely on `isPlaying` alone to detect completion in that case.
