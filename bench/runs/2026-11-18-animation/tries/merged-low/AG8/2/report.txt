I derived the values from note 16, which states that `runFrameInterpolation` passes the frame delta in seconds to `step`, but clamps any delta of 0 or less to 1/60. So a 0 ms or −5 ms frame both yield 1/60 s (×1000 → 16.67), while 1 ms and 40 ms pass through as 0.001 s and 0.04 s (×1000 → 1.00 and 40.00). I'm confident in all four values; nothing was uncertain.
