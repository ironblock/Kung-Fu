I wrote `src/fade-in.ts` which builds a two-key linear clip (0 → 1 over `durationMs`) on the `alpha` path, then creates a non-looping property animation group on the given manager so it plays immediately and stops at the end (setting `isPlaying` to false). I was not sure whether `createPropertyAnimationClip` and `createPropertyAnimationGroup` are re-exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` root or only from a sub-path, but since the type declarations place them in the animation module of the package I assumed the root export is available.
