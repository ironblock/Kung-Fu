I wrote `/home/dev/8e8d7e/app/src/sun-shadows.ts`. `addSunShadows` adds the sun to the scene only if `scene.lights` doesn't already hold it, creates a PCF generator for it with `forceRefreshEveryFrame: true` so the shadow pass redraws it every frame, links it with `sun.shadowGenerator`, and returns it.

I could not check any of this against the library, since it isn't on this machine and I had nothing to run. Things I'm not sure of:

- **`scene.lights.includes(sun)`** is how I detect the sun is already in the scene. That relies on `scene.lights` (which recipe 12 mentions), and I'm not sure `addToScene` is safe to call twice.
