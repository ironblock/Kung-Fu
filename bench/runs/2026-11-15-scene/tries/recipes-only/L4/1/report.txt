I wrote `app/notes/sun-shadow-options.json` with all four set to `true`: `{ "normalBias": true, "bias": true, "darkness": true, "mapSize": true }`. I could not verify these against Babylon Lite 1.39.0, because the library isn't on this machine and Bash is disabled here. They come from my knowledge of Babylon.js's shadow generator, plus one hint in the recipes (`setShadowGeneratorEnabled` turns off the shadow pass and sets darkness to 1, which suggests darkness is sent to the GPU). `darkness` is the least certain of the four, since Lite may differ from full Babylon.js, and the file has no room for caveats. Check these against the Lite source or a quick test before you commit the sliders.
