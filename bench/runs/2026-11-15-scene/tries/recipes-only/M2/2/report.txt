I added `src/group.ts`, which creates a `TransformNode` at the origin and parents each part to it with `setParent`. That follows recipe 4 in `scene-recipes.md`, which says this keeps each part's world position and adds it to the node's `children`, so adding, hiding and moving the node all carry the parts with them. I could not check this against the library, since it isn't on the machine, and I did not compile or run anything.

Things I'm not sure of:
- I relied on the recipe for the signatures of `createTransformNode(name, x, y, z)` and `setParent(child, parent)`, and for the claim that `setMeshVisible` works on a transform node.
- I did not check whether `Mesh` and `TransformNode` are exported as types from `@babylonjs/lite` in exactly the way the import line assumes.
