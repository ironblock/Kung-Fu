I added `/home/dev/42f484/app/src/group.ts`, which exports `groupParts`. It creates an identity transform node with the given name and parents each part to it with `setParent`, the call the recipe uses. Moving, scaling or hiding the group then carries the parts, and `addToScene` on the group adds them too.

- I'm relying on the recipe's claim that `setParent` keeps world position. With an identity group that shouldn't move anything, but I haven't confirmed it.
