I wrote `app/src/terrain-sun.ts`. It creates the sun at intensity 1.3 along the normalised `direction`, adds it to the scene, and attaches a 2048-texel PCF shadow generator. The depth range is set so the whole valley sphere (radius about 1428) sits in front of the light's origin for any direction. I could not run or type-check it, since the library isn't installed here.

I'm not sure of several things:
- **Width of the shadow map:** the declarations don't show how the map's width and height are chosen. I think they come from the caster meshes, not the valley. If so, the valley is only covered as far as the casters reach, and an explicit valley-sized footprint may be needed.
- **Depth semantics:** I assumed `orthoMinZ`/`orthoMaxZ` are distances from the light's position along its direction. That is why I moved the light's origin behind the valley centre.
- **Imports:** I assumed `addToScene`, `createDirectionalLight` and `createPcfDirectionalShadowGenerator` are exported from the package root. The declarations only show their module paths.
- **Casters:** `setShadowTaskCasterMeshes` appears in the recipes but not in the declarations, so I did not touch it.
