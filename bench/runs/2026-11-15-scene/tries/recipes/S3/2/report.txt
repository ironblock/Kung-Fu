I wrote `app/src/terrain-sun.ts`, which exports `createValleySun`. It creates a directional light with intensity 1.3, positions it behind the valley centre along the reversed direction, adds it to the scene, and attaches a 2048-texel PCF shadow generator. The light sits far enough back that the whole valley box (x and z in ±1000, y in 0 to 400) falls inside the depth range, so any caster in the valley can reach the map. I haven't compiled or run it, since the library isn't on this machine.

Things I'm not sure of:

- **Imports:** I import `addToScene`, `createDirectionalLight` and `createPcfDirectionalShadowGenerator` from `"@babylonjs/lite"`. The declarations I read don't include the package index, so I haven't confirmed these are exported from the root.
- **Casting setup:** I didn't call `setShadowTaskCasterMeshes`, which the recipes use. It isn't in the declarations I read, and you said the caller sets casters.
