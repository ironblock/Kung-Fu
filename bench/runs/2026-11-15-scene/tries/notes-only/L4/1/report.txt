I wrote `notes/sun-shadow-options.json` with all four set to `true`: `normalBias`, `bias`, `darkness` and `mapSize` each change something the library computes or uploads for the shadow. The library source isn't on this machine and `scene-notes.md` doesn't cover these four options, so these answers come from general Babylon shadow-generator behaviour and haven't been checked against Babylon Lite 1.39.0. Before you commit to sliders, it's worth confirming against the library's PCF generator code, or by changing each slider and seeing whether the shadow visibly changes.
