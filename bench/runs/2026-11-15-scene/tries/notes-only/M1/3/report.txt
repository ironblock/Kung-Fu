I added `src/systems.ts` in `/home/dev/7c611e/app`. `addSystem` keeps a list of systems per scene, and registers one `onBeforeRender` callback on that scene the first time it is used. That callback calls the list in order, so systems run first-added-first, including ones added later, and each scene runs only its own systems.

I could not check this against the real library. The app has no Babylon Lite package on disk, and I had no shell to type-check or run it. The call `scene.onBeforeRender(cb)` and the callback's `deltaMs` argument are my guesses from the wording of note 15, so they may need adjusting to the actual 1.39.0 API. I also made a choice on your behalf: a system added during a frame first runs on the next frame.
