I wrote `src/terrain-sun.ts`, which places the sun about 1,528 units back along `-direction` so the whole valley lies inside the default 1..10000 shadow depth range, then creates the 2048-texel PCF generator and adds the light to the scene. I could not compile or run it: the library isn't on this machine, and `app/src` and `babylon-lite` aren't readable, so the factory signatures (`createDirectionalLight`, `createPcfDirectionalShadowGenerator`, `.position.set`, `.direction.set`) are my guesses from the notes and need checking against the real API. Also, the scene must be registered with `registerSceneWithShadowSupport` (note 3), and casters must be given via `setShadowTaskCasterMeshes`. Thin-instanced casters whose instances spread beyond their prototype's bounds will still fall off the map unless their `boundMin`/`boundMax` are widened (note 16), which this function can't fix.
