I wrote `src/ramp.ts`. `createRamp` builds the wedge from five faces, each with its own vertices and outward normal (18 vertices, 8 triangles), creates the mesh with `createMeshFromData`, adds it with `addToScene`, and returns it. It also throws a `RangeError` if any dimension isn't a positive number, since a zero or negative size would give broken normals or an inside-out mesh. I couldn't compile or run anything; I checked each face's corner order and normal by hand.

What I'm not sure of is the triangle winding. The declarations don't say which winding the renderer treats as the outside of a face, and the box's vertex data isn't in them. I followed Babylon.js's own box, where each triangle's corners run the opposite way to its normal. If Babylon Lite changed that, the ramp will look inside-out. The fix is to swap the last two indices in the single `indices.push(...)` line, which has a comment on it.
