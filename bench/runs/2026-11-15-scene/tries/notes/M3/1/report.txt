I wrote `/home/dev/7e12b4/app/src/remove-keep.ts`. It detaches each direct child from the mesh with `setParent(child, null)`, then calls `removeFromScene(scene, mesh)`. I did this because the notes (item 10) say `removeFromScene` also removes everything in `children`, so the children have to be emptied first. Children keep their world transform, and since they were never removed from `scene.meshes`, each stays there once.
