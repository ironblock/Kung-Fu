I wrote `src/remove-keep.ts`. It detaches each child of the mesh with `setParent(child, null)`, then calls `removeFromScene` on the mesh. I detach first because the notes say `removeFromScene` also removes a mesh's children, and the children are never re-added, so they stay in `scene.meshes` exactly once.

I could not verify several things, since the library isn't on this machine and nothing was run:
- I assumed `setParent(child, null)` removes the child from `mesh.children` and keeps its world transform. The code throws before removing the mesh if any child is still attached, so a wrong assumption fails loudly rather than deleting the children.
- I assumed `removeFromScene` and `setParent` are exported from the package root, which the declarations don't confirm.
- Nothing was compiled or tested.
