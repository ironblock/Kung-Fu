I created `/home/dev/6dbdff/app/src/systems.ts`. `addSystem` keeps a per-scene list (in a `WeakMap`) and registers one `onBeforeRender` dispatcher per scene, which calls the systems in the order they were added. This is needed because your notes say `onBeforeRender` runs callbacks newest first, so registering each system directly would reverse the order.
