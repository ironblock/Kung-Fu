I wrote `src/sun-shadows.ts`. `addSunShadows` creates a 2048 PCF directional shadow generator for the sun, sets it as the sun's `shadowGenerator`, adds the sun to the scene only if it isn't already in `scene.lights`, and returns the generator. If the scene is already built, it also calls `rebuildSceneRenderables` (not awaited) so receivers pick up the shadow. Nothing was compiled or run, since the library isn't on this machine.
