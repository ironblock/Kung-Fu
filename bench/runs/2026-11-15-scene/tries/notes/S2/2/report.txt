Things I'm not sure of:

- **Shadow pass:** The scene has to be registered with `registerSceneWithShadowSupport`, not `registerScene`, or there is no shadow pass at all. I did not change your registration code.
- **Shadow frustum:** Per your notes, a directional PCF map only keeps casters between `orthoMinZ` and `orthoMaxZ` (defaults 1 and 10000) measured from the light's position, which defaults to the origin. If the courtyard sits outside that range, casters will be clipped. I left those at their defaults because you didn't specify them.
