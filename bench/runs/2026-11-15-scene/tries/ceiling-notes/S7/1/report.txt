I read the type declarations and the notes, then wrote `notes/shadow-redraws.json` with: 1 render over the first three frames, redrawn after the direction change (true), not after the intensity change (false), and not after the in-place matrix edit (false). The PCF generator keeps a copy of the casters' versions and the light's version, and only redraws when one changes. A direction change changes the light's version. Writing straight into the matrix array changes nothing, because nothing is sent to the GPU until `flushThinInstances` (note 18).

The one answer I'm not sure of is the intensity change. A plain `sun.intensity = …` write changes nothing the generator checks, so the map is not redrawn. But `setLightIntensity` most likely bumps the light's version, and the map may then be redrawn even though nothing in it changed. I couldn't confirm which happens without the library. The quickest check is to wrap `sg._renderShadowMap` with a counter and try both.
