I added `src/lamps.ts` with `addLamp`. It creates the warm lamp (colour `[1, 0.85, 0.6]`, intensity 1, range `reach`) with `createClusteredPointLight`, appends it to `lamps.pointLights` if it isn't already there, marks the container dirty, and returns the lamp. Existing lamps keep their indices because nothing is reordered.
