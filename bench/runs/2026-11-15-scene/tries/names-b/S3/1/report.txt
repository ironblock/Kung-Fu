I wrote `src/terrain-sun.ts` in `/home/dev/51e965/app`. It creates a 1.3-intensity directional light along `direction`, adds it to the scene, and attaches a 2048-texel PCF shadow generator with a depth range of ±2857 units (the valley's 3D diagonal), so every valley point is covered whatever the light direction.

I'm not sure of these points, since I couldn't inspect the library source:
- **Lateral coverage:** the type declarations don't show how the shadow map's x/y extent is fitted. If it's fitted to the caster meshes only, ground points far from any caster may fall outside the map.
- **Depth origin:** I don't know where the library places the shadow camera, so the symmetric ±diagonal range is a conservative guess rather than a verified fit.
- **Imports:** I assumed `createDirectionalLight`, `addToScene` and `createPcfDirectionalShadowGenerator` are exported from the package root.
- **Scene registration:** I only add the light to the scene explicitly. I assumed the generator registers itself, but I didn't confirm that.
