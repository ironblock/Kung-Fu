It builds the five flat faces with 18 unshared vertices, orients each triangle against its outward normal, creates the mesh with `createMeshFromData`, adds it with `addToScene`, and returns it. Two things I could not confirm:
- **Winding convention:** the declarations don't state it. I assumed Babylon's usual one, where a front face's `(v1 - v0) × (v2 - v0)` points out of the face. If your box renders inside-out, that assumption is wrong, and the fix is to flip the test in `addFace`.
- **Imports:** I import `createMeshFromData` and `addToScene` from the package root, but the declarations only show module-internal paths. If the root doesn't re-export them, the import path needs changing.
