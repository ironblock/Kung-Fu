I wrote `app/src/group.ts`. `groupParts` creates a `TransformNode` at the origin with identity rotation and unit scale, then calls `setParent` on each part so the parts stay where they are and follow the group.

I'm not sure of three things, and I couldn't run any of it because the library isn't here:
- **Adding the group to a scene:** `addToScene` takes a `SceneEntity`, and the declarations don't say whether it recurses into children. If it doesn't, the parts won't render unless they're added too.
- **Root exports:** `createTransformNode` and `setParent` aren't re-exported from `scene.d.ts`, so I've assumed the package root exports them. If it doesn't, the import will fail.
- **Hiding:** I took the "hide with everything under it" call to be `setMeshVisible`, which is `setSubtreeVisible` re-exported from `scene.d.ts`. I haven't checked how it behaves at runtime.
