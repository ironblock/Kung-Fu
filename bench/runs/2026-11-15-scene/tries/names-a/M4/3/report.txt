I'm not sure of these points:

- **Imports:** `addToScene`, `initSceneNodeTransform` and `setParent` are imported from `"@babylonjs/lite"`. `setParent` is not in the scene barrel I read, so it may need a deeper import path.
- **`setParent` side effects:** I assumed it keeps the local transform I set. If it rebases to world space, the copy will land in the wrong place.
- **Scale:** `dx` is in the shelf's local units. If the shelf is scaled, the world distance is `dx` times its x scale.
- **Unsupported books:** skinned, morph-target, VAT-animated or thin-instanced books throw. Shadow-caster registration for the copy is not handled.
