I wrote `app/src/terrain-sun.ts`. It creates a 1.3-intensity directional light along the normalised `direction` and a 2048-texel PCF shadow generator. The light is placed one valley bounding-sphere radius (about 1429 units, plus a 1-unit margin) upstream of the valley centre, and the shadow depth range is set to cover that sphere, so the whole valley fits in depth regardless of light direction. The function throws if the direction is zero-length or not downward.

I could not check any of this against Babylon Lite itself, so please verify these before relying on it:
- **Depth frame:** I assumed `orthoMinZ`/`orthoMaxZ` are measured from `light.position`. If the library measures from somewhere else, the valley could be clipped.
- **Light position:** I assumed the library honours the `position` I set, and that `ObservableVec3` has a `set(x, y, z)` method. I did not see its declaration.
- **Lateral coverage:** the PCF config has no width/height option, so I'm relying on the library fitting the shadow window to the casters. If it uses a fixed window, casters near the valley edge could still fall outside it.
- **Imports:** I import `addToScene`, `createDirectionalLight` and `createPcfDirectionalShadowGenerator` from `@babylonjs/lite`. Your declarations show them in the internal modules, not the package entry point, so the exports may differ. I also assumed `addToScene` registers the shadow generator and that setting `sun.shadowGenerator` is needed.
- **Bias:** with a depth range of about 2860 units, the default bias may need tuning to avoid acne or peter-panning.
