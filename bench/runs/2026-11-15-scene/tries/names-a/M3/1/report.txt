I wrote `src/remove-keep.ts` in `/home/dev/5b6dbe/app`. It exports `removeKeepingAttached`, which unparents each child of the mesh, sets the child's position, rotation and scale from its world matrix so it stays where it was, and then calls `removeFromScene` on the mesh. The children are detached first so the removal can't take them along, and they are not re-added to `scene.meshes`.
