I wrote `/home/dev/ecc912/app/src/group.ts`. It creates the node with `createTransformNode(name)` at the origin, with no rotation and a scale of 1, and attaches each part with `setParent(part, group)`. Since the node sits at the origin and `setParent` keeps each part's world position, no part moves. `setParent` also adds each part to `group.children`, which is what makes adding the node to a scene and `setMeshVisible(group, false)` reach every part. Recipe 4 warns that setting only `part.parent` misses this, and pushing onto `children` alone leaves `part.parent` unset until the node is added to a scene.
