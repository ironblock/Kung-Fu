I wrote `/home/dev/3ea7c6/app/src/crate-field.ts`. It builds one 1-unit box with a standard material, because a mesh without a material is never drawn. It gives the box one unrotated thin instance per centre, in the order given, and calls `enableThinInstanceWorldBounds` before adding the mesh to the scene and returning it. Without that call, the mesh's bounds would be just the single box at the origin, and the shadow map would be fitted only around that.
