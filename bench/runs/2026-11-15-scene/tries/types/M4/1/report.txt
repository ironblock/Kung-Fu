I wrote `src/duplicate.ts` in `/home/dev/36e027/app`. It clones the book, parents the copy to the book's shelf, copies the book's local position, turn and scale, adds `dx` to the local x position, and adds the copy to the scene. I have not compiled or run it, since the library isn't on this machine.

Things I'm not sure of:
- **Units:** `dx` is in the shelf's local units. If the shelf is scaled, the world distance is `dx` times that scale.
- **Writable fields:** I assumed the `ObservableVec3` and `ObservableQuat` components (`x`, `y`, `z`, `w`) can be assigned directly. Their declarations aren't in `scene-api.d.ts`.
- **Imports:** I assumed the package root exports `addToScene`, `cloneTransformNode` and `setParent` as values. I could only confirm them in the scene module declarations.
- **Matrix-driven books:** if a book's transform comes from a raw matrix (a glTF `matrix` node), its position, rotation and scale may be stale, and the copy would inherit them.
- **Name:** I named the copy `<book name>_copy`. Change it if you want something else.
