# MEND

Each checker case was reproduced by a new probe case before the note was changed; the checker was right in all 15. None dropped. Items marked holds were left as they were.

- n35 (dropped the claim that builders run only once at registerScene (a mesh of a new material family added later is built at runtime)). Was: "Material group builders run once, at registerScene. A later addToScene(light), light.shadowGenerator = sg or receiveShadows = true re-runs none of them, even after unregisterScene/registerScene; call rebuildSceneRenderables(scene)." Now: "A later addToScene(light), light.shadowGenerator = sg or receiveShadows = true re-runs no material group builder that already ran, even after unregisterScene/registerScene; call rebuildSceneRenderables(scene)."
- n02 (an empty caster list counts as given). Was: "A create*ShadowGenerator result is used only once light.shadowGenerator = sg, the light is in scene.lights and setShadowTaskCasterMeshes(sg, meshes) gave casters. addToScene(scene, sg) adds it to nothing, not even scene.shadowGenerators." Now: "A create*ShadowGenerator result renders only once light.shadowGenerator = sg, its light is in scene.lights and setShadowTaskCasterMeshes(sg, list) ran, even with []. addToScene(scene, sg) adds it to nothing, not even scene.shadowGenerators."
- n01 (a re-add before teardown keeps its buffers when a clone shares the geometry). Was: "removeFromScene of a mesh from its last scene queues its disposal (waitForGpuResourceRetirements awaits it); after it, addToScene and cloneTransformNode throw, and a re-add made before it still loses its buffers. Hide with setMeshVisible." Now: "removeFromScene from a mesh's last scene queues its disposal (waitForGpuResourceRetirements awaits it); then addToScene and cloneTransformNode throw, and an early re-add loses its buffers unless a clone shares them. Hide: setMeshVisible."
- n07 (the UBO refill is triggered by any light's version bump or a light added/removed, not only this light's). Was: "Plain writes to a light's intensity, diffuse, specular, range or exponent are not uploaded until something bumps its version: setLightIntensity, setLightDiffuseColor, position/direction set, or a spot angle write." Now: "Plain writes to a light's intensity, diffuse, specular, range or exponent wait for the next lights UBO refill: setLightIntensity, setLightDiffuseColor, position/direction set or spot angle write on any light, or a light added or removed."
- n04 (setParent does not touch p.children when child.parent was already p). Was: "node.parent = p keeps the local transform and leaves both children arrays alone, so addToScene, setMeshVisible and cloneTransformNode on p skip that child. setParent(child, p) moves it into p.children and keeps its world transform." Now: "node.parent = p keeps the local transform and changes no children array, so addToScene, setMeshVisible and cloneTransformNode on p skip that child. setParent(child, p) adds it to p.children, keeping its world transform, unless parent was p."
- n22 (depth starts at the light's world position, which differs from light.position under a parent). Was: "computeDirectionalLightMatrix spans depth orthoMinZ..orthoMaxZ (defaults 1..10000) ahead of light.position along direction; position starts at [0,0,0], so casters behind it or within 1 unit are outside. X/Y fit casters +10%." Now: "computeDirectionalLightMatrix spans depth orthoMinZ..orthoMaxZ (default 1..10000) ahead of the light's world position along direction (if unparented, light.position: [0,0,0]); casters behind it or within 1 are out. X/Y fit casters +10%."
- n11 (after a first setFog, a new scene.fog object is written, and an in-place edit is written once the camera or aspect changes). Was: "Assigning scene.fog writes no fog uniforms; setFog(scene, config) does. Editing that fog object in place, even with a second setFog of the same object, is not rewritten; call setFog with a new object." Now: "Fog uniforms are written only after a first setFog(scene, config); before it, assigning scene.fog writes nothing. After it, a new scene.fog object is written at once, but an in-place edit waits until the camera or aspect changes."
- n28 (named the shape factories; createLines takes a name and has a material). Was: "Mesh factories take no name: mesh.name is the shape ("box", "sphere", "ground", "plane", ...), mesh.id is undefined, material is null and receiveShadows false. boundMin/boundMax are local and ignore position and scaling." Now: "createBox, createSphere, createGround, createPlane and other shape factories take no name: mesh.name is the shape, id undefined, material null, receiveShadows false (not so createLines). boundMin/boundMax are local, ignoring transforms."
- n10 (a non-empty includedOnlyMeshes takes over from includedOnlyMeshIds). Was: "A mesh with no id (every factory mesh) is never excluded by excludedMeshIds, and is unlit by a light whose includedOnlyMeshIds is non-empty; set mesh.id or use excludedMeshes/includedOnlyMeshes." Now: "A mesh with no id (every factory mesh) is never excluded by excludedMeshIds, and while includedOnlyMeshes is empty it is unlit by a light whose includedOnlyMeshIds is non-empty; set mesh.id or use excludedMeshes/includedOnlyMeshes."
- n18 (at capacity 0 addThinInstance throws RangeError instead of growing). Was: "setThinInstances and setThinInstanceColors keep the arrays passed, not copies; capacity is count. addThinInstance past capacity moves to a new doubled array (first one: 16), so later writes to your array are lost." Now: "setThinInstances and setThinInstanceColors keep the passed arrays; capacity is count. addThinInstance past capacity moves to a new doubled array (first: 16), so later writes to your array are lost; at capacity 0 it throws RangeError."
- n19 (setThinInstanceMatrix marks only its own slot, so it does not flush other direct writes). Was: "Writes made straight into thinInstances.matrices are not uploaded until flushThinInstances(mesh) or another thin-instance setter runs; setThinInstanceMatrix needs no flush." Now: "Writes made straight into thinInstances.matrices are not uploaded until flushThinInstances(mesh). setThinInstanceMatrix(mesh, i, m) needs no flush but marks only slot i, so direct writes to other slots stay stale."
- n27 (removal does not dispose the generator; its task resources go at the next rebuildSceneRenderables or disposeScene). Was: "removeFromScene of a light disposes its shadow generator and sets light.shadowGenerator to undefined; re-adding the light brings no shadow back. removeFromScene(sg) unlinks it from its light too." Now: "removeFromScene of a light, or of its generator, sets light.shadowGenerator to undefined; re-adding the light brings no shadow back. The generator's task resources are disposed only at the next rebuildSceneRenderables or disposeScene."
- n31 (also uploaded by direction.set or any light's refill trigger, or a light added). Was: "HemisphericLight colors are diffuseColor, specularColor and groundColor (default [0,0,0], direction [0,1,0]); a write to them is uploaded only after light._bumpLightVersion() or setLightIntensity." Now: "HemisphericLight colors are diffuseColor, specularColor, groundColor (default [0,0,0]; direction [0,1,0]); writes to them wait for a lights UBO refill: _bumpLightVersion, setLightIntensity or direction.set on any light, or a light added."
- n29 (throws a TypeError when the subtree holds a camera). Was: "cloneTransformNode names each copy name + "_clone", deep-clones children parented to the copy, shares the mesh's GPU geometry, and adds nothing to any scene." Now: "cloneTransformNode names each copy name + "_clone", deep-clones children parented to the copy, shares the mesh's GPU geometry, and adds nothing to any scene. It throws a TypeError if the subtree holds a camera."
- n20 (RangeError is checked first and always; an unchanged count never throws; a later write makes it throw again until synced). Was: "setThinInstanceCount does no checks: it ignores a mesh with no thin instances and accepts a count above capacity. setThinInstanceDrawCount throws until a first GPU sync, and a RangeError above capacity." Now: "setThinInstanceCount checks nothing: it ignores a mesh with no thin instances, accepts a count above capacity. setThinInstanceDrawCount throws RangeError above capacity and, for a changed count, an Error until all writes are GPU-synced."

Dropped: none.
