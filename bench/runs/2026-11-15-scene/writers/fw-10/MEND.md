# Mended recipes

All four checker cases were reproduced by a test before changing anything (cases added to probes r01, r03, r13, r14). None were dropped; nothing marked "holds" was touched.

- r01: without[0] said "Without a material (no factory assigns one), ... nothing is built"; now it says the shape factories (createBox, createSphere, createGround, ...) assign none, while createLines and the other line factories assign a line material themselves. Probe: sphere/ground material is null, createLines material is set and the line is built with no material assigned.
- r03: without[0] said casters "around the origin" get depth below 0 when the light is left at the origin, and the step said only "back along the light"; now the maps start 1 unit in front of the light's position, so any caster behind it or under 1 unit in front is left out, and the step says to place the light back along its direction beyond every caster. Probe: light at y=50 drops a caster at y=60 (depth < 0), at y=61 still drops it (0.5 unit in front), at y=100 holds both.
- r13: without[0] said a runtime change "has no effect on a mesh until its world matrix changes" (and the step comment said only "also move the mesh"); now: "until its world matrix changes or a light is added to or removed from the scene", step comment "also move the mesh, or add/remove a light". Probe: adding a light, and removing one, applied a runtime excludedMeshes change with no move.
- r14: the step comment said "the map is redrawn only when a caster or the light moves"; now "the map is not redrawn while nothing changes", and a new without sentence says a caster's world-matrix or thin-instance change, a light position/direction .set(), setLightIntensity or setLightDiffuseColor redraws it, a plain sun.intensity write does not. Probe: each of those cases.
