I added `src/fly-controls.ts` as a thin wrapper around the library’s configurable free camera controls, setting Space/PageDown as up/down keys and Shift left/right as fast keys with a 3× multiplier, and returning the library’s dispose function. I was not completely sure about the exact key-name normalization used by `attachConfigurableFreeControl`, so I included both `event.key` and `event.code` spellings, and I assumed the configurable options replace the default vertical/fast key mappings rather than merge them.
