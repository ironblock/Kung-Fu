I added `app/src/label-position.ts` using Babylon Lite’s camera matrix helpers and `projectWorldToScreen` to compute CSS-pixel label positions, marking points visible only when they are in front of the camera and inside the viewport/picture while ignoring far-plane clipping. I am not sure whether all of these helpers are exported from the package root or whether `worldOrigin`/floating-origin handling should be passed explicitly, so I omitted `worldOrigin` and relied on `projectWorldToScreen`’s `behindCamera` and `offscreen` flags.
