I added `app/src/fast-orbit.ts`, which calls the library's `attachControl` with keyboard controls disabled after halving `camera.angularSensibility`, leaving `wheelPrecision` untouched so drag and inertial glide are twice as far, and restores the original sensibility when the returned function is called. I was unsure whether Lite treats `angularSensibility` as inverse sensitivity like BabylonJS, whether keyboard controls should be disabled or included, and whether `attachControl` is exported from the root `@babylonjs/lite` package; if any of those assumptions are wrong, the helper, options, or import path may need a small adjustment.
