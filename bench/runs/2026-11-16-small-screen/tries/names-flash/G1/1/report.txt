I added `src/mode-limits.ts` with per-camera wrappers that clamp `radius` and `yaw` writes to the active mode limits, keep `camera.limits` reporting the active mode object, and also wrap `_setOrientation` when present so internal orientation writes are clamped too. I was not completely sure whether some camera properties could be non-configurable/frozen or whether yaw ranges are intended to wrap across ±π; I assumed they are radians and handled wrap-around headings as a circular interval.
