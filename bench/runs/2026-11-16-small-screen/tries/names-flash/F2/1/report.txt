I added `src/walkthrough.ts` that creates a `FreeCamera`, sets it as `scene.camera`, attaches the library’s usual free-camera controls, and implements `jumpTo` by updating the camera position/target and refreshing its internal `_yaw`/`_pitch` so keyboard and mouse controls continue from the new viewpoint; `dispose` calls the detach function returned by `attachFreeControl`. I was not sure of the exact internal yaw/pitch convention without the runtime library, so `jumpTo` asks `createFreeCamera` to compute the orientation via a temporary camera and falls back to a standard Y-up `atan2` computation if that is unavailable.
