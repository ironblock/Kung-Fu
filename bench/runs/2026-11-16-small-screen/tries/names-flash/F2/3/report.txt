I wrote `src/walkthrough.ts` to create a `FreeCamera`, set it as `scene.camera`, attach the library’s free-camera controls, and re-attach those controls after each `jumpTo` so keyboard/mouse behavior continues from the new viewpoint; `dispose` detaches the controls without changing the camera itself. I was not certain whether `attachFreeControl` or `attachConfigurableFreeControl` is the better choice for “usual keyboard and mouse controls,” and I used the internal `_yaw`/`_pitch` fields defensively because the control internals are not visible.
