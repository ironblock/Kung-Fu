I wrote `app/notes/tighter-zoom.json` with the inferred values: changing `limits.radiusMax` alone does not move the camera, and direct `radius`/`center` self-assignments do not reapply the limit, while `setGeospatialOrientation(camera, {})` re-applies the current orientation under the new limits and pulls `radius` down to `2000`. I was not fully certain about the exact runtime behavior of the property setters because the `.d.ts` declarations do not encode whether they clamp or reapply limits, so I relied on the library’s orientation helper being the mechanism that applies limits.
