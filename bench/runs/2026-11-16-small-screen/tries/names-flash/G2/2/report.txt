I added `src/center-on.ts` that converts latitude/longitude degrees to a point on the planet sphere and updates the camera centre while keeping the existing yaw, pitch, and radius unchanged via `_setOrientation`. I was not completely sure about the library’s internal handedness/axis ordering because only the camera type declarations were available, so I used the common Y-up convention where lat/lon 0 is on +X, north is +Y, and east is +Z.
