I added `src/center-on.ts` to convert latitude/longitude degrees into a surface point using the camera’s planet radius, then applied that new centre with `_setOrientation` while preserving the existing yaw, pitch, and radius. I was not certain about the exact Cartesian axis convention because the declarations do not explicitly state where lat/lon 0,0 is placed; I assumed the default frame has lat/lon 0,0 at +Z with north +Y and east +X.
