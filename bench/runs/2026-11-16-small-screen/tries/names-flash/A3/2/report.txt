I wrote `src/screen-shift.ts` to snapshot the view/view-projection matrices plus viewport/CSS dimensions on `mark()`, then use `projectWorldToScreen` to compute current-minus-marked CSS pixel offsets. I was unsure how best to supply `worldOrigin` for `_useFloatingOrigin` cameras and how much to rely on the CSS/backing viewport conventions, so I used hidden origin fields/camera position as a best guess and defensively fall back if helper functions or matrix caches are unavailable.
