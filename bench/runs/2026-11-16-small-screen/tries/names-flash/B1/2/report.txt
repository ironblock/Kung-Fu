I wrote `src/limited-orbit.ts` to attach Babylon Lite’s normal arc-rotate controls, set radius limits through `setCameraLimits`, and add a cancellable per-frame clamp using `requestAnimationFrame` plus any safely removable render observables I could detect. I was not fully sure how `SceneContext` exposes its render loop in Babylon Lite 1.39.0, so I made the clamp defensive rather than depending on a specific scene-only API.
