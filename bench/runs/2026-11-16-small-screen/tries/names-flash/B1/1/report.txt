I added `src/limited-orbit.ts`, composing `attachControl` for the library’s mouse/touch controls with `setCameraLimits(..., scene)` for per-frame radius clamping, plus an immediate radius clamp and a disposer that removes the controls, the frame clamp, and restores the previous radius limits. I was not fully sure whether `setCameraLimits` already performs immediate clamping or restores previous limits, so I explicitly set the limits, call `_clampToLimits` when available, clamp manually, and restore previous limits in cleanup.
