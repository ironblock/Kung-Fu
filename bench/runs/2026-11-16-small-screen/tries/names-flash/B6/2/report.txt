I added `src/chase-camera.ts` using `createBankedFreeCamera` and its `upVector` observable, rolling the camera by setting `upVector` to the normalized projection of the car’s up onto the plane perpendicular to the current target-eye direction, while preserving the previous roll when the requested up is parallel or anti-parallel to the line of sight. I was not fully sure about Babylon Lite’s exact `upVector` mutation API and whether `upVector` is meant to hold the raw supplied up or the already-orthonormalized camera up, so I wrote the updater defensively and used perpendicular projections to match the visible camera up as closely as possible.
