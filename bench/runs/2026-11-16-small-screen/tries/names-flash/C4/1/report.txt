I wrote `src/turntable.ts` using Babylon Lite’s `interpolateArcRotateCamera` to smoothly animate `alpha` forward by `2π · turns` while preserving `beta`, `radius`, and `target`, temporarily disabling alpha limits and inertia so the transition cannot clamp or jump. I was not completely sure how the library handles negative `turns` or whether `interpolateArcRotateCamera` might choose a shortest angular path for large turns, so I assumed numeric positive interpolation and treated negative `turns` as magnitudes.
