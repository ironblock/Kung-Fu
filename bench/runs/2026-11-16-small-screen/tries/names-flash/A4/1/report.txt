I wrote `app/notes/over-the-top.json` using the standard Babylon arc-rotate spherical convention inferred from the declarations: `beta` is measured from the +Y direction, and the camera looks at `target` with world up. Under that convention, `beta = -0.5` puts the eye at `(-4.794..., 8.775..., 0)`, keeps the world up direction upright in the picture, and makes the view identical to a fresh `alpha = Math.PI, beta = 0.5` camera. Since the actual `@babylonjs/lite` implementation is not available, the remaining uncertainty is whether Lite has hidden pole/up-vector logic that diverges from this standard convention.
