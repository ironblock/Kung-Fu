I added `src/globe-tilt.ts` so it preserves the camera’s yaw, radius, and center, widens/clears pitch limits before using `_setOrientation` to apply the requested tilt at the current zoom. I was unsure about the exact runtime semantics of `pitchDisabledRadiusScale` and `_lookAt` from the declarations alone, so I used a best-effort approach that makes the pitch allowed at the initial fully zoomed-out radius and keeps it allowed during later zoom changes.
