I added `src/fly-to-place.ts` using the library’s `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` animation, then resolving `true` only if the camera orientation matches the requested place and `false` if the flight was canceled or interrupted. I’m not fully sure how consistently Babylon Lite calls the optional private `_cancelFly` hook for cancellations initiated outside this module, so I both track this module’s active flights and hook `_cancelFly` when possible, while also falling back to a final orientation check.
