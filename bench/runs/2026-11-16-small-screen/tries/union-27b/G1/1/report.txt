I wrote `src/mode-limits.ts`, where `useModeLimits` assigns the mode's `GeospatialLimits` to `camera.limits` (which, per note 37, makes the camera clamp every subsequent radius/yaw write to that mode's ranges and report them) and additionally clamps the camera's current radius and yaw into the new range, since a limits change only bites on a value-changing write and the pose would otherwise sit outside the new mode's limits until the app's next write.

Two things I was not fully sure of: I assumed each mode's heading range is given as a plain `[yawMin, yawMax]` within `[-PI, PI]` (yaw reads back wrapped per note 54), so I clamp with a simple min/max — a range that straddles the ±PI wrap point would not be handled, and the library's own clamp presumably wouldn't either; and I assumed snapping the current pose into the new range on switch is wanted (the spec only mandates clamping of writes) — if you'd rather the app animate in, the two clamp blocks can be dropped, leaving just the `camera.limits = limits` assignment.
