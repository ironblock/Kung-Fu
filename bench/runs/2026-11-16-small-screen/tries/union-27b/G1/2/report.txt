I wrote `src/mode-limits.ts` so that `useModeLimits` assigns the mode's limits to `camera.limits`, then immediately clamps the current radius and yaw back into that mode's ranges via writes (which the camera clamps against its limits and which adopt the new limits), plus a same-object `center` re-assignment to force adoption when the pose was already in range. The one thing I was not fully sure of: the notes state radius and pitch clamping explicitly but never say so verbatim for yaw, so I assumed yaw writes are clamped against `yawMin`/`yawMax` (which the limits type and note 37's mention of yaw writes as an application trigger strongly imply), and that each mode's yaw range lies within the camera's wrapped `[-PI, PI)` readback range.
