I wrote `src/mode-limits.ts` so that `useModeLimits` assigns the mode's limits object to `camera.limits` (which is what makes writes clamp to that mode's radius/yaw ranges and what `camera.limits` reports) and then does `camera.center = camera.center`, the documented way to force a limits change to apply immediately so the current pose is clamped into the new mode's ranges rather than waiting for the app's next write. The one thing I was not fully sure of is whether that center self-assignment also clamps the *current* pose or merely arms the new limits for the next write — note 37 says it "applies" the change, and I read that as clamping; even if it only arms, the stated requirements (future writes clamped, `camera.limits` reporting the mode's values) are still met, just with the pose catching up on the next write.
