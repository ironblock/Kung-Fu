I added `src/chase-camera.ts`, which creates a banked free camera and implements `bank` by projecting the car’s up vector onto the plane perpendicular to the current eye-to-target direction, then writing that normalized up to `upVector`; when the projection vanishes, it leaves the camera unchanged. I assumed `createBankedFreeCamera` is exported from `@babylonjs/lite` and that mutating `upVector.x/y/z` is the supported roll API, and I used a deterministic fallback up if the initial line of sight is vertical.
