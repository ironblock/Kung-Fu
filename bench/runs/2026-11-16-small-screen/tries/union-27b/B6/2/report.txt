I wrote `src/chase-camera.ts` to create a banked free camera and, on each `bank`, project the car's up onto the plane perpendicular to the current eye-to-target line, leaving the camera's up unchanged when that projection is degenerate. I assumed the package exports `createBankedFreeCamera` and that `upVector` is written in place, and that the camera is not parented (so `camera.position` is the eye).
