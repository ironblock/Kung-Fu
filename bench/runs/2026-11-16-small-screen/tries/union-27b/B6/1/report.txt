I added `src/chase-camera.ts`, which creates a `BankedFreeCamera` at the given eye/target and rolls it by writing the normalized car up into the camera’s `upVector`, skipping writes when that up is parallel to the line of sight. I was mainly unsure whether the runtime expects in-place mutation of `upVector` versus a setter/property write, and I added a fallback up only for the undefined initial case where the line of sight is parallel to world +Y.
