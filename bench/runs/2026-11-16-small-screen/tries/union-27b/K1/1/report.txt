I added `src/orbit-camera.ts` exporting `createOrbitCamera`, which builds a 10-unit arc-rotate camera aimed at the origin, installs the keyboard factory (the notes say `keyboard: true` is ignored without it), and attaches the standard controls to the canvas with the scene and `keyboard: true`. I wasn't sure whether `SceneContext` and `ArcRotateCamera` are exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` package root or only subpaths (I assumed root), and I picked alpha 0 / beta PI/2 as the starting pose since no angle was specified; the `attachControl` dispose function is deliberately not retained since the required return type is the camera.
