I wrote `src/center-on.ts`, which converts the degrees to radians, places the centre on the sphere of radius `camera.limits.planetRadius` using the note‑derived mapping (origin at `+X`, north `+Z`, east `−Y`), and moves it via `setGeospatialOrientation` so zoom, heading and tilt are untouched. The only things I had to infer rather than verify are that `setGeospatialOrientation` is exported from the `@babylonjs/lite` root and that passing only `center` leaves yaw/pitch/radius as they were — both are consistent with the type declaration and notes 7/11, so I'm confident, but I couldn't run anything to confirm.
