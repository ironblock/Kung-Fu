I wrote `notes/pointer-lock.json` based on the free-camera notes: button 0/1/2 drags turn the camera, a one-finger touch drag is a button-0 drag, and there is no button-less pointer-lock mode. I was not fully certain about `lockedButtonHeld` because the notes do not state whether the drag delta comes from `movementX` or `clientX`; I assumed `movementX`, so if the control actually uses `clientX`, that value would be `false`.
