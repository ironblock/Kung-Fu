I read `camera-api.d.ts` and `camera-notes.md`, and wrote the four answers to `notes/pointer-lock.json` based on notes 40, 55 and 66: `attachFreeControl` turns only via a drag started on button 0, 1 or 2 and has no pointer-lock handling, so lock-without-button turns nothing, a held-left drag under lock keeps feeding movement to the active drag (plus the existing right-drag and one-finger touch-drag cases both turn). One caveat: the notes never say whether the drag delta is read from `movementX`/`movementY` or from `clientX`/`clientY` diffs — I assumed the former (the notes describe the delta as the drag's movement, and that is what the browser reports while locked), so if the control instead diffs `clientX`/`clientY`, `lockedButtonHeld` would be `false`.
