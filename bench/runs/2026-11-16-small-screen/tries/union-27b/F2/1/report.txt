I added `src/walkthrough.ts`, which creates a free camera at the given eye/target, sets it as `scene.camera`, and attaches the standard free control with the scene (so its `_beforeRender` step actually moves); `jumpTo` rewrites `position`/`target` in place and resyncs the control's `_yaw`/`_pitch` so W and drags carry on from the new view, and `dispose` calls the returned detach, which removes the canvas listeners and the per-frame step.

Things I was not fully sure of: the notes document `_yaw = atan2(dx, dz)` but not `_pitch`, so I assumed the matching elevation convention `atan2(dy, hypot(dx, dz))`, clamped to the control's own ±(PI/2−0.01) range; I assumed `SceneContext.camera` is a plain settable field (its declaration isn't in the provided d.ts); and a jump made while a drag is still gliding can't cancel the control's hidden turn velocity (it isn't exposed), so a small amount of glide may linger for a few frames after the jump.
