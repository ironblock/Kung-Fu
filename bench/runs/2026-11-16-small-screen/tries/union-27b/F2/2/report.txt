I added `src/walkthrough.ts` to create a free camera, attach the usual free control, and make `jumpTo` update the camera position/target plus `_yaw`/`_pitch` so W and drags continue from the new view, with `dispose` calling the control's returned detach. I assumed `scene.camera` is assignable, and I clamped exact/near-vertical targets to the control pitch limit because the notes say a FreeCamera view parallel to world Y degenerates.
