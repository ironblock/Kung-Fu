I made `attachBigWorldFly` a thin wrapper around the library's `attachConfigurableFreeControl`, passing the documented default rise/sink keys (since providing them replaces the defaults) and marking all movement keys as fast keys with `fastMultiplier: 20`, so key movement is 20x while mouse look and the library's own detach stay untouched; I deliberately did not scale `camera.speed`, because the look glide threshold is speed-based and that would have changed mouse look. I was not fully sure of two things, unverifiable without the library: that `fastMultiplier` applies as a straight 20x multiplier to the per-frame increment of exactly the listed fast keys (inferred from the option name and your note 16), and that `attachConfigurableFreeControl`'s mouse drag behavior is identical to `attachFreeControl`'s (no look options are passed, so it should be, but the notes don't state it explicitly).
