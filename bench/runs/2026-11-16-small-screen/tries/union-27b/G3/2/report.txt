I added `src/fly-to-place.ts`, which delegates to the library’s `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` and resolves `true` only if the camera’s yaw, pitch, radius, and center match the requested place once the flight promise settles. I wasn’t fully sure of the library’s internal cancellation timing, so I relied on the documented behavior that the promise resolves when a flight is cut short and the final camera pose then indicates whether we actually got there.
