I wrote `src/turntable.ts` so that `spinAround` zeroes any leftover inertial offsets (so no drag/wheel/pan coasting can slip into the first frame) and then delegates to the library's own `interpolateArcRotateCamera` with an alpha-only goal of `camera.alpha + 2π·turns` — beta, radius and target are omitted so they stay put, the default easing does the gliding, and the returned promise resolves exactly on arrival at the unwrapped goal.

Things I was not sure of: I assumed `interpolateArcRotateCamera` is exported from the package root `"@babylonjs/lite"` (the declarations you gave show the internal modules, not the package entry); and the exact-arrival contract assumes the camera has no alpha limits set, since per your notes the interpolator clamps its goal to the camera's limit fields and would then land on the limit instead of `start + 2π·turns`.
