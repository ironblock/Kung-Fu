I wrote `src/turntable.ts` exporting `spinAround`, which hands the job to the library's `interpolateArcRotateCamera` with a goal `alpha` of the starting `alpha` plus `2π·turns` (and nothing else in the goal, so `beta`, `radius` and `target` stay put), returning that promise so it resolves exactly when the camera arrives. The one thing I was not fully sure of is how the notes' "alpha takes the shortest arc mod 2π" (note 56) interacts with multi‑turn goals, but note 58 states alpha is unwrapped and that a `2π` goal from `0` ends at `2π`, so I treated a single call as correctly handling `turns` of 1 and 2 as well as 0.75.
