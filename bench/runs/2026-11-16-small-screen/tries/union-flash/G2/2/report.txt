I added `app/src/center-on.ts` using the camera’s `limits.planetRadius` and the documented globe frame where north is `+Z` and east is `-Y` at `(R,0,0)`, so positive longitude is eastward. I was slightly unsure whether a plain `{x,y,z}` object is always accepted by the runtime `Vec3` type, but the notes indicate assigning a new object to `center` is the intended way to force the camera to re-orbit immediately.
