I wrote `notes/tighter-zoom.json` in the app with the numbers based on the notes: changing `limits.radiusMax` does not pull the camera in by itself, a same-value `radius` write does not apply the new limit, but assigning `center` itself does, and `setGeospatialOrientation(camera, {})` is treated as refreshing the current orientation and therefore applying the tightened radius limit. I was not completely sure whether `setGeospatialOrientation(camera, {})` is a no-op unless at least one field is present, but the notes imply it can re-orbit/apply limits from the current orientation, so I recorded `2000` for that case.
