I added `src/fast-orbit.ts` by wrapping the library's `attachControl` and temporarily halving `camera.angularSensibility`, which doubles standard mouse/touch drag rotation and its inertia while leaving wheel/pinch zoom unchanged; the returned disposer removes the controls and restores the original sensibility. I was not sure whether `keyboard: false` is the desired default here versus letting the library choose its own keyboard behavior, and I also assumed `attachControl` is exported from the root `"@babylonjs/lite"` entry point.
