I added `app/src/walkthrough.ts` using `createFreeCamera` and `attachFreeControl`, assigning `scene.camera`, updating `position`/`target` and the library’s `_yaw`/`_pitch` on jumps, and calling the disposer returned by `attachFreeControl` in `dispose`. I was not fully sure whether `jumpTo` should explicitly reset Babylon Lite’s internal drag/key inertia; this version leaves the controls attached and relies on updating `_yaw`/`_pitch`/`target`, and it also treats near-vertical target viewpoints carefully because a world-Y FreeCamera basis degenerates when the look direction is exactly vertical.
