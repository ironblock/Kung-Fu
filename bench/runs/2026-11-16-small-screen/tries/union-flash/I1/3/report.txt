I wrote the answer file `app/notes/go-home.json` based on the camera notes: in (a) the pre-first-frame coasting change becomes the interpolation start and inertia is zeroed, so the `await` resolves; in (b) a user drag changes the camera between interpolation frames, so it rejects; and in (c) a second `interpolateArcRotateCamera` call on the same camera rejects the first call’s promise. I was slightly unsure only about ambiguous edge cases, such as whether a “drag” could mean a middle-button drag that attachControl ignores, but for an actual camera-affecting drag the notes clearly imply rejection.
