I implemented `spinAround` by using `interpolateArcRotateCamera` sequentially in quarter-turn segments, omitting `beta`, `radius`, and `target` so only `alpha` changes, clearing pending inertial motion, and temporarily lifting only alpha limits that would block the requested unwrapped final alpha. I was not sure how negative `turns` should be treated, so I made them rotate positively by their magnitude, and I was not sure what should happen if finite alpha limits would clamp the exact requested final `alpha`; in that case I leave the interfering limit lifted rather than jumping or clamping.
