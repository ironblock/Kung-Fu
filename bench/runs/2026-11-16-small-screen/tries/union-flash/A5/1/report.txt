I created `notes/inset-slide.json` inside the app and used the notes to compute the clamped pixel viewport (`x` and `y` are top-left pixels, the right edge is clipped to the canvas) and the rounded aspect ratio. I am not completely sure whether `getEffectiveAspectRatio` also clamps viewport edges; I assumed it returns the effective/clamped viewport aspect (`200 / 150 = 1.33`), whereas if it uses the unclamped `0.3 × 0.3` viewport size the aspect would be `2.00`.
