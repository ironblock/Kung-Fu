I added `app/src/mode-limits.ts` and implemented per-camera `radius`/`yaw` accessors that clamp writes and reads to the current mode's `GeospatialLimits`, while also assigning `camera.limits` and forcing a limits apply via a center assignment. I was not sure about the exact intended semantics for yaw ranges that touch ±PI or cross the wrap boundary, so I chose a circular nearest-boundary clamp; also, if `radius`/`yaw` or `camera.limits` are non-configurable in the real runtime object, the wrapper may fall back to best-effort behavior rather than fully intercepting writes.
