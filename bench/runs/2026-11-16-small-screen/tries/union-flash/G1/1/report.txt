I added `src/mode-limits.ts` to install per-camera clamping for `radius` and `yaw`, switch `camera.limits` to the current mode’s `GeospatialLimits`, and clamp the current pose on mode changes. I was unsure whether Babylon Lite already enforces yaw limits or whether some internal paths bypass the public `radius`/`yaw` fields, so I defensively wrapped both the public fields and `_setOrientation` while preserving existing accessors where possible.
