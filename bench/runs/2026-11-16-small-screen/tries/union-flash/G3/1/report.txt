I added `src/fly-to-place.ts` as a wrapper around `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync`, using a per-camera token so overlapping `flyToPlace` calls make earlier promises resolve `false`, and deciding `true` by comparing the final camera pose to the requested place. I was unsure about the exact arrival tolerance and the edge case where an unrelated fly-to starts during our flight but happens to end exactly at the same place, because the library’s fly promise does not report why it resolved.
