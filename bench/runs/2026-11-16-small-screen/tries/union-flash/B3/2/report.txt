I wrote `src/fly-controls.ts` to use Babylon Lite’s configurable free-camera controls, overriding the vertical keys so Space is up and PageDown is down, and making left/right Shift a 3× speed key that should not move the camera vertically by itself. I relied on the declarations/notes for the assumption that `attachConfigurableFreeControl` is exported from `@babylonjs/lite` and that its returned disposer removes the canvas listeners and frame hook; I’m not certain whether its disposer also restores canvas `tabIndex` behavior beyond the controls themselves.
