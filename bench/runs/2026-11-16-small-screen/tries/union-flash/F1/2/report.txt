I created `src/big-world-fly.ts` so it attaches Babylon Lite’s configurable free-camera controls to the canvas with every default movement key marked as a fast key and a `fastMultiplier` of 20, returning the library’s dispose function to remove listeners and frame hooks. I was not 100% sure from the notes alone whether `fastKeys` means “movement keys affected by the multiplier” versus “modifier keys that enable fast movement”; I assumed the former because `attachConfigurableFreeControl` exposes `fastKeys` alongside `fastMultiplier`.
