I implemented `attachBigWorldFly` as a thin wrapper around `attachConfigurableFreeControl`, listing all default movement key codes as `fastKeys` and setting `fastMultiplier` to 20, then returning the library’s dispose function so the controls and their listeners/frame hooks are removed. I’m not fully certain how `fastKeys` is interpreted internally—whether it means “keys that get multiplied” or “modifier keys that trigger multiplication”—but I assumed that including the movement keys themselves with `fastMultiplier: 20` is the intended way to make every movement key move 20× while held without changing mouse look.
