I implemented the chase camera using `createBankedFreeCamera` because the notes say only banked free cameras expose an `upVector` that can roll, and `bank` projects the car's up onto the plane perpendicular to the current view direction while falling back to the previous roll when the requested up is exactly along the line of sight. I am not completely sure whether `@babylonjs/lite` exports `createBankedFreeCamera` from the package root or whether all `ObservableVec3` mutation paths mark the camera dirty, so I guarded the singular case and wrote through `copyFrom`/`set`/fields when available.
