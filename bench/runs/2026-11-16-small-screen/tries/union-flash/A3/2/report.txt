I added `src/screen-shift.ts` and implemented `trackScreenShift` by snapshotting copied view/view-projection matrices, the camera viewport/aspect, canvas backing/CSS sizes, and the camera world position when `_useFloatingOrigin` is enabled, then projecting the point with `projectWorldToScreen` and subtracting the CSS coordinates. I was not fully sure about edge cases: whether canvas/viewport resizing should be counted as motion (I snapshot those at `mark()`), how to handle direct camera writes that do not mark the cache dirty (such as in-place `target` changes), and whether points that go behind the camera should return raw projected coordinates or zero; I currently use raw finite coordinates if they are finite, and guard invalid states to zero.
