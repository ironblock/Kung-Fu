I implemented this by using `attachControl` for the library’s usual mouse/touch controls and `setCameraLimits` to clamp every radius write to `minDistance`/`maxDistance`, restoring the previous radius limit fields when the returned disposer is called. I was not completely sure whether callers expect the previous radius limits to be restored versus left in place, and I also assumed `attachControl` and `setCameraLimits` are exported directly from `@babylonjs/lite` as stated.
