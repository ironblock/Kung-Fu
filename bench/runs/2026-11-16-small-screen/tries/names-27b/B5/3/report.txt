I added `src/label-position.ts` to project the point through the camera’s view/projection matrices and map the resulting NDC coordinates to CSS pixels, while treating points beyond the far plane as visible if they are in front and inside the picture. I wasn’t sure whether those helper functions are re-exported from the package root in your build, so I imported them from `@babylonjs/lite`; I also assumed Babylon’s column-major matrix layout and camera convention where in-front points have negative view-space Z.
