I wrapped the library fly-to with a per-camera active-flight guard so a newer fly-to cancels the older one and resolves it false, and I resolve true only when the camera state matches the place (false on rejection/cancellation). I assumed `flyGeospatialCameraToAsync` and `setGeospatialOrientation` are exported from the package root and that cancellation settles the library's fly promise; if cancellation can leave that promise pending, that’s the part I couldn’t verify from the declarations.
