I added `src/fly-to-place.ts`, which starts the library’s geospatial fly-to and wraps the camera’s current `_cancelFly` so a cancelled or replaced flight resolves `false`, while normal completion resolves `true` and any rejection is mapped to `false`. I was not sure whether the library installs `_cancelFly` synchronously; if it delays installing that hook, a cancellation started before the hook is installed could be missed.
