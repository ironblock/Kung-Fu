I added `src/mode-limits.ts` to install clamped `limits`, `radius`, and `yaw` accessors on the camera (and wrap `_setOrientation` when present), with the active mode limits tracked per camera so mode switches replace the ranges. I was not sure of the exact runtime yaw convention or internal side effects of the library’s `_setOrientation`, so I normalized yaw to `[0, 2π)` and made the interception defensive based only on the provided declarations.
