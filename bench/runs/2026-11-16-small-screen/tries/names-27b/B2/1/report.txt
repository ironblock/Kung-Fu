I made `attachFastOrbit` use the library’s standard `attachControl` while temporarily doubling `camera.angularSensibility` for the lifetime of the controls, then restore it on detach, so drag and glide are twice as responsive while wheel zoom stays unchanged. I’m assuming the library’s pointer and inertia logic derives both active drag and post-release glide from `angularSensibility`; if it stores raw pointer velocity for glide independently, that would need a different hook.
