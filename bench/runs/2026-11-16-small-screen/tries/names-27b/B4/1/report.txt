I made `tiltGlobeCamera` preserve the current yaw, radius, and center, loosen the pitch limits and clear the radius-dependent pitch disable so the requested tilt is legal at all zoom levels, then call the camera’s orientation setter so the view is rebuilt. I wasn’t sure whether the app expects its original pitch limits to remain untouched, but I left them widened because that’s what keeps the tilt when the camera is later zoomed.
