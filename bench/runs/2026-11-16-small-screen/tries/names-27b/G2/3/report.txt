I added `src/center-on.ts`, which converts latitude/longitude to a point on the planet sphere using the convention that lat 0/lon 0 is +Z, north is +Y, and east at that point is +X, then applies it while leaving yaw, pitch, and radius unchanged. I was not certain whether the public `setGeospatialOrientation` helper would be preferable to the camera’s `_setOrientation`, so I used the latter to keep the import type-only.
