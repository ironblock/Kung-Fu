I added `app/src/center-on.ts` to convert the latitude/longitude to a point on the sphere of the camera’s planet radius and apply it via `setGeospatialOrientation`, passing the current yaw/pitch/radius so zoom, heading, and tilt are preserved. I was not certain of the library’s exact default axis convention, so I assumed latitude 0/longitude 0 is at +z with positive longitude toward +x; if your `createGeospatialCamera` default center uses a different prime-meridian axis, the x/z terms would need adjusting.
