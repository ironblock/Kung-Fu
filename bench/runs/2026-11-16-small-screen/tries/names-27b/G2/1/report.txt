I added `src/center-on.ts`, which converts the requested latitude/longitude to a point on the planet sphere and updates the camera’s centre while keeping its yaw, pitch, and radius unchanged. I wasn’t fully certain of Babylon Lite’s exact world-axis convention, so I used the Y-up convention implied by “lat 0, lon 0 is the new camera centre” and “heading 90° is east”: lat 0/lon 0 at +Z, north +Y, east +X.
