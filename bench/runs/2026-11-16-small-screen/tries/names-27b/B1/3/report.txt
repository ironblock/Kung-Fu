I wrote `src/limited-orbit.ts` to use the library’s `attachControl` for the usual mouse/touch orbit and zoom controls, and `setCameraLimits` to keep the radius within the requested range every frame, with an immediate clamp and a cleanup that disposes both. I wasn’t certain whether the package re-exports those functions from the root import or whether `setCameraLimits` itself restores limit state, so I restored any previous radius limits on detach.
