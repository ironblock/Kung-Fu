What the ledger does settle (from checked rows unless noted):
- The free camera has two control modules, and each adds its own per-frame hook to the end of the scene's per-frame list.
- The public `onBeforeRender` helper puts callbacks at the front of that list, and the list runs front to back. So a boost callback added that way would run before the controls each frame.
- The camera directory never registers anything with the scene's dispose machinery. The controls are not removed when the scene goes, so the returned function must detach both controls and remove our own per-frame callback explicitly. `disposeScene` does empty the per-frame list.
- The "frame order: what it means" note says the same about ordering, but nobody has checked it.
