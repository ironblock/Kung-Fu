I wrote /home/user/work/2e3220/app/src/globe-tilt.ts. It exports `tiltGlobeCamera(camera, pitch)`, which does three things:
1. Sets `camera.limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale = null`. Otherwise the zoom-dependent pitch limit pulls a zoomed-out camera (including a new one) back toward looking straight down, and the tilt would not survive zooming. This change is persistent for the camera.
2. Calls `setGeospatialOrientation(camera, { pitch })`. Yaw, radius and centre are omitted, so they keep their values.
3. Reads `camera.pitch` back and throws a RangeError if it differs from the request by more than 1e-6. This happens when the pitch is outside `limits.pitchMin`/`pitchMax`, since the library would otherwise clamp it silently. The function also throws on non-finite input.
