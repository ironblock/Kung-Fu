I wrote /home/user/work/a546f6/app/src/fly-controls.ts. It exports attachFlyControls(camera, canvas, scene) and delegates to the library's attachConfigurableFreeControl with upKeys ["Space"], downKeys ["PageDown"], fastKeys ["ShiftLeft", "ShiftRight"], and fastMultiplier 3. The returned cleanup is the library's own function.

Why not attachFreeControl: the declaration in camera-api.d.ts documents its up/down keys as "Space/Shift", so holding Shift would sink the camera. attachConfigurableFreeControl's defaults also put ShiftLeft/ShiftRight in downKeys, which is why I override downKeys.

- I dropped the library's default PageUp up-key to match your spec (Space only). Add "PageUp" to upKeys if you want it.
