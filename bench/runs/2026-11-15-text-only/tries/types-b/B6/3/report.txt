I wrote /home/user/work/00b059/app/src/chase-camera.ts. It exports createChaseCamera(eye, target) and the ChaseCamera interface, as specified.

How it works: it builds the camera with createBankedFreeCamera(eye, target, worldUp), which is the banked FreeCamera from camera-api.d.ts. On each bank(up) call it normalizes up, removes its component along the eye-to-target direction, normalizes what is left, and writes that into camera.upVector. If the remainder is near zero (up parallel to the line of sight), it returns without writing, so the previous roll is kept and the target is still looked at.
