I wrote /home/user/work/29678c/app/src/limited-orbit.ts. It exports attachLimitedOrbit(camera, canvas, scene, minDistance, maxDistance): () => void.

How it works, based on /home/user/work/29678c/app/camera-api.d.ts:
- It calls setCameraLimits(camera, { lowerRadiusLimit: minDistance, upperRadiusLimit: maxDistance }, scene) first. That clamps the current radius into range right away, and the camera's radius setter then clamps every write (wheel, pinch, inertia) with no per-frame hook of ours.
- It then calls attachControl(camera, canvas, scene), which gives the standard drag, wheel and pinch controls and registers the inertia hook on the scene.
- The returned function calls both disposers (attachControl's cleanup removes the canvas listeners and the inertia hook; setCameraLimits' disposer removes the clamp hook). It is idempotent.
- It throws a RangeError if the bounds are not finite, negative, or min > max.
