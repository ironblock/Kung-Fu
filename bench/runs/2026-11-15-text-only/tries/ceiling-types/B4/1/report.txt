**Why the limits change is needed:** the type declarations say the camera reduces the allowed pitch as it zooms out, so a fully zoomed-out camera looks straight down. A newly created camera starts fully zoomed out, so just setting the pitch would have had no visible effect. Turning that off is also what keeps the tilt when the app later zooms in or out.

**What I'm not sure of:**
