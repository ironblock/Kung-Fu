What the ledger tells us that matters for the implementation:
- attachControl(camera, canvas, scene) must get the scene, or nothing moves the camera (row "no scene: what it means"). Keyboard is off by default and is not needed for this task.
- Drag and glide accumulate in the public inertialAlphaOffset and related fields (row "no scene"). Doubling the drag could mean adding an extra offset in step with the library's own, but the ledger does not give the default mapping from mouse pixels to offset, so I cannot compute the extra amount. Scaling it by 2 needs that constant from the source.
- Do not add a per-frame hook with onBeforeRender. Per the frame-order note, it runs before the camera applies input, so it is one frame behind. Any extra motion should go through the offsets, not a per-frame callback.
- The function returned by attachControl is the disposer, and it splices the frame list. It must be called, and our own canvas listeners must be removed separately.
- Beta is held away from the poles, so an extra beta offset must respect that range.
