I wrote /home/user/work/96e04a/app/src/globe-tilt.ts. `tiltGlobeCamera` sets `camera.limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale = null`, which stops the allowed tilt from shrinking as the camera zooms out. It widens `pitchMin`/`pitchMax` if they don't already include the requested pitch. Then it calls `setGeospatialOrientation(camera, { pitch })`, so the zoom, heading and centre aren't passed in. Without the first step, the starting fully-zoomed-out camera would likely get pulled back toward looking straight down, and a tilt would be lost when zooming out.
