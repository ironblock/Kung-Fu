I wrote /home/user/work/d480b3/app/src/fly-controls.ts. It exports `attachFlyControls(camera, canvas, scene): () => void` with the requested signature. Nothing else in the app was changed.

What it does:
- Sets the camera's movement keys to W/A/S/D and the arrow keys, Space to rise (keysUpward) and PageDown to sink (keysDownward). Mouse look is handled by `camera.attachControl(canvas)`.
- Tracks ShiftLeft and ShiftRight on window keydown/keyup in a set, and multiplies `camera.speed` by 3 while either is held. Shift is not in any movement or vertical key list, so it cannot move the camera up or down.
- Clears the boost on window blur, so a missed keyup does not leave it stuck.
- The returned cleanup removes the window listeners, calls `camera.detachControl()`, and restores the previous keys and speed. It is safe to call twice.
- It registers nothing with the scene, so there is no per-frame work to remove. The `scene` parameter is accepted but unused.
