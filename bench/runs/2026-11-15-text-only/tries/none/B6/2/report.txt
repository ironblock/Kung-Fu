I wrote /home/user/work/a04cd9/app/src/chase-camera.ts. It is the only file I touched.

What it does: the roll math is pure and does not depend on the library. The forward direction is the normalized (target - eye). On each `bank(up)` call, the code projects the car's up onto the plane perpendicular to forward and normalizes the result. That is the closest up a roll about the line of sight can reach. The camera starts upright, using world Y projected against forward (world Z if the sight line is vertical). If the projection is near zero (under 1e-6), or the input is non-finite or zero-length, the camera keeps its previous roll and still re-aims at the target.
