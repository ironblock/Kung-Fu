I wrote /home/user/work/56650b/app/src/limited-orbit.ts. It exports attachLimitedOrbit with the requested signature. It sets lowerRadiusLimit and upperRadiusLimit on the camera so wheel and pinch stop at the bounds, clamps camera.radius immediately, and clamps it again each frame through scene.onBeforeRenderObservable. The returned function detaches the controls, removes the frame observer, and restores the previous radius limits. It is idempotent. It throws a RangeError for non-finite values, a non-positive minimum, or min > max.
