I wrote /home/user/work/b13f9a/app/src/limited-orbit.ts, exporting attachLimitedOrbit with the requested signature. It sets the camera's lowerRadiusLimit/upperRadiusLimit to the range, clamps the current radius immediately, and calls camera.attachControl(canvas, true). A requestAnimationFrame guard re-clamps camera.radius each frame. The returned function cancels that guard, calls camera.detachControl(), and restores the previous radius limits. Invalid ranges (non-finite, min <= 0, or min > max) throw a RangeError. Nothing else in the app was touched.
