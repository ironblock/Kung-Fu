I wrote /home/user/work/185ba3/app/src/fly-controls.ts. It exports attachFlyControls(camera, canvas, scene), which wraps the library's attachConfigurableFreeControl from "@babylonjs/lite". The options are upKeys ["Space"], downKeys ["PageDown"], fastKeys ["ShiftLeft", "ShiftRight"] and fastMultiplier 3. Because Shift is only in fastKeys and not in downKeys, it never moves the camera vertically on its own. W/A/S/D, the arrows and mouse look come from the library defaults. The returned cleanup calls the library's cleanup, which the notes say removes the listeners and the per-frame hook. It also removes the tabindex the wrapper added, if the canvas had none.
