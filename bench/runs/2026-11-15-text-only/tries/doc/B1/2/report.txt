I wrote /home/user/work/115c05/app/src/limited-orbit.ts. It exports attachLimitedOrbit(camera, canvas, scene, minDistance, maxDistance) with the requested signature.

How it works:
- It calls the library's attachControl(camera, canvas, scene) with default options, so the mouse and touch controls are the library's own. It adds no canvas listeners of its own.
- Before attaching, it clamps camera.radius into [min, max]. That covers a camera that starts out of range.
- It registers a per-frame hook with onBeforeRender(scene, ...). The hook clamps radius, since pinch writes radius directly. It also limits the pending inertialRadiusOffset so that the next zoom step cannot leave the range, and zeroes it when it would push past a limit. Zooming back toward the range still works right away. The clamp works whether it runs before or after the library's own inertia step.
- The returned function removes the frame hook and calls the attachControl cleanup.
- It throws a RangeError if minDistance is not greater than 0 or maxDistance is less than minDistance.
