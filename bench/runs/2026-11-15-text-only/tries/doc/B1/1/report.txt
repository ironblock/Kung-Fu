I wrote /home/user/work/d3e7a1/app/src/limited-orbit.ts with `attachLimitedOrbit(camera, canvas, scene, minDistance, maxDistance): () => void`. It calls the library's `attachControl(camera, canvas, scene)` for the default mouse, wheel and touch controls, then clamps `camera.radius` into [minDistance, maxDistance]. The clamp runs immediately on attach, on canvas `touchmove` and `gesturechange` (registered after the library's own handlers), and once per frame through `onBeforeRender(scene, ...)`, which is registered after the library's inertia step. When a limit is hit it also zeroes `inertialRadiusOffset`, so queued wheel momentum cannot push the radius back out. The returned cleanup removes both canvas listeners, calls the beforeRender unhook, and calls the library's detach; it is idempotent.
