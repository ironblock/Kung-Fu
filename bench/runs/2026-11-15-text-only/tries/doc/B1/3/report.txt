I wrote /home/user/work/5c6211/app/src/limited-orbit.ts, exporting attachLimitedOrbit with the signature you specified. It clamps the camera's radius to [minDistance, maxDistance] immediately on attach, then calls the library's attachControl for orbit, wheel and pinch. A beforeRender clamp is registered after the controls' hook, so each frame's radius is clamped after the library applies its zoom input. When the clamp fires, it also zeroes inertialRadiusOffset so a reversed scroll responds at once. The returned cleanup removes the clamp hook and calls the attachControl cleanup, and is safe to call twice. I did not run or type-check it, since the library is not on this machine.
