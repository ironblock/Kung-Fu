I wrote /home/user/work/e25077/app/src/chase-camera.ts. It exports `createChaseCamera(eye, target)` and the `ChaseCamera` interface as specified. The camera is built with `createBankedFreeCamera` from the notes. `bank(up)` removes the component of `up` along the fixed line of sight, normalizes what is left, and writes it into `camera.upVector`. If that leftover is near zero (up parallel to the line of sight, or a NaN/zero input), it returns without writing, so the previous roll is kept and the camera keeps looking at the target.
