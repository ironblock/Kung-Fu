I wrote /home/user/work/b90aeb/app/src/label-position.ts. It exports labelPosition(camera, canvas, point) and uses the library's getViewMatrix and getProjectionMatrix (both from camera.ts in the notes) to build view and clip coordinates. It does not check depth against the far plane. It computes x and y in CSS pixels from clientWidth and clientHeight. The aspect ratio comes from canvas.width / canvas.height. Visible means view-space z > 0 and the NDC position is within [-1, 1] on both axes.
