I wrote /home/user/work/38992b/app/src/chase-camera.ts. It exports `createChaseCamera(eye, target)` and the `ChaseCamera` interface as specified. It is unverified and has not been compiled or run.

What it does: the forward vector is fixed from eye and target. On each `bank(up)` it projects `up` onto the plane perpendicular to forward and writes the normalized result to `camera.upVector`. That is the picture-up closest to the car's up. If the projection is shorter than 1e-6 (up along the line of sight), it returns without touching the camera, so the previous roll is kept. Creation starts upright, using world Y projected against forward, or world Z if the line of sight is vertical. It throws a RangeError if eye equals target.
