It attaches the library's controls, clamps camera.radius to [minDistance, maxDistance] immediately, and keeps it clamped via a per-frame hook plus canvas input listeners. The returned function removes the listeners, the frame hook and the controls. Nothing was compiled or run, because the library is not on this machine.
