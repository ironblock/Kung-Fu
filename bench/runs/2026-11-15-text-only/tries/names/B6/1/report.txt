I wrote /home/user/work/6cb21d/app/src/chase-camera.ts. It exports createChaseCamera(eye, target), which builds the camera with createBankedFreeCamera (a FreeCamera subtype), and bank(up), which projects the car's up onto the plane perpendicular to the line of sight and writes that into camera.upVector. If up is parallel to the line of sight (or zero), bank returns without changing anything, so the target and previous roll are kept. The camera starts upright: world up with the forward component removed, falling back to +Z if the view is vertical. Eye equal to target throws.
