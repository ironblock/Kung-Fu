I wrote /home/user/work/04572d/app/src/fast-orbit.ts. `attachFastOrbit` halves `camera.angularSensibility`, which is a divisor, so drag turns twice as far. It then calls the library's `attachControl(camera, canvas, scene)`. The glide after release comes from the same inertial offsets, so it doubles too. Wheel zoom and panning are untouched. The returned function calls the library's detach and restores the original sensibility. It is safe to call more than once, and it adds no listeners or per-frame hooks of its own.
