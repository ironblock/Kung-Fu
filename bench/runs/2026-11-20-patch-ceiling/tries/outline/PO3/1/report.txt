I couldn't see the body of `report`, so I renamed it to a private `baseReport()` and made a new `report()` that calls it, then adds `describeEvent` lines for the last three entries of `this.events`, newest first. That array is in the order events were added, not sorted by time. The existing lines are untouched, and fewer than three events (or none) just gives fewer lines.

I assumed `log` appends to `this.events` in order, and that `report` returns a fresh array. I also assumed `report(): string[] {` appears only once in the file, as the outline suggests.
