I added `openMinutes` at the end of `src/tides.ts`. It clips each window to the stretch from `from` to `to`, sums the lengths of what remains, and returns 0 when `to` is not after `from`. It only reads the windows, so the list and its windows are left as they are, and their order doesn't matter.

I couldn't see the rest of the file, so I assumed nothing at the end of it would clash with a new function. I also didn't handle a window that wraps past midnight (`closes` less than `opens`), since you described all times as minutes from midnight; such a window would add nothing.
