I added `berthsFor` at the end of `src/moorings.ts`. It filters to berths with `boatId === ""` that pass `fits(boat, berth)` and, if `needPower` is true, have power. It then sorts by berth length, with ties broken by the original position, and returns the ids. It works on a filtered copy, so `berths` and its berths are not changed.

I couldn't see the file body, so I haven't checked how `fits` or `isFree` are written. I tested for freeness directly with `boatId === ""` instead of calling `isFree`, to match your description exactly.
