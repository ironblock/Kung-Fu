I added `usageByBoat` at the end of `src/fuel.ts`. It sums draw volumes per boat for days from `fromDay` up to but not including `toDay`, skips `YARD` draws, and sorts by volume descending, then by name. It builds a new array and does not change the ledger.

I couldn't see the rest of the file, so I haven't checked how the ledger entries are used elsewhere. Name ties are ordered by plain character comparison (so uppercase sorts before lowercase), not by locale.
