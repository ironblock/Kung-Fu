I couldn't see the body of `shiftPayPence`, so I renamed it to a private `basePayPence`, which keeps its existing calculation and rounding unchanged. A new `shiftPayPence` calls it and adds 1,250 pence when the shift's start day (`shift.day`) is a Sunday. The allowance goes on after rounding and doesn't depend on length or rate. A night shift is judged only by the day it starts.

I'm not sure which number `weekdayOf` returns for Sunday. I assumed the week starts on Monday, so Sunday is 6 (`const SUNDAY = 6`). If your `weekdayOf` counts from Sunday = 0, change that one constant. I also assumed `shift.day` is the day the shift starts, as the field name suggests.
