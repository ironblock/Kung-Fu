I couldn't see the body of `quoteHire`, so I renamed the existing function to `quoteHireWithoutPercentage` and put a new `quoteHire` in front of it. The new one calls the old one and adds ` (N%)` to the line labelled exactly `Long hire discount`, using the percentage from `longHireRate(hire.nights)`. Amounts, other lines, their order and the total are untouched, and a hire with no discount line is unchanged.

I assumed two things I couldn't check:
- `longHireRate` returns a fraction (0.1 for ten percent). If it returns whole percents, drop the `* 100`.
- The discount line's label is exactly `Long hire discount`. If the label differs, such as having extra text, the match will miss and the label won't change.

The cap doesn't affect the label, since the rate comes from `longHireRate` and not from the capped amount.
