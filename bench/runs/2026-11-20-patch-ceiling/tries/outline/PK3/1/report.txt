I couldn't see the body of `certificateLine`, so I renamed the existing function to a private `certificateLineBase` and put a new `certificateLine` in front of it. The new one adds `, N days left`, `, 1 day left` or `, lapsed` to started certificates, interim ones included. Lines for no certificate and for a certificate not yet started pass through unchanged.

I wasn't sure whether `expires` is the first uncovered day or the last covered one. I assumed the first uncovered day, so days left is `expires - today`. If `expires` is the last covered day, change that line to `cert.expires - today + 1`. Check `currentCertificate` or `runsOut` to confirm which it is.
