I added `stillOut` just before `gearCharge`. It sums `issued - back` per kind over open loans only, skips settled loans, and starts from zero for all six kinds. Damaged items are not subtracted a second time because `back` already includes them. It reads the loans without changing them.

I wasn't sure what to do if a line had `back` greater than `issued`, which `takeBack` prevents, so I didn't guard against it.
