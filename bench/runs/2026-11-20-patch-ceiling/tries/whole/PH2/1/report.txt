I changed `Manifest.addLoad` so an item is folded into an existing row only when the stowage, the lowercased tidied label and the unit weight all match. Otherwise it is pushed as a new row at the end. The refusals, label tidying and the return value are unchanged. Unit weights are compared with exact `===`, so 13.5 and 15 stay apart. Two weights that differ only by floating-point noise would also stay apart, which I assumed is acceptable.
