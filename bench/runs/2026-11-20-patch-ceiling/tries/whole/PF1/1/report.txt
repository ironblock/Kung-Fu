I added `partsBySku()` to `WorkLog`. It skips jobs in the `dropped` state and sums `qty` per `sku` over the parts lines of all other jobs, whatever the unit price. It only reads the jobs, so nothing in the log changes.

Keys are the stored `sku` strings as they are. `addPart` already stores them trimmed and upper-cased, so lines that differ only in unit price fall under one key. A part whose lines on counted jobs add up to zero would still appear with 0, but `addPart` removes lines that reach zero, so this should not happen.
