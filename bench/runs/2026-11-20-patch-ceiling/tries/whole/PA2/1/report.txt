I changed `stayFee` so the discount is calculated once on the nights after the seventh only (`(nights - 7) * rate`), at the same 10% and still rounded down, then subtracted from the full bill. Stays of seven nights or fewer now get no discount.

The old code discounted a stay of exactly 7 nights, since it checked `nights < LONG_STAY`. Under your rule that stay has no later nights, so it now pays in full.
