In `closeFor`, a leftover piece on either side of a cut window is now kept only if it is at least 12 minutes (`LOCKING`) long, and a shorter piece is dropped with its minutes added to the returned total. Windows the closure doesn't touch still skip the cut logic and are left as they are. I haven't run it. If a window is cut so that both sides are too short, it is removed entirely, and I took that to be what you want.
