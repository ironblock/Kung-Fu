I added `usageByBoat` at the end of `src/fuel.ts`. It sums draw volumes per boat for days in `[fromDay, toDay)`, skips `YARD` draws, and sorts by volume descending, then by name. It only reads the ledger. Names are compared by plain code-unit order (so uppercase sorts before lowercase), which is my reading of "A before B"; say if you'd prefer a locale-aware comparison.
