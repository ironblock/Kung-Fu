I changed `gearCharge` so that any missing boat keys cost one boat key in total (nothing if none are missing), and each damaged lifejacket is charged the full lifejacket cost. Missing gear of other kinds, missing lifejackets, and damaged gear of other kinds, including damaged boat keys, are charged as before, and the loan is not modified. A key that comes back damaged is not counted as missing, so a loan with a damaged key and no missing keys gets only the half-cost damaged-key charge, with no lock-change charge.
