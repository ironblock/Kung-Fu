In `describe`, a notice with an end date now gets `, last day N` after its first day and before the cause, for closures and restrictions alike. Notices with no end date keep their current line.

In this file `to` is the day the stretch reopens, and `inForce` is false on it. So N is `to - 1`, which gives "last day 131" for a notice with `to` of 132. I took your "last day on which the notice is still in force" to mean exactly that. If your example was meant to have `to` of 131, the offset would need changing.
