`boatBill` now sums the boat's draws within the day range by day, then writes one line per day in ascending day order, with the pence charged once on the day's total volume. The bill's volume and total are still summed over the lines, and a day with no price still throws the same error.

One small difference: if several days lack a price, the error now names the earliest such day, where before it named the first one in entry order.
