`Handover.report` now ends with up to three more lines, one for each of the last three events added, newest first, each written by `describeEvent`. The timeline is already stored newest first by insertion order (`log` uses `unshift`), so taking the first three follows when events were added, not the time on them. With fewer than three events you get one line each, and with none you get no extra lines. The existing lines, including `events: N`, are unchanged, and the new lines come after them.
