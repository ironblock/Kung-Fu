I added a 14-night threshold in `hireCharge` so hires of 14 nights or more take 15 per cent off the same pre-discount total, rounded up to the whole pound as the tenth is. Hires of 7 to 13 nights keep the tenth, and shorter ones get no discount.

One thing to be aware of: only wide boats (max 14) and narrow boats (max 21) can reach 14 nights, so cruisers (max 10) are unaffected. I haven't run anything, so this is untested.
