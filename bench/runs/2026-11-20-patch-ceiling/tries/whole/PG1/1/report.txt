I added `BookingBook.outstanding(boatId)`, placed right after `forBoat`. It sums `chargePence - paidPence` over the boat's held and confirmed bookings, with each booking floored at 0 so overpayments never reduce the total. Unknown boats and boats with no such bookings give 0, and nothing in the book is changed.

One thing to note: a held booking has paid nothing, so its full charge counts as owed, which follows your wording.
