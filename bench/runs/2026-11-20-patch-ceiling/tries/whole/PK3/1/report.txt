For a started certificate, `certificateLine` now ends with `, N days left` (or `, 1 day left`), or `, lapsed` when today is on or after the certificate's `expires`. This applies to interim certificates too. The no-certificate and not-yet-started lines are unchanged.

Days left is `cert.expires - today`, since `expires` is the first day the boat may not go out, so today counts while it is before `expires`. I didn't add the days of a follow-on certificate that carries on from this one, because the function only receives the one certificate. If you want those included, it would need the history, as `runsOut` has.
