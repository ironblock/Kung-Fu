I changed `BookingBook.cancel` so a confirmed hire now returns the old amount plus the deposit share from `refundShare`, using notice = `fromDay` minus the day of cancelling. The total feeds the returned sum, the note, the logged event and the reduction of `paidPence`; holds still get 0.

Two things I chose myself: the deposit share is rounded down to whole pence (deposits are whole pounds, so it rarely matters), and a cancellation after the hire's first day gives negative notice, which the scale treats as no share.
