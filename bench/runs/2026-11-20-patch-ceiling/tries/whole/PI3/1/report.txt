I gave `visitsOf` the optional `latest` parameter. Because `record` uses `unshift`, the log is already ordered newest-recorded first, so I filter by boat and keep the first `latest` matches, in log order. This doesn't depend on day or start time, and the log isn't modified. Leaving `latest` out returns the same result as before, and 0 gives an empty list. A negative `latest` is treated as 0, a case you didn't specify.
