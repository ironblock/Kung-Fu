I added `openMinutes` at the end of `src/tides.ts`. It clips each window to the stretch from `from` to `to`, sums the overlaps, returns 0 when `to` is not after `from`, and doesn't modify the list or its windows. Since I couldn't see the end of the file, I assumed it ends cleanly. The function doesn't depend on window order. It also assumes no window crosses midnight (`closes` greater than `opens`), as I didn't see anything handling that.
