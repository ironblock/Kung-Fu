I added `openShifts` to `Rota`, just before `shiftsFor`. It filters `this.shifts` into a new array, so the rota itself is unchanged, and sorts that copy by `day` and then `start`. It uses its own comparator because I couldn't see `sortShifts` or `byStart` and didn't want to assume their ordering. Like `shiftsOn`, it returns the rota's own shift objects rather than copies.
