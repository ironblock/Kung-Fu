I added `berthsFor` at the end of `src/moorings.ts`. It keeps berths that are free (`boatId === ""`), pass `fits`, and have power when `needPower` is true. It sorts them by length, with ties kept in their original order by using the original index. It builds new arrays, so `berths` and its berths are not changed.

I checked for a free berth with `berth.boatId === ""` directly rather than calling `isFree`, since I couldn't see its body. It probably does the same thing.
