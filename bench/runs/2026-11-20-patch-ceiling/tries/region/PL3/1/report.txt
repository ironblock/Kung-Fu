In `describe`, a notice with an end date now gets `, last day N` straight after the first day and before the cause, closures and restrictions alike. A notice with no end date keeps its "until further notice" line.

I couldn't see whether `to` is the last day itself or the day after it. So I ask `inForce` about `notice.to`: if it says yes, N is `to`, otherwise N is `to - 1`. If you know `to` is always inclusive or always exclusive, you can replace that with the plain value.
