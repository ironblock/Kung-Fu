I added `shortfall` right after `freeOf` in `PartsStore`. It returns `units` for an unknown part. Otherwise it returns `units` minus the job's reach (free stock plus what the job has set aside), floored at 0. It only reads from the store, so nothing changes.

I assumed `parts` is keyed by `keyOf(partNo)`, as `heldFor` does with its holds. I also clamp free stock at 0 in case it ever comes out negative, which I couldn't check from the outline.
