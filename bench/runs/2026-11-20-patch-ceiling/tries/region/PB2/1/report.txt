In `closeFor`, a leftover piece on either side of a cut window is now kept only if it is at least `LOCKING` (12) minutes long; shorter ones are dropped and their minutes added to `lost`. Windows the closure doesn't touch are skipped by the existing `continue`, so they stay as they are.

I assumed `LOCKING` is a numeric constant equal to 12 (the outline shows it as `LOCKING = 12` without a `const` prefix, so I couldn't see its declaration).
