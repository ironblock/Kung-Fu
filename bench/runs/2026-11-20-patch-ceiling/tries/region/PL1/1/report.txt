I added `closedDays` right after `routeOpen` in `src/stoppages.ts`. It steps through each day from `fromDay` up to but not including `toDay`, collects the days where `routeOpen` is false, and returns them in rising order as a new array. It returns an empty list when `toDay` is not after `fromDay`, and it doesn't change the register or route. I couldn't run anything, so it hasn't been compiled or tested. It assumes day numbers are whole numbers, since it steps by 1.
