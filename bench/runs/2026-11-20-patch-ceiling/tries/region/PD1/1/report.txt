I added `usageByBoat` at the end of `src/fuel.ts`. It reads the ledger only, sums `"draw"` volumes per boat for days from `fromDay` up to but not including `toDay`, skips `YARD`, and sorts by volume descending, then by name.

I couldn't see the rest of the file, so I wrote it without the existing helpers (`drawsOf`, `drawnBetween`) and assumed draw volumes are stored as positive numbers. Names are compared by plain character order, so uppercase letters sort before lowercase.
