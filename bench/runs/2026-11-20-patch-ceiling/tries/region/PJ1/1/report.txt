I added `stillOut` at the end of `src/keys.ts`. It skips loans that `isOpen` says are settled, and for each line it adds `issued - back` to that kind's total, without using `damaged`, since `back` already includes damaged items. It starts from all six kinds at 0 and only reads the loans.

I couldn't see the body of `isOpen`, so I'm trusting it to mean "not settled". I also clamp each line's count at 0, so a line with more `back` than `issued` can't make a total negative.
