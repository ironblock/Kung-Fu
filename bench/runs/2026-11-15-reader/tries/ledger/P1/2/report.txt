Why, from the code in babylon-lite/packages/babylon-lite/src/camera/arc-rotate-controls.ts (attachControl):
- Drag (mouse, or one finger) only adds to camera.inertialAlphaOffset and inertialBetaOffset. Those are applied by applyInertia, which is pushed onto scene._beforeRender only when a scene is passed. With no scene, alpha and beta never move.
- Wheel only adds to inertialRadiusOffset, which is applied the same way. No scene means no change.
- Two-finger pinch (onTouchMove) writes camera.radius directly, with no scene needed. So radius changes on a touch screen.
