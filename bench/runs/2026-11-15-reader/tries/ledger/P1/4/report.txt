Why: with no scene, attachControl never registers its per-frame applyInertia hook (arc-rotate-controls.ts, `if (scene) scene._beforeRender.push(applyInertia)`). Mouse drag and wheel only accumulate inertialAlphaOffset/BetaOffset and inertialRadiusOffset, which only applyInertia reads, so alpha, beta and radius stay put. Pinch (onTouchMove) writes camera.radius directly, so it changes radius without a scene.

Verification: I ran a temporary vitest probe against the pinned checkout (import resolved to babylon-lite/packages/babylon-lite/src). Without a scene: drag and wheel left alpha/beta/radius at 1 / 1.2 / 10, and a two-finger pinch moved radius to about 4.29. With a scene, drag and wheel did change the pose after frames ran, so the probe could see changes. I deleted the probe afterwards; the app's src directory is still empty.
