Why, from reading `attachControl` in `babylon-lite/packages/babylon-lite/src/camera/arc-rotate-controls.ts`:
- Drag only adds to `inertialAlphaOffset`, `inertialBetaOffset` and the panning fields. Those are applied only by `applyInertia`, which is pushed onto `scene._beforeRender` only when a scene is passed. With no scene, alpha and beta do not move.
- Wheel only changes `inertialRadiusOffset`, which is applied by the same per-frame function, so radius does not move.
- Two-finger pinch writes `camera.radius` directly in `onTouchMove`, with no scene needed. The `radius` setter in `arc-rotate.ts` stores the value, so it changes.

The ledger rows I checked agree with this: the "controls lifetime" note says there is no requestAnimationFrame fallback, and the `run` searches (`scene._beforeRender.push` in the camera directory and the `$C._beforeRender.length = 0` search in `disposeScene`) match their stated counts.
