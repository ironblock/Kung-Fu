I wrote `src/chase-camera.ts` in the app, exporting `ChaseCamera` and `createChaseCamera(eye, target)` as specified. It wraps the library's existing `createBankedFreeCamera` (ledger row "factories: banked camera reuses the free factory", which I confirmed by reading the code). `bank(up)` projects `up` onto the plane perpendicular to the current view direction and writes that to `upVector`, so picture-up is the closest possible match. On a degenerate up (parallel to the view, zero, or NaN) it re-projects the last applied picture-up and keeps the roll, with the target untouched. I also added `src/chase-camera.test.ts` (not requested; delete it if unwanted). All 10 tests pass under vitest, and tsc reports no errors in `src/`.
