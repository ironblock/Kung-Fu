I added src/chase-camera.ts, which exports createChaseCamera(eye, target) returning { camera, bank(up) }. It builds the camera with the library's createBankedFreeCamera (its upVector is mutable and feeds the look-at basis), starts it with up = world +Y, and bank(up) sets upVector to the normalized up. Calls where up is parallel to the line of sight, within 1e-9 of the sine of the angle, are ignored so the previous roll is kept, and the library's identity fallback is never reached. I also added src/chase-camera.test.ts, and all 7 tests pass under `npx vitest run` in the app; `tsc -p tsconfig.json` reports no errors in the chase files. Both files are under /tmp/claude-0/-home-user/5a6488dd-41c7-5be7-b55c-ee990999c7a9/scratchpad/reader-work/ledger/B6/1/app/src/.

Things I am not sure of: I did not run the ledger's `run` searches, since the chase camera does not depend on the rows they check. The `is` notes I relied on are checked only by my own reading of banked-free-camera.ts, write-look-at-world-mat4-lh-into-buffer.ts and observable-vec3.ts, not by a tool. If eye equals target, or the view is exactly vertical at start, the library's identity fallback makes the initial roll undefined, and I did not special-case that.
