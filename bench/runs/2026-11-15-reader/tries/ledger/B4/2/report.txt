I added `tiltGlobeCamera(camera, pitch)` in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000/scratchpad/reader-work/ledger/B4/2/app/src/globe-tilt.ts`. It sets `camera.limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale = null` and then calls `setGeospatialOrientation(camera, { pitch })`.

Why: in the library's `geospatial-limits.ts`, the pitch ceiling falls with zoom-out and reaches about 0.001 at `radiusMax`. `applyOrientation` in `geospatial-camera.ts` re-clamps pitch on every orientation change. A fresh camera sits at `radiusMax`, so a requested tilt would be flattened to straight down, and any later zoom-out would flatten it again. Turning that falloff off keeps the tilt at every radius. Yaw, radius and centre are not touched.

Things to know:
- **Behaviour change:** `pitchDisabledRadiusScale = null` applies to that camera for the whole app. The automatic "look down when zoomed out" falloff no longer happens for it. This is the only way I found to keep the tilt when zooming out.
