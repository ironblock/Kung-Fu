Reasoning, from the checkout at .../P1/1/babylon-lite/packages/babylon-lite/src/camera/arc-rotate-controls.ts (package version 0.1.0):
- Drag (mouse or single touch): pointer handlers only add to camera.inertialAlphaOffset/BetaOffset. Those offsets become alpha/beta only in applyInertia, which runs solely from scene._beforeRender. With no scene passed to attachControl, nothing applies them, so alpha and beta stay put.
- Wheel: onWheel only adds to inertialRadiusOffset, which is likewise applied only through the scene's per-frame hook. Radius does not change.
- Pinch: the touchmove handler writes camera.radius directly, with no scene needed, so pinch zooms. This needs two touch points (a touchscreen tablet). A laptop trackpad pinch arrives as a wheel event, which does nothing here.
