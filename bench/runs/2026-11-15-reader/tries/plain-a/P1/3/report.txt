Why: with no scene passed to attachControl, mouse drag and the wheel only accumulate inertial offsets (inertialAlphaOffset/BetaOffset, inertialRadiusOffset). Those offsets are applied only from the scene's _beforeRender hook, which is never registered without a scene, so alpha, beta and radius stay put. Two-finger pinch writes camera.radius directly in the touchmove handler, so it works without a scene.
