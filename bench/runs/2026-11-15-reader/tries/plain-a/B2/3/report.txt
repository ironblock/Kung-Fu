How it works: attachFastOrbit calls the library's attachControl unchanged, then doubles each frame's realized alpha/beta change. Doubling the input sensitivity looked simpler, but the library's glide cutoff is an absolute threshold, which left the total about 1-2% off 2x. The test suite caught that.

Cleanup: the returned function removes the library's listeners and frame hook plus my two hooks, and is safe to call twice. A second attach on the same camera throws.

Unsure:
- Only a fake-DOM test harness was used, not a browser, so the feel on a real device is unverified.
- Pan and wheel are untouched. Keyboard controls are not enabled.
- The hook relies on the library's _beforeRender order and duplicates its 0.01 beta limit.
- The tsc errors in the babylon-lite checkout are pre-existing WebXR type gaps, not from this change.
