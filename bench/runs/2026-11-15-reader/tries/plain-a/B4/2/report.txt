Why the first step is needed: in this checkout, `applyOrientation` clamps pitch to `getEffectivePitchMax(limits, radius)`. The default limits have `pitchDisabledRadiusScale: {x: 2, y: 4}`, so at 4x the planet radius (where a new camera starts) the allowed pitch is `pitchMin` (0.001). Any requested tilt would snap back to about 0, and it would also reset on later zoom changes.

Things to be aware of:
- Setting `pitchDisabledRadiusScale` to `null` is a permanent change to that camera's limits. The camera then keeps full pitch at every zoom, so the library's zoom-out-to-look-down behaviour is gone for that camera. I chose this because the tilt has to survive zoom changes. If you need the default behaviour back, this would need a different approach.
- Pitch is still clamped to `[pitchMin, pitchMax]` (about 0.001 to 1.56), so the requested 0.1 to 1.2 range is unaffected.
