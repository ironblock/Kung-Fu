It sets `camera.limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale = null` and then calls the library's `setGeospatialOrientation(camera, { pitch })`.

Why the null line is needed: the library caps pitch by the current radius on every orientation change (`applyOrientation` calls `getEffectivePitchMax`). Beyond 4x the planet radius the cap is effectively 0, so a new camera (which starts fully zoomed out) would be flattened back to pitch ~0 by a plain pitch assignment, and later zoom-ins/outs would keep reducing it. Disabling that radius-based cap is what makes the tilt stick at any zoom.
