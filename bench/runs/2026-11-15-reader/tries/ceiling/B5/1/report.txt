Things I wasn't sure of:
- **CSS size:** I read the canvas's CSS size from `clientWidth`/`clientHeight`. If the canvas has a CSS transform or border, labels positioned with `getBoundingClientRect` would be slightly off. If the canvas isn't laid out yet (size 0), positions come back in raw canvas pixels.
- **Large-world mode:** when the camera is in the library's large-world (floating origin) mode, I shift the point by the camera's position, which is how the library's own helper for this works. To do that I read an internal camera field, `_useFloatingOrigin`, so it could change in a future library version.
- **Camera viewport:** if the camera ever has a `viewport` set to less than the whole canvas, "the picture" means that sub-rectangle.
- **Near plane:** a point in front of the camera but closer than its near clipping distance counts as visible.
