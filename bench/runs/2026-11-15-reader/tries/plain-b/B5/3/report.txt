Things I was not sure of:
- Under floating origin I only worked out the rebasing by reading the code. My test needed the F64 matrix allocator to match, which a real engine sets itself.
- A canvas with zero backing size makes the library throw a RangeError, and I did not guard against that.
- Points between the camera and the near plane count as visible, because the spec only asks for "in front and inside the picture".
- The view matrix cache is keyed on `worldMatrixVersion` only, so I am not certain a `_useFloatingOrigin` flip is picked up immediately without a transform change.
