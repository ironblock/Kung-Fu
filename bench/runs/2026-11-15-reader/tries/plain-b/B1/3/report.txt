I added `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000/scratchpad/reader-work/plain-b/B1/3/app/src/limited-orbit.ts`, which exports `attachLimitedOrbit(camera, canvas, scene, minDistance, maxDistance): () => void`.

How it works: it calls the library's `setCameraLimits` with `lowerRadiusLimit = minDistance` and `upperRadiusLimit = maxDistance`, then calls `attachControl(camera, canvas, scene)` for the standard drag, wheel and pinch controls. The library's radius setter clamps on every write, so wheel inertia and pinch writes cannot leave the range. Attaching also clamps an out-of-range camera immediately. The returned function detaches the controls, removes the radius limits, and restores the camera's previous `lowerRadiusLimit` and `upperRadiusLimit`. It is idempotent. The module throws a `RangeError` for non-finite values, `minDistance < 0.01` (the library's own radius floor), or `maxDistance < minDistance`.
