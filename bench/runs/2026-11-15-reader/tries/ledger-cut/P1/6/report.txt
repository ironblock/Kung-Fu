Answer, from reading `src/camera/arc-rotate-controls.ts` and `src/camera/arc-rotate.ts` in the checked-out Babylon Lite 1.39.0 (the exact tree you specified):
- Drag: false. Pointer moves only add to `inertialAlphaOffset` and `inertialBetaOffset`. Those are applied only in `applyInertia`, which is registered on `scene._beforeRender` only when a scene is passed. With no scene, alpha and beta never change.
- Wheel: false. The handler only adds to `inertialRadiusOffset`, which has the same no-scene problem, so radius does not change.
- Pinch: true. The touch-move handler writes `camera.radius` directly, with no scene needed. Alpha and beta are not touched.

Not sure of, or caveats for QA:
- The pinch result assumes real touch events, as on a tablet. On a laptop trackpad, a pinch arrives as a wheel event with `ctrlKey`, so it goes through the wheel path and does nothing on that page.
