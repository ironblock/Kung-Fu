Why:
- `attachControl` in babylon-lite/packages/babylon-lite/src/camera/arc-rotate-controls.ts only registers the per-frame `applyInertia` hook when a scene is passed (`if (scene) scene._beforeRender.push(applyInertia)`). Without a scene, drag and wheel only fill `inertialAlphaOffset`, `inertialBetaOffset` and `inertialRadiusOffset`, and nothing applies them, so alpha, beta and radius do not change.
- Pinch (touchmove with two fingers) writes `camera.radius` directly, with no scene needed, so it changes the radius.
- I checked this with a temporary vitest probe that simulated a canvas with no scene. Drag and wheel left alpha, beta and radius unchanged, and pinch changed them. I deleted the probe afterwards and recreated the app's empty `src` directory, which I had accidentally removed and restored.

Things I was not sure of:

- Laptop trackpad pinch usually arrives as wheel events with `ctrlKey`, not as touch events. Under this answer, that gesture on a laptop would change nothing, while a touchscreen pinch on the tablet would zoom. QA should expect that difference.
