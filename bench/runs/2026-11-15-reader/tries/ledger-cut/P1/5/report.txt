Basis, from reading babylon-lite/packages/babylon-lite/src/camera/arc-rotate-controls.ts and arc-rotate.ts:
- Drag (mouse or single touch) only accumulates `inertialAlphaOffset`/`inertialBetaOffset`/panning. Those offsets are applied in `applyInertia`, which is pushed to `scene._beforeRender` only when a scene is passed. With no scene, alpha and beta do not change.
- Wheel only accumulates `inertialRadiusOffset`, which is applied in the same scene-only `applyInertia`. Radius does not change.
- Pinch is a direct write, `camera.radius = pinchStartRadius * (pinchStartDist / dist)` in the touchmove handler, with no scene involved. Radius changes.
