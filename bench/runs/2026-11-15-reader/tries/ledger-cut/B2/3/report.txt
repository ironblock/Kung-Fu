What I verified in the library source (`babylon-lite/packages/babylon-lite/src/camera/arc-rotate-controls.ts`): a drag adds `delta / angularSensibility` to the inertial offsets, and the per-frame glide is built from those same offsets. Not certain or not exact:
- The drag is exactly 2x. The glide is 2x plus a small overshoot, measured at about 2.019x on beta for one test drag. The library stops inertia at a fixed absolute threshold of 0.001 rad per offset step (`ROTATION_EPSILON`), and a doubled offset crosses it a few frames later. Getting exact glide would mean reimplementing the library's inertia step outside it, which I judged too fragile. The test allows this residual.
- Changing `camera.angularSensibility` while the control is attached will be overwritten by the cleanup if it still holds the halved value. The doc comment says so.
