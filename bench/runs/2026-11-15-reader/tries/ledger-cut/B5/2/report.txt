Key choices:
- `visible` is `!offscreen && z <= 1`. I did not use the library's `clipped` flag, because it also flags depth beyond the far plane. The library uses reverse-Z, so `z > 1` means nearer than the near plane, which I treat as not visible.
- If `camera._useFloatingOrigin` is set, the point is rebased on the camera position, matching how `getViewMatrix` zeroes the translation in that mode.
- The aspect ratio and viewport are taken from the canvas itself, as you said the camera renders to the whole canvas. `camera.viewport` is ignored.
- If the canvas has zero backing size, the function returns NaN coordinates with `visible: false`.
