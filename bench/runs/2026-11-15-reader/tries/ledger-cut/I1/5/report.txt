On every frame after the first, it compares the camera's alpha, beta, radius and target with the values it wrote last frame. Any difference throws, and the promise rejects. On the first frame it zeroes the inertia and skips that check.
- attachControl's applyInertia (src/camera/arc-rotate-controls.ts) is pushed onto scene._beforeRender at attach time, so it runs before any driver pushed later. Scene._update walks _beforeRender in insertion order.
- (a) Coasting is applied by applyInertia, then the driver's first frame zeroes the offsets and does not check, so it runs to completion.
- (b) A pointermove only adds to the inertial offsets. The next frame's applyInertia moves the camera, the driver sees the change and throws.
- (c) The second call's driver is later in the list. On the next frame the first driver runs, sees no change, and moves the camera. The second driver's first frame then moves it again without a check. On the following frame the first driver sees the camera moved by someone else and throws.
