I wrote app/src/limited-orbit.ts exporting attachLimitedOrbit(camera, canvas, scene, minDistance, maxDistance). It calls the library's attachControl for orbit, wheel and pinch, then the library's setCameraLimits with lowerRadiusLimit/upperRadiusLimit set to the range. That clamps camera.radius inside the radius setter, so wheel inertia, pinch writes and overshoot all stay in range, and a camera that starts outside the range is moved inside before the function returns. The returned cleanup detaches the controls and the clamp hook and restores the camera's previous radius limits; it is idempotent. Invalid ranges throw a RangeError.

I also added app/src/limited-orbit.test.ts (wheel, pinch, initial clamp, cleanup, validation). All 5 tests pass with vitest against the checkout. tsc reports errors only in the library's src/xr files (missing WebXR types), none in the app.
