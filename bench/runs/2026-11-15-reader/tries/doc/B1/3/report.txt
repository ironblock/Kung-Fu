Things to be aware of:
- Cleanup removes the hook but leaves `camera.lowerRadiusLimit` and `camera.upperRadiusLimit` set to your values, because the library's disposer only removes the hook. Tell me if you want those cleared too.
- The clamp relies on the library's `setCameraLimits`, which is public, but it works through the camera's internal `_clampToLimits` hook. If the library's behaviour changes, the tests should catch it.
- I did not pass `keyboard` or `pointerMappings` to `attachControl`, so you get the default mouse and touch behaviour only.
