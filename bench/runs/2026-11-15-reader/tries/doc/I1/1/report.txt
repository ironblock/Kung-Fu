Why, from the library source (babylon-lite/packages/babylon-lite/src/camera/arc-rotate-interpolate.ts, frame-interpolation.ts, arc-rotate-controls.ts):
- (a) The attachControl inertia hook runs before the interpolation driver each frame. On its first step the driver zeroes all inertial offsets and never checks the pose on that frame, so the coasting stops and the transition runs to completion.
- (b) A drag sets inertialAlphaOffset/BetaOffset (or panning offsets). The next frame's inertia moves the pose before the driver's step, the driver sees the pose differs from what it last wrote, and it throws "ArcRotate camera interpolation was interrupted."
- (c) The first call's driver runs first each frame and records its pose. The second call's first step then writes a new pose, so on the next frame the first driver sees interference and rejects with the same "interrupted" error. The second call continues and resolves.
