I added /tmp/claude-0/-home-user/5a6488dd-41c7-5be7-b55c-ee990999c7a9/scratchpad/reader-work/doc/B4/1/app/src/globe-tilt.ts, which exports `tiltGlobeCamera(camera: GeospatialCamera, pitch: number): void`. It sets `camera.limits.pitchMin = pitch` and then calls `setGeospatialOrientation(camera, { pitch })`. The reason is that the library's `getEffectivePitchMax` drops the maximum pitch to `pitchMin` (~0.001) when the camera is zoomed out, and a new camera rests at `radiusMax`, so a plain pitch assignment would be clamped to almost straight down. With `pitchMin` raised to the tilt, the allowed pitch range stays at or above the tilt at every radius, so later zoom changes keep the tilt, while the zoom-out auto-pitch still applies to pitches above it.

I checked it with a temporary vitest file (since moved out of the app tree to /tmp/claude-0/-home-user/5a6488dd-41c7-5be7-b55c-ee990999c7a9/scratchpad/globe-tilt-scratch.test.ts). It covered tilts 0.1, 0.5 and 1.2 on a fresh camera and a non-trivial heading and centre; all 4 tests passed. `tsc` reports no errors in the app's `src/`; its 30 errors are all inside the library checkout.

Things I am not sure of:
- Side effect: `limits.pitchMin` is now the tilt, so the camera's own controls cannot pitch below it until `tiltGlobeCamera` is called again with a smaller value. If you want the controls to go to straight down, the other option is clearing `limits.pitchDisabledRadiusScale` instead, which removes the zoom-out pitch behaviour entirely. I chose the first option because it changes less.
- I did not test the attached geospatial controls or fly-to, which may also write pitch.
- The function throws a RangeError for non-finite pitch values. It does not check that pitch is at most the library's `pitchMax` (about 1.56), but your range of 0.1 to 1.2 is below it.
