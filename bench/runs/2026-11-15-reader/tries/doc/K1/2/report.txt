Things to be aware of:

- I took the alpha/beta starting values from the library doc (camera on +Z looking at the origin). I did not check them visually since nothing is rendered.
- Arrow keys use the library's legacy defaults (`keyboard: true`). The doc describes Ctrl and Alt modifiers for panning and zooming. I did not add any custom key mappings.
