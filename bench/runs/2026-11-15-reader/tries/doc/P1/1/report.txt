Answer for the preview page (camera made with `createArcRotateCamera`, `attachControl(camera, canvas)` with no scene):
- Drag: false. Mouse drag only adds to `inertialAlphaOffset` / `inertialBetaOffset`. Those are applied per frame in `applyInertia`, which is only registered on `scene._beforeRender` when a scene is passed, so alpha and beta do not change.
- Wheel: false. Wheel only adds to `inertialRadiusOffset`, which is likewise never applied without a scene.
- Pinch: true. The two-finger path in `onTouchMove` writes `camera.radius` directly, with no scene needed.

What I read: `docs/lite/architecture/02-camera.md` and `packages/babylon-lite/src/camera/arc-rotate-controls.ts` (plus the radius setter in `arc-rotate.ts`) in the checkout.
